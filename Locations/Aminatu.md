Hammerhead ship that was owned by a pirate named Spider. Nadiif "took it off his hands" and used it as his own ship for some time.

After ambushing us just outside the Kinshala Sphere, we defeated them and took the ship to Thunder Junction to turn it in (along with Nadiif and Brightspark).

Shortly after we turn this ship in, we see it being taken off by Trist and Boyd - the ??? Mafia took ownership of it (which pissed Mara off a LOT).

We follow the ship since it seems to be involved in some kind of "job" and wind up getting involved with the ??? Mafia. Orevana makes a deal with Sabaal to get ownership of the ship in exchange for doing him a favor (scrying on Felix).
