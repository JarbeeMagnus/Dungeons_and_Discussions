This is where the Ravnican Sphere is floating. It is a massive shapeless mass of gasses and chunks of minerals. There are magically dead pockets. The phosphrus gas pockets are extremely flammable. Electric beams. And creatures that inhabit the zone - Scavers, Goon Balloons, Space Clowns, Vampirates

There is a Flow Beacon inside. Zellhane tells us they are ancient artefacts that emit light. And if you get too close (within about 20 miles) you disappear and are never seen again.
