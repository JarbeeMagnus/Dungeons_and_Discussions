The 6th District is a large swamp located on Ravnica. Something happened 200 years ago that caused acid pools and undead in the area. The story most people accept is that an old Izzet barrier broke down and destroyed the area. 

We encountered undead that had Simic cytoblasts.

There are “Sky swallowers” here - likely leviathans which would make flying with a zeppelid difficult

The storms in the East (by the Reclamation Zone) started about 15 years ago (9971)

### More Details we got from Pannikin:
**Sixth District of Ravnica** 
Approx. 14 million square kilometers 
Population - approx. 92 billion 80% Guildless population 

5,900 kilometers south of the **Tenth District** 
Weather - 50s/60s with omnipresent fog and rain (very Bladerunner) 

Industrial / Biochemical / Manufacturing / Mining 

**Lúka** 
Main hub of commerce and population in the west of the **Sixth**. 
HQs for **Izzet**, **Orzhov**, and **Simic** guilds. 

The **Izzet League** operates hundreds of factories, utility stations, and mizzium foundries surrounding **Lúka**, spreading to the east and south. 
**Izzet** manufacturing and processing factory clusters make up the bulk of the district on the central and west side (like North Dakota). 
**Simic** (in partnership with the **Izzet**) maintain canals, runoff cisterns, and pumping stations to mitigate issues from the near-constant rainfall in the district. 
**Simic** have expansive laboratories and testing facilities where they create medicines, vaccines, and other biological experiments. Major R&D facilities. 
**Orzhov** cartels run the banking chapels and cathedrals, sucker guildless into protection rackets, and run mining operations to supply the **Izzet** with raw materials for **mizzium** production. 
Major **Orzhov** oligarchies operating in the Sixth: **Lashka**, **Pracovisku**, **Kartashov**, **Botasavo**, **Tezirek**. 
**Rakdos** cultists run brothels, nightclubs, etc. that cater to the guildless workers and guild administrators in the Sixth. 
**Golgari** rot farmers in the undercity and along the reservoir channels provide the bulk of the food for the district. 
The city is built up on the west side of the district, breaking up into smaller urban clusters divided by stretches of industrial mesas and valleys.

**Majak Pavilion** 
**Azorius** civic center in **Lúka**. 

**Dawnbreak Garrison**
**Boros Legion** HQ in the **Sixth**. Located in **Lúka**. 
**Boros** troops are only contracted for the **Izzet**, **Orzhov**, and **Simic** in the **Sixth** as military security forces. No **Wojeks** patrol here. 

**Lashka Cathedral** 
Seat of power for the largest **Orzhov** cartel in the **Sixth**, the **Lashka** family. 

**Zahrada**
**Selesnya** territory. 

**Lashka Station** 
Airship hub located on the east side of **Luka**. 

**Reclamation Zone - RZ.6.9733.A** 
Owned by **Pracovisku Development** (4.3 million square kilometers) 
The area was closed off after an **Izzet** industrial disaster in the year **9,733**. 
The area has devolved into **corrosive swamps** overrun with **mutated horrors**.
**Sky Swallowers** hunt in the ever-present storm clouds above the area. 
Per the **Guildpact**, reclamation zones must remain closed off to settlement for a period of no less than **400 years**, after which they can be redeveloped for habitation. 

Many ancient city names remain in use throughout the **Sixth District**. The names of cities over 10,000 years old persist despite becoming swallowed up by the ecumenopolis of **Ravnica** after the establishment of the **Guildpact**.

**Haazda**
Guildless law enforcement, similar to Texas Rangers and wild west sheriffs. Not officially affiliated with the **Boros Legion**, but the **Haazda** share information on cases and attempt to work with the **Boros** where possible (the gesture is not officially reciprocated by the **Boros** in the **Sixth**).
