This is where Project Firmament is taking place. It's in the eye of a MASSIVE storm that is created by 3 trapped djinns inside the area.

Map is below.

#### Rules for Workers inside Koren:
- There is a curfew
- Rooms house 4 people per room
- The top of the mine is off limits  
- There is a recreation zone  
- When leaving, workers go to the Way Station to the North of the site to “go home”  
- Pracovisku Citadel is on the other side of the Guildless area (we are currently in the construction worker zone)  
- Every 10 days, the workers get to go to Nadvlada Cathedral to participate in a blessing ceremony, overseen by the “Ministrant of Obligation”  
- **Work schedule is:**
	- 4 days of work, 10-hours each day
	- 2 days’ break
	- 4 additional days of 10-hour work
- There is also another Cathedral (called Vernost) along the way to the leisure zone for workers to use  

#### Rite of Obligation at Nadvlada Cathedral
- What we notice before the ceremony:
	- There is not just one mass going on, but rather a stream of services. The workers enter through the front and the Guildless enter through the back
	- The cathedral itself has its back facing the prison "housing" area
	- There is a large concentration of spirits inside Nadvlada Cathedral that emit a strong feeling of grief
	- The inside of the cathedral is decorated with gold filigree. The Orzhov workers are upstairs and the Guildless are downstairs at the front of the cathedral. There is also a choir
- The Rite of Obligation ("blessing ceremony")
	- It starts with the choir singing a song for a few minutes
	- The priest then does his schtick. He mentions “The Black Dawn” and “Agyrym's ascension across Ravnica” - which does not ring any bells for Orevana
	- The Workers appear to be bored; they’ve done this time and time again. The Guildless in general are sad and weary during the ceremony
	- The ceremony ends with more choral music. The Guildless exit through the back
	- We don’t hear or see any kind of magical spell put over the Guildless
	- Orevana sees many many Orzhov spirits in the rafters
