## What We've Done So Far:
- Gajic asked us to gather intel on a rival family within the Orzhov in exchange for helping us with some legal trouble
- The team gets jobs as construction workers on a Pracovisku site
- Orevana dates Donwec Pracovisku to get more info
- The party finds out that workers are being asked about "Fight Club Night" by Orzhov outside normal work hours and that there is a "lucrative job" in the 6th district
-  We learn from Volenco that she's working on "**Project Phoenix**" that we believe is related
- In a sneaky mission, we get more info out of Volenco and Ljudo Jr. that we'll also give to Madam Gajic ([[Vizkopa_Meeting.jpg]]). The Pracovisku's are responsible for:
	- Harvesting souls and husks
	- Instigating the fighting between Rakdos and Gruul in the 6th District
	- Barges arrive at a "Waystation" in Koren
	- They have 206 soul cages secured in the vaults of Vizkopa Bank
	- The Ghost Council is unaware of what they are doing
- Ljudo Sr. is often talking to himself inside the family chapel. Ljudo Jr. seems to have been running things for the past 3 years
- We learn about the Reclamation Zone (Koren) in the 6th district has construction by Pracovisku Development. It's surrounded by a constant storm. We believe this is Project Firmament
	- We learned from a Rakdos commander that he and his people were hired "by Orzhov" (the Pracovisku family) to bring in caravans of people into the storm
	- The Pracovisku's are working with the Golgari Swarm - Okdaz is aware of what's going on
-  **Zuun 13, 9986** - We learn that Vianka Pracovisku was imprisoned in a stained glass mirror by her husband in 9966 (it’s currently 9986). Yunaska and Carodejnik were planning on killing Vianka and pinning it on us
- Also **Zuun 13, 9986** - We find out:
	- Ljudo Sr made a deal with the Neogi **20 years ago** - this is Project Firmament
		- Orzhov provides living people (Guildless) to the Neogi - 5,000 per shipment (approximately every 3 weeks)
		- While picking up the people, the Neogi drop off husks (for Golgari) and souls (for Orzhov), presumably from the previous shipment of people
		- The Neogi then deliver the people to the Nulviir
		- The number of souls/husks returned from the Nulviir are LESS than the number of living people sent out, so the they are taking a cut (most of it)
	- The Neogi are able to get in and out of Ravnica easily because of "some sort of controller"
	- Ljudo Jr, Ljudo Sr, and Countess Malenka (The High Mage) are the only ones that interact with the Neogi
	- The souls for Project Firmament are taken from the Waystation to Vizkopa bank by the Golgari
	- Once they complete Project Requiem, the Orzhov "will be granted dominance over Ravnica by the Nulviir" - Project Requiem is the big hole they are digging
	- The [[Intermediary_Device.jpg|intermediary device]] implanted into Ljudo Jr (and later Donwec) took place at "the Monolith" - unclear how this fits into everything else
- **Zuun 16, 9986** - Orevana and Belia go to Gajic and disclose the following information:
	- Orevana tells her all about the Pracovisku plotting, dead sons/cousin, and the matriarch is back. She hands copies of the manifesto
	- Orevana also tells Gajic that we’re sharing this info with Boros and Azorius and that she may want to distance her family from the Pracovisku's (since there is evidence that the Pracovisku family is the only one responsible for infringing on the Guild Pact)
	- As a reward, Gajic gives us some bottles of wine, including some Casarta (expensive). QUEST COMPLETE
