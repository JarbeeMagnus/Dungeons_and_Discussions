## What We've Done So Far:
- As we prepare to enter Koran/Reclamation Zone, Grizzut tells us that there are six Izzet guild mages that are helping Pracovisku that we need to capture for Nivmizit
- After Rakdos cultists destroyed the tower, we needed to get the missing parts to allow for the capture of the Guild Mages. Specifically:
	- 4 “positronic field entanglers” - We find 5 positronic field entanglers on a necklace of the Rakdos leader of the caravan
	- 6 “cascade emitters” - We find 4 cascade emitters at a Rakdos cultist camp nearby inside the drum of a troll and 2 cascade emitters on the body of the Rakdos commander on a bug mount
	- 1 “mana transplonder” - We find the mana transplonder on the body of the Rakdos commander on a bug mount
- We find out the equipment we gathered were for Grizzut to create 3 Counterveil Anchors. When pressed, we find out:
	- The 6 mages we are looking for should be with 3 djinn, which are causing the storm
	- When the counterveil anchors touch a djinn, the djinn will be neutralized  
	- Grizzut does not know how long the djinn will be neutralized  
- **Zuun 11, 9986** - Grizzut is caught sneaking around an MC dome. This is an issue because HE HAS the Counterveil Anchors
- **Zuun 12, 9986** - While investigating the 9th floor of the Operations & Logistics Tower, Michael and Peri find the floor is a teleportation room
	- Circles #1 and #3 appear to go to MC towers, as they saw Izzet Guild Mages enter/exit them
	- They exchange crystals to be used in “Dome 1”. They also mentioned something about charging them. Peri gets the sense that the crystals are running low
	- From this, we know the Izzet can communicate with each other in "real time" and then use the circles almost instantaneously to exchange items
	- We later find out that Circle #4 goes to the Waystation (on Zuun 13)
- **Zuun 14, 9986**
	- Rescued Grizzut from his holding cell in the Operations & Logistics Tower
	- Learned that Izzet plans to keep the djinn as forced labor in “the water stations”
	- We decided we didn't want to force the djinn as indentured servants - so we decide to ABANDON that part of the quest
- **Zuun 15, 9986** - We return the 2 guild mages we did grab (both dead) and return them to the Izzet League. As a reward, we get a set of Myzzium armor (half-plate & paldrons), a pyro converger (flamethrower), and a bag of jewels (worth 6k gold). QUEST COMPLETE
