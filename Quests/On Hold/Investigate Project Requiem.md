## What We've Done So Far:
- On **Zuun 11, 9986**, we enter the Requiem Gate in Koren AKA Reclamation Zone (where Project Firmament is taking place) and find:
	- We get through the gate and find a giant hole in the ground (estimate 2km deep). Mara stops in her tracks, as she has seen something similar on another, distant planet
	- About halfway down, we see more focused digging areas at the bottom
	- Orevana casts see invisibility and Michael opens his third eye. Orevana senses a large amount of spiritual energy and Michael sees many spirits around, some engaging in the work they had done while alive
	- At the bottom, we see they are dumping dirt and rocks into a channel below the floor of the pit
	- Mara suspects this will be some kind of “soul dump hole” like she saw before, but this one isn’t activated yet
- **Zuun 12, 9986** - While investigating in the library of the Operations & Logistics Tower, Mara discovers this project has been ongoing for about **20 years**. Target finish date is 9993 (in 10 years)
- **Zuun 12, 9986** - While decoding a book Mara stole inside the Operations & Logistics Tower, Hollia discovers:
	-  There are 32,000 workers in and out each day, with 12-hour shifts
	- She confirms the completion date is 9993 (*this is what Mara found, too*)
	- Target depth is 5 km (3.1 miles). Currently they are 2.2 miles down
	- "Avg worker viability duration" is a statistic that keeps popping up. After this period (14 weeks/100 days), they are sent to the Waystation
- **Zuun 13, 9986** - We find out:
	- Ljudo Sr made a deal with the Neogi **20 years ago** - this is Project Firmament where Orzhov exchanges living people for souls and husks
	- Once they complete Project Requiem, the Orzhov "will be granted dominance over Ravnica by the Nulviir" - Project Requiem is the big hole they are digging
	- The [[Intermediary_Device.jpg|intermediary device]] implanted into Ljudo Jr (and later Donwec) took place at "the Monolith" - unclear how this fits into everything else
- **Zuun 16, 9986** - We leave Ravnica. Since the target finish date for Project Requiem is 9993, we have about 7 years to figure out what to do next. QUEST ON HOLD
- **Zuun 28, 9986** - On the last day of travel out of the sphere, Orevana faints and has a vision. She  theorizes that the Ravnican stars may be soul cages based on the vision and Michael's interaction with Boccob while he was dead (he was surrounded by white light)
