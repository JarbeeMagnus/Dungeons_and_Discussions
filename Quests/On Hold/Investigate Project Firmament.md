## What We've Done So Far:
- **Xivaskir 19, 9986** - Learned about **Project Firmament (1500)** and **Project Phoenix** from papers at the construction site. Specifically:
	- General construction plans for Project Phoenix - the series of re-builds after the Rakdos riots. It also included information about a new (very tall) basilica called Strazit Sanctuary 
	- Information on a project in the 6th District called Project Firmament (1500)
- During downtime between **Xivaskir 21-27, 9986** - We learn that there is a "lucrative job" in the 6th district that we think is related to Project Firmament/Project Requiem
- **Xivaskir 27, 9986** - Just before the fashion show, Hollia and Kullic overhear Ljudo Jr. complain about going to “the way station” in the 6th district to “fix this” tomorrow morning. We also learn someone named "Volenco" is involved
- **Xivaskir 28, 9986** - We learn from Volenco that she's working on "Project Phoenix" that we believe is related; we overhead a conversation between her and Ljudo Jr. ([[Vizkopa_Meeting.jpg]]). The Pracovisku's are responsible for:
	- Instigating the fighting between Rakdos and Gruul in the 6th District
	- Barges arrive at the "Waystation" - and the Neogi ("spider fucks") are BEHIND in sending barges ("just 2 ships in the last month")
	- Harvesting souls and husks - 1 soul cage and multiple husks per barge
	- There are 6 prisoner buildings already constructed, with 8 more requested be built within the next 3 months. The Orzhov construction workers build the buildings
	- They currently have 206 soul cages secured in the vaults of Vizkopa Bank
	- The Ghost Council is unaware of what they are doing
- We learn from Donwec that Ljudo Sr has been locked up in the manor house for the past three years and never leaves. He is obsessed with being in the personal family chapel and is often there talking to himself. However, sometimes Donwec heard a thin, high-pitched voice answer his father during his ramblings. Mara thinks perhaps that is the voice of The Emissary
- The Reclamation Zone, where Project Firmament is taking place, is always surrounded by storm clouds
	- We learn from the Gruul Clans nearby that the storm in the center only came into existence 15 years ago
	- We learned from a Rakdos commander that he and his people were hired "by Orzhov" to bring in caravans of prisoners into the storm
	- There are three domes in the eye of the storm that shoot lightning into the storm - we presume the djinn are housed in those domes and are creating the protective storm (see [[Capture Izzet League Guild Mages]])
	- Best guess is that the storm is probably about a mile wide and 10-15 miles tall (in a circle around the eye)
- **Zuun 6,9986 -** Recon into the Eye of the Storm
	- We discover the heavily guarded gates heading into the area inside the storm
	- There is a mining facility just inside the entrance
	- The tunnel to get inside is HEAVILY guarded
- Upon first entering Koren (Zuun 7), we find out:
	- The workers' paperwork mentions "Project Firmament" and that they are on a 3 month contract (no less)
	- The whole area is surrounded by tall plateaus that the workers call the "dead zone"
	- There is a Rite of Obligation ceremony held at Nadvlada Cathedral every 10 days. There doesn't appear to be any magic done during the ceremony, but there is an almost constant stream of Guildless lead in, then a sermon that mentions “The Black Dawn” and “Agyrym's ascension across Ravnica”, and then the Guildless are lead back out. The ceremony itself is overseen by groups of Orzhov workers (they appear to have seen this numerous times). Guildless workers look TIRED and DIRTY 
- There is another project within this one called [[Investigate Project Requiem]] that involves digging a large hole
- On **Zuun 12**, while in the Logistics Tower in Koren, we learn there are stone slabs with water on them and runes on the floor inside the building (on the 9th floor) - it seems to be teleportation magic (according to Hollia). The 10th floor also has "consoles" (the slabs)
- **Zuun 12, 9986** - While investigating in the library of the Operations & Logistics Tower, Mara discovers:
	- Orzhov are working with the Golgari Swarm, specifically in the Waystation
	- We already know that there are husks and souls being differentiated from the Volenco/Ljudo Jr conversation from the Bank Recon mission (Xivaskir 28, above)
	- We’re presuming the Golgari get the husks and the Orzhov get the souls
	- The decommissioned pass on the map is a tunnel between the Garrison and the Waystation that they collapsed about 15 years ago. We saw a decommissioned machine with a driller arm on the way in that is probably related to this incident
- **Zuun 12, 9986** - While investigating the 9th floor (teleportation room) of the Operations & Logistics Tower, Michael and Peri discover:
	- When people enter this room, there is some kind of tone when the door opens (Michael has this committed to memory & demonstrated it to the rest of Gatewatch)
	- Room Layout:
		- There is some kind of “console” in the room with bowls of water and crystals throughout
		- The rest of the room is bare save the console and a teleportation circle nearby
		- The room seems to extend and curve around the whole tower, with 4 circles & consoles equally apart (like a t or an x)
		- There are about 8 guards sprinkled about the room. There are a handful of other people operating the consoles
	- Teleportation Circles Used:
		- Yunaska used circle #4 and disappeared. There were operators at the console at the time
		- After some time, a centaur dressed as an Izzet Guild Mage emerges from teleportation circle #1
		- A human male Guild Mage emerges from #3 shortly after. They exchange crystals to be used in “Dome 1”. They also mentioned something about charging them. Peri gets the sense that the crystals are running low (likely related to the [[Capture Izzet League Guild Mages]] quest)
		- Soon after, Yunaska emerges from circle #4 with another person - an ornately dressed mage
		- We did not see anyone enter or exit circle #2
		- We confirmed later that circle #4 goes to the Waystation
- **Zuun 13, 9986** - We find out:
	- Ljudo Sr made a deal with the Neogi **20 years ago** - this is Project Firmament
		- Orzhov provides living people (Guildless) to the Neogi - 5,000 per shipment (approximately every 3 weeks)
		- While picking up the people, the Neogi drop off husks (for Golgari) and souls (for Orzhov), presumably from the previous shipment of people
		- The Neogi then deliver the people to the Nulviir
		- The number of souls/husks returned from the Nulviir are LESS than the number of living people sent out, so the they are taking a cut (most of it)
	- The Neogi are able to get in and out of Ravnica easily because of "some sort of controller"
	- Ljudo Jr, Ljudo Sr, and Countess Malenka (The High Mage) are the only ones that interact with the Neogi
	- The souls for Project Firmament are taken from the Waystation to Vizkopa bank by the Golgari
	- Once they complete Project Requiem, the Orzhov "will be granted dominance over Ravnica by the Nulviir" - Project Requiem is the big hole they are digging
	- The [[Intermediary_Device.jpg|intermediary device]] implanted into Ljudo Jr (and later Donwec) took place at "the Monolith" - unclear how this fits into everything else
	- Mara summons a homunculus to test the dead zone - it immediately dies
- **Zuun 14, 9986**
	- While releasing one of the djinns, a floating black tower appeared and started shooting red lasers at the djinn (The Monolith)
	- We learn from being high in the air that there is some kind of black fungus growing at the top of the plateau around the site
	- A teleportation circles with a large cut/gash through the middle is unusable - perhaps this can be replicated
- **Zuun 17-28, 9986**
	- We leave Ravnica on the 16th
	- During the journey to the edge of the Ravnican Sphere, Orevana scries on Donwec and sees that Project Firmament is still ongoing. He has an intermediary device installed. After the first successful scry, she is no longer able to scry on Donwec
	- On the last day of travel out of the sphere, Orevana faints and has a vision. She  theorizes that the Ravnican stars may be soul cages based on the vision and Michael's interaction with Boccob while he was dead (he was surrounded by white light)
	- Peri and Orevana agree that we want to save the spirits in the soul cages ASAP
- We leave Ravnica on Zuun 16. Since the target finish date for Project Requiem (and thus, probably Project Firmament) is 9993, we have about 7 years to figure out what to do next. QUEST ON HOLD
