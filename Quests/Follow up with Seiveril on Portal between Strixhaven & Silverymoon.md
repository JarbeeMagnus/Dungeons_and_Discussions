## What We've Done So Far:
Hollia's mentor Seiveril discloses that he went to Strixhaven instead of Ravnica when he went out searching for her. Information he disclosed:

- Instead of Ravnica, he ended up going to some kind of arcane college ("Lorehold College" in the Strixhaven universe) on a planet called Arcavios
- The world had general teleportation issues that blocked their Word of Recall. They worked with the college to get it sorted out for him to return to Silverymoon
- After hearing about Ravnica using souls, he explains that Strixhaven uses spirits to manage records
- He plans to get teleportation set-up between Silverymoon and Strixhaven
- Seiveril also mentions that someone back in Strixhaven mentioned Rakdos, but he didn’t have many details

**We asked to stay posted on the progress of the Strixhaven portal**
