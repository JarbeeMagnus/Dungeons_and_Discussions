## Detailed Notes
- The 4-eyed man says his name is Tallison Vos (or "Funcle Tally")
	- He says he’s been with Peri since we found him on that ship - Michael Landon remembers that this guy is a Kitsune
	- Tallison pulls a long needle out of his pants and says it will repay the debt of Peri saving his life
	- Michael uses Identify and says it’s just a normal needle. Peri tries to give it back but that upsets Tallison
	- We chat with Tally and Hollia goes outside
	- Tally says something about “Peri’s friend, Nameless” sending him to watch Peri
	- After a while, Mara Underhill goes to check on Hollia outside where Hollia talks about a dream she was trapped in - something about ravens
	- Hollia comes back inside and checks Orevana's heartbeat (which requires Orevana to take her top off)
	- Tally says he “Jumped off your ship onto the derelict ship” which seems backwards?
	- Michael goes outside to be with Mara - they pray to gods to get her magic back so we can BAMF to the ship
- Peri asks for Tally’s story
- Tally previously (a long time ago) was in the Court of Shapes, then moved to the Drifting Court (“but King Nothing wasn’t very exciting”), then the Candescent Court
- He says every 150 years, the courts meet-up to discuss goings-ons
- Hollia says she didn’t sleep well (no long rest) and had a very personal dream that she didn’t want to share
- Hollia also says that Orkus' goal is to kill a death god - then he’ll ascend to godhood. So we send Tally out to check on it
- We think if Hollia rests, she’ll be able to commune with her god again and get her spells back. Orevana plays her a lullaby to fall asleep while the rest of the team goes outside to hang out
	- Peri thinks Tally was trying to trick him into owing him by gifting the needle
	- Tally returns 20-25 minutes later and says that the hut was a mess and nobody was there - including no bodies
	- Tally also tells the team that he can get us off planet - via a “Fey Crossing”
	- To pass the time, Michael and Tally play Rock, Paper, Scissors for a bit. Then, Mara, Peri, Michael, and Tally play Never Have I Ever with coffee
	- After about 10 minutes, Mara goes inside to get Hollia and Orevana
	- Orevana picks up a sleeping Hollia to carry her to wherever this Fey Crossing is. Tally turns into a fox to lead the way
- We arrive at a hole in the gear and go down inside. After a while, we find a dead myconid whose body crept up the walls to form a circle. We step in one at a time
	- On the other side, we end up in a place that still looks like Mechanus. As we walk, the tunnel becomes more organic
	- The tunnel opens up to a steep cliff a few hundred feet above a vast ocean. Tally says we have to jump. After almost no hesitation, Peri jumps
	- Mara and Orevana gently wake Hollia. After a bit, Orevana runs and jumps off the cliff. Hollia and Mara jump shortly after (holding hands)
	- We suddenly appear, soaking wet, on The Ru
- Captain Urn Nadiif & Mr. Brightspark are tied to the cargo mast with Marge, Jilly, and Belia surrounding them with weapons bared
	- Mara immediately punches Nadiif in the nuts
	- Orevana casts Zone of Truth as Nadiif relays what happened. The slime monster that attacked Zellhane apparently dissolved their helmsman - so the Hammerhead (The Aminatu) is drifting about 200ft nearby. Nadiif pleads that we take care of "Crabitha" (who’s back on his ship)
	- Mara stabs Nadiif a few times as we interrogate him. We decide to loot The Aminatu with the intention of donating it to the Elven Imperial Navy for the war effort
	- On The Aminatu, we find Crabitha! And a journal depicting their vampirate escapades - apparently the Hammerhead was stolen from another pirate named "Spider" (Mara’s met him!)
- We get the locations of various pirate hideouts
	- The helm of the Hammerhead is more extravagant than ours
	- We also find various crates/goods (including wine & dry goods) and other spoils (20k gold). And a bunch of coffins which we throw into space
	- Michael looks for hidden treasure as well, adding another 1k gold
	- With Peri’s help, Orevana gets Crabitha to trust her - a little
- 6 hours later…
	- Mara and Hollia have a quick chat - Hollia seems much more into fitness now? And she has Mara destroy her old librarian uniform
	- Hollia appears to have her powers back!
- We have another long rest & get into Kinshala (on Redmesa 23, 1069)
	- The stars of Kinshala sphere are humanoid statues holding fire braziers
	- Yadda yadda travel for 12 days to go to one of the planets to get air & leave
- We’ve got some roleplay coming up! We will yadda yadda all the way to Thunder Junction where we will attempt to hire crew, turn in the vampirates & Hammerhead ship, get repairs, etc
