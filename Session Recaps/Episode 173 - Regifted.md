## Detailed Notes
**DATE: Highnoon 14**
- Brightspark is alive!
- When asked what he remembers, he says “silver sky with no stars or air or time” & pools of colored water in the sky
	- Mara asks about what gods he worships and he says he can’t remember their names. He’s been lost from his home planet for so long
	- When asked how old he is, he doesn’t remember. Nadiif confirms he’s at least 22 because that’s how long they’ve known each other
	- Neither Nadiif nor Brightspark (Nadiif added the “Mr.” to be more formal) remember where Brightspark was picked up
	- The Giff have been lost to their homeworld for ages (Nadiif whispers that he heard they blew up their own planet)
	- Brightspark seems to ignore Nadiif
	- We learn that guns are sacred to the Giff, so Michael offers his rifle temporarily. Brightspark holds the rifle like a baby and seems to calm down a little bit
- Mara had been holding Hollia’s head this whole time (Hollia is passed out after resurrecting Brightspark)
	- Mara, wanting to talk to Jilly and Zellhane about if there are any Giff-sized clothes on board
	- Orevana brings Hollia into her tent downstairs in the cargo hold
	- When approached by Mara, Zellhane is confused and frustrated about Nadiif and Brightspark being on board and that we’re helping them since the goo monster almost killed her
	- Jilly mentions that, when she talked to Brightspark back when they were pretending to check our papers outside Kinshala, he didn’t seem nefarious
	- Mara also discusses potentially stripping the Aminatu for parts (they think the helm alone would be worth100k gold)
	- Zellhane mentions the trio of new crew may be stopping by (three hadozee)
- Back in the cargo hold, Orevana asks Nadiif about the nature of his relationship with Brightspark
	- She learns they’ve known each other for 22 years - Nadiif took Brightspark captive from his ship and forcibly turned him into a thrall. He had no plans to actually turn him into a vampirate ("some people want to be ordered around, they just don't know it")
	- Nadiif was turned into a vampirate over a century ago (meaning he wasn’t born that way)
	- When asked about the process to turn into a vampirate, Nadiif confirms that the whole process starts with a bite
	- Brightspark was Nadiif’s only thrall
- Mara and Peri discuss purchasing Brightspark some clothes. Peri offers to go out and buy them, so she sends him into town
- Hollia wakes up and seems to have forgotten everything after entering the portal to Ravnica - she asks about Befallor and Delandri
	- The team is concerned about this development
	- Orevana suggests that Hollia check her journal to jog her memory
	- Hollia says “perhaps someone could kiss me to jog my memory” and Mara passionately kisses her in response
	- Nadiif notices that Hollia was lying about having lost her memory - Orevana tells him to let Mara think she fixed it
- Hollia, now “with her memory back” mentions wanting to take revenge on Sabaal
	- At the mention of Sabaal, Brightspark remembers Sabaal swung his trident at him and beheaded him
	- Hollia asks if he wanted to be resurrected and Brightspark confirms that he wants to die at some point - so it’s confirmed that he didn’t actually want to be a thrall
	- Hollia wants to dig up history on the old gods (which Giff worshipped). Hollia realizes the place Brightspark described was the astral plane, which is where gods go when they die (note: there are alive gods there too)
- Peri arrives with his purchases - clothes, various holy symbols, a cactus, a 2nd set of clothes, a whistle, a lantern & an explorer’s packs, and holy water (Orevana covers Nadiif’s ears for that last part)
- We all have breakfast and Togor’s people arrive to work on the ship. Shortly after, we hear three rambunctious voices below asking to come aboard
	- We meet the three new crew: Pomo, Munu, and Sogo
	- Peri brings out the four trees he was given yesterday for the hadozee to install for him. Two in the front room by the helm and two inside the cargo hold to align with the names on the cards - “BugHeadCabin” and “ThoraxSnoringRoom”
	- They’ll install the trees when it’s appropriate (everything is still under construction and being figured out)
- Brightspark stands up and thanks us for the hospitality and says he’s going to go on a walk
	- Peri says he wants to follow him and Orevana hands out the Sending Stones. One pair between Peri and Mara and the other between her and Hollia
	- Suddenly, a stranger comes up to the ship and says he’s looking for Periwinkle. He looks like some kind of homeless man. He says his name is Fendril Tuskhorn
	- Peri needs to deal with this guy, so Mara and Hollia will follow Brightspark instead of him
	- When Peri looks over the edge of the ship, he recognizes the man as the person he invited to our crew while pissing together outside a few days ago
	- Fendril sold his ship for 5 barrels of whiskey to join our crew and he is drunk off his ass
	- Peri is concerned about the man because he’s REALLY out of it. He tries to let him down gently and gives him 20 gold to go drink and NOT be hired. Fendril seems to accept the payment and heads off to the nearest bar
	- Peri turns invisible for a bit to escape and heads out to follow Mara and Hollia (very stealthily)
- Orevana takes Nadiif down to the kitchen to talk privately. She wants to learn some information about him and vampiraticism
	- His village was attacked by vampirates. Nadiif is his first name - Urn was a joke name he came up with a long time ago
	- His previous captain had kidnapped him and turned him into a thrall for about 6 months before fully turning him into a vampirate. Orevana is concerned about free will during the thrall stage, but he doesn't elaborate
	- The vampirate captain that turned Nadiif was named Captain Ethelinda and she was later killed. Nadiif mentioned some kind of mutiny
	- He mentions feeding on both blood and energy (it’s intoxicating). He didn't mention how often he needs to feed or a distinction between the two
	- He asks about what Orevana’s home world was like and asks if she was fulfilled during that time - she wasn’t able to give him a straight answer. She said her world is much bigger now so it's hard to say if she was fulfilled on Ravnica. They bonded over having left behind their old, small lives
	- The process for turning into a vampirate involves being bitten, dying, and then waking up - Nadiif didn't give many other details
	- Caught up in the moment, Orevana kisses Nadiif and they have sex in the kitchen. Notably, Nadiif attempted to bite Orevana, but she successfully avoids it
- Cut to Hollia and Mara
	- Hollia mentions that she wasn’t unconscious when Orevana was whispering with Nadiif in the cargo hold. Specifically about the process of becoming a vampirate
	- They’re both concerned about Orevana wanting to be turned - they may want to do an intervention since "that's kind of her thing"
	- Hollia says she hasn’t properly resurrected anyone before (the others were all recently dead). And she only needs **a piece of someone** to bring them back - Hollia could bring back Glindaaro Ru. This is the wizard that initially hired Mara's adventuring crew. Future quest!
	- They keep tabs on Brightspark as he moseys towards the casino
	- Peri pops up behind them and we find out he’s both a virgin and doesn’t know what sex is. Hollia says she has a book he could borrow
	- Hollia seems to be troubled with how she’s been wanting to hurt people - she wants to take revenge on Sabaal and also kill Nadiif
	- At the mention of Nadiif, Hollia suddenly realizes Orevana is alone on the ship with only Michael and Nadiif. She’s concerned about Orevana turning into a vampire; if Orevana becomes a vampire, Hollia can’t bring her back to life
	- Peri mentions how he had offered the man that came over earlier today a job on The Ru, but he let him go (much to Mara's surprise and slight frustration)
- They get close to the casino and notice Brightspark get near, hesitate at the door, tilt his head, and then turn to go inside a nearby feast hall (which is like a big celebratory pub)
	- They discuss how they see potential in Brightspark and that they don’t want him and Nadiif both on the ship together
	- If they had to choose, they’d take Brightspark over Nadiif. If he's up for it, they would consider hiring Brightspark
	- They decide to go inside and offer friendship to Brightspark. However, when they enter, they see him amongst a group of about 15 other Giff. They are all happy to see Brightspark (a fellow Giff) and they all have drinks and are generally jolly
	- Instead of interrupting, Mara, Hollia, and Peri grab a table nearby and each have a drink. They talk about Peri’s lack of a sex life/drive
- After confirming Brightspark seems to be in good company, they head back to The Ru
	- After cleaning up the kitchen, Orevana and Nadiif hang out on deck for a bit when a voice calls up saying they have a Sterling gram for her
	- She reads it and finds out it's from Sabaal - he's turning in the favor she promised him
	- The rest of the gang arrives shortly thereafter (it’s now early-afternoon)
	- We briefly discuss the Sterling-gram and how we’ll go to The Voyager Grande tomorrow morning
