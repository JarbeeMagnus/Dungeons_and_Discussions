## Detailed Notes
- We need to deal with the air problem
	- Peri creates some plants but they likely wouldn’t help with the soiling of the air (they would just create more oxygen)
	- Peri also discusses turning into a lizard balloon and blowing the bad air out of the envelope
- Hollia tells everyone she can teleport herself and 5 others to Silverymoon. They should be able to come back afterwards
	- Befallor & Delandri are from there & would stay if they went back; anyone else would want to return
	- They could then research ways of dealing with the bad air
- Orevana tells the party that we could do a seance for her to get Divination, then she can ask a god (Boccob) one question - namely if Hollia’s plan to go to Silverymoon would be successful. Note, no spirit actually shows up
	- We do the seance and Orevana uses Divination
	- Michael’s book starts talking, so he pulls it out of his satchel. Tony Robbins is animated and says hello to Hollia & Orevana, then answers the question
	- “When you go there, if you go there, look for my friend Lustria Silverhand” - which was not super helpful
- Michael starts a ritual with the book but after 8 minutes, he throws a tantrum which interrupts the ritual
- When Hollia asks about others coming, Peri says he’s where he belongs - in the stars
	- Suddenly, Orevana is reminded of her experience when they left the “stars” in Ravnica
	- She relays to the group what her experience was & theorizes that the Ravnican stars may be soul cages
	- Peri walks out and says that was the “saddest thing” Orevana has ever said
	- She also relays what she dreamt of the other night in greater detail
- They remember the raven statuette and Hollia asks if it’s black like Orevana’s dream. Michael uses the raven statuette they found in the wrecked ship and a black raven is summoned. It does not communicate or react to Michael talking to it. Hollia is excitedly taking notes. Michael, disappointed in the ‘stupid raven’ gives it to Orevana
- We check on Peri before executing the plan
	- Peri is distressed because he thinks his power comes from dead spirits and he might be evil
	- Orevana tries to comfort Peri by saying her power also comes from spirits
	- His rock had sang when we got close to the stars too, which added to his distress. Hollia says that there is an elven tree in Silvery Moon that could be taken and turned into some kind of staff/magic focus for him (though it would take some convincing)
	- Hollia detects magic on the rock and gets transmutation magic
	- Peri wants to save the spirits in the soul cages - as does Orevana. We will fix it, but we can’t just yet
	- Peri changes his mind and asks to come with to Silvery Moon
- Mara and Hollia talk about the plan - we should return within 36 hours. (Mara confirms “I will panic at hour 37"). Hollia brings Befallor, Delandri, Orevana, and Michael to Silverymoon
	- It worked!! We are teleported into a room full of books - and a man diligently making a copy of a book
	- Michael startles the man by asking if he’s a wizard. He welcomes us to the vault of the sages and does a whole spiel (his name is Wordsmith Eldrin)
	- We ask about Lustria Silverhand, but Eldrin tells us she hasn’t been here for a few years. Orevana asks about a statue of Lustria and finds out there are many throughout Silvery Moon
	- Befallor and Delandri go their separate ways
- We follow Hollia upstairs to her mentor’s office (Seiveril) and she finds his name has been replaced with someone else’s name (which she ignores). She gets info from another Wordsmith on where to find her mentor (who she finds out has resigned). Hollia is SASSY with everyone working here. Some dark skinned elf woman is REAL MEAN to Hollia. Hollia might also resign
- We head over to the Woman’s College
	- The city is gorgeous! We stop and smell flowers along the way
	- We get broadsheets from a sell boy - Orevana buys one (with a zino) and Hollia overpays with Ravnican gold. She also bestows Orevana the ability to comprehend languages because she couldn’t read anything
	- There’s a translucent bridge going over the river with a big map that we look over
	- Peri talks to a tree to ask about getting a branch for a staff - the tree says it (and the other trees) are healthy and don’t have any branches ready to fall. He talks to a horny raspberry bush, then a nearby tiger lily
	- Hollia has an internal epiphany about the staff - her father is a gardener and could maybe get him a stick
	- Meanwhile, Michael walked up to a group of students (after Hollia told him to). He changes his jacket to white question marks. They ask to touch his hair (he allows it). Michael asks them about air purification magic but they’re first years. Planar studies is a more advanced subject - they aren’t able to point him to anyone. He tries to arcane gate to another group of students and it fails. They say something about how “the mithil” stops higher magic. Hollia then comes to the group and bores them into leaving
	- When we enter a building, a bell rings and a robed man approaches us and says we need to leave since we’re not students. Hollia explains she was on the interplanar expedition and asks for Befallor and the man summons a candelabra to lead us to Befallor
- We follow the candelabra into a room labeled “Planar Expedition Program”
	- Befallor is talking to the program leader (Magus Odisar) - Hollia gets flustered and asks Orevana to do the talking
	- We go further into the lecture hall and Michael shakes Odisar’s hand and shows him the Boccob book. This gets his interest
	- Michael asks him about purifying air and persuades him to help us. His ideas include:
		- Portal to elemental plane of air
		- Summon an air elemental
		- Open a bag of wind (he recommended going to Shining Scroll)
		- Suck the air out via a planar vacuum
		- Bottled breath
		- Fireball or flame wall (these are both out)
	- Hollia slowly disengages from the conversation and moves to Befallor. She gives Befallor her tome of notes of everything she’s encountered since leaving along with her religious & arcanic insights
- Hollia wants the knowledge to go to the program and not the vault of sages (“they piss me off”)
	- Hollia, Peri, and Orevana leave shortly after
	- Michael chats with Odisar a bit longer before following everyone else
	- There may be a way to change fireball to pull air instead of fire?
- We go to Shining Scroll to try to buy a bag of air
	- We cross the moon bridge to the older part of town
	- We pass a raucous tavern singing a song (a common gathering spot for singles)
	- A woman calls from the back welcoming us, named Bessa (PRICES BELOW UPDATED BASED ON RICK’S LATER PRICING ON 8/11)
	- We buy one bottle of air and order 9 more for pickup later (150gp each, Orevana already paid 1375 for the 1 in stock + downpayment for 9; we owe 225gp total at pickup). Bessa throws in a few healing potions on the house
	- She can source one (or more) bags of wind (quoted at 1100gp each, need updated pricing) in about a week. Another scroll of “at your side” (125gp) or “galder’s speedy courier” (650gp) - we’ll definitely come back for fun scrolls
	- Bessa recommends “The Interesting Beard” for lunch
- MEANWHILE ON THE RU
	- The ship is much quieter with most of the party being gone. Mara and Ellegorin are chatting on the deck while she paces
	- The ship comes out of top speed in an asteroid field of giant translucent rocks
	- A light flickers nearby briefly. Mara watches and sees more flickering in different areas. It reminds her of the lights they encountered before - 11 lights dancing around. They suddenly form a circle and start spinning about 500ft ahead of the ship
	- Belia stops the ship. The lights speed up and get smaller. A humanoid silhouette appears in the center and floats toward the ship. It is a slender humanoid with long hair and appears to be made of light with glowing eyes
	- Mara calls out and says “Greetings”. It tilts its head and mimics Mara’s waving. It fades away after holding up one finger, then the lights form a triangle pointing down before they shoot down in the direction the triangle was pointing through a cloud below, creating a small tunnel in the cloud that Mara could see through
	- She looks through the tube and sees 1 spider ship & 2 Nyogi ships that we encountered before fly underneath the ship
