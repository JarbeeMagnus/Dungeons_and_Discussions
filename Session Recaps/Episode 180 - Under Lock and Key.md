## Detailed Notes
**What are we trying to prove?**
We are absolutely willing to pay the fines for having the Sterling goods - it is our fault that we didn't come to the authorities sooner. 
We were checking on one of our recent hires because her mother isn't fond of her joining our crew. We stumbled upon the goods half-submerged in Cinderbog Cemetery, near Panner's Gulch. We couldn't just leave all of those guns lying around, someone could have gotten hurt! So we took them to keep an eye on them and keep them safe; we were planning on returning them once things calmed down. You see, we are adventurers, and powerful ones.

In our effort to be good citizens, we caught the attention of a ne'er-do-well that has been provoking us since we got here. He seems to have some sort of crime organization in the underbelly of Prosperity, just out of reach from Sterling. After all, you're very busy with the influx of ships from the Scro War, so you'd have less time and resources to catch him.

But why would you take my word for it? Since we arrived, we have done multiple good deeds for the people of Prosperity:
- Highnoon 7 - turned in vampirate & thrall
- Highnoon 8 - helped a family reunite with their missing dog
- Highnoon 11 - we provided free healing to the people in the market
- Highnoon 12 - we resurrect a spa attendant that was killed by the vampirate we turned in; we also save a bartender from some kind of mage
- Highnoon 14 - we resurrect an innocent man that was killed by this crime-boss
- Highnoon 16 - we broke up a fight that had started in The Voyager Grande hallways
- Highnoon 17 (today) - we go to the library to find out how we can turn the guns in without getting framed; we save the library from some psycho's lizards

We suspect that the crime-boss alerted the authorities of our "stolen" goods (that we found and were planning on returning) and submitted false reports of our people being violent (we wouldn't keep bad company, refer back to our good deeds & Jemzer planning on donating much of her casino winnings to a local temple)
- He was working with the vampirate that we brought into custody and was *somehow* released - twice! How could that have happened? We know Sterling Company is an honorable company that wouldn't be easily swayed by bribes
- We own the two ships that have been impounded (show paperwork), yet somehow they got taken into custody without our knowledge. How strange...

We are willing to work with Sterling Company to bring this man and his cohorts to justice. I propose that we bring one of his higher-ups into custody. If he manages to get released or escapes somehow, you'll have just cause to pursue the crime-boss more broadly. And if he doesn't get released, you'll have a man from the inside of the organization that you can question and get more information out of.

What do you think?

What we know...
**General Info:**
- There has been a sudden influx of ships the past few months due to Scro War
- There's a parking ramp behind the Voyager Grande for expensive ships - this is reserved for VIPs of the casino, which are people that have won 50k+ gold; the Aminatu was kept here after it was impounded the first time

**Sabaal & his gang:**
- Rivaled by The Brass Vipers, headed by a centaur
- Sabaal lives at the casino - in the 2nd highest floor; He employs at least 3 Sterling Deputies
- Adizar is the 2nd in command & she seems to have a rocky relationship with Sabaal (Adizar says if we find the shipment for her, she would be willing to ally with us, unclear if she would keep it herself or give to Sabaal); Sabaal reprimands her harshly after the job falls through
- Adizar purchased her bone sentinels from Tulgan (or at least he supplied them)

**Sterling Company**
- Baron Bertram Graywater is the head of Sterling Company, which is the law around town
	- The Sterling Company was established about 50 years ago
	- They use Sterling Hounds and Deputies (automatons)
	- Bertram owns The Voyager Grande
	- Sterling employees do not seem to take bribes (Mara tried on Highnoon 9)
	- There is a jailhouse inside The Voyager Grande, which is where they bring Nadiif after he kills the spa attendant. He is released later that day
- Interactions with Sterling Company:
	- Highnoon 9 & 12 & 16 - the party goes to The Voyager Grande, so Sterling Deputies would have seen them enter/leave
	- Highnoon 13 - Orevana would have been seen by Deputies going into Sabaal's apartment, the art gallery, then the restaurant he was eating in
	- Highnoon 15 - Hollia tells a Sterling post office she was looking to drop of a private package to the Dawnstrider Ranch
	- Highnoon 16 (night) - Peri and Tryst have a fight in the hallway of The Voyager Grande, which alerts Sterling Deputies; Orevana breaks up the fight after Tryst is way down the hall
- The shipment of weapons that Sabaal tried to intercept was headed for Fort Harmony. Bertram (the head of Sterling Company) has a vault there and often ships valuables hidden in sealed caches among other shipments. Sabaal wanted to intercept and steal the valuables within that particular weapons shipment - but it never arrived. When burst open, we find out the contents of the inner cache include gems, a dagger (non-magical), an animal skull, and the key piece
- Sabaal was planning on selling the contents of the inner cache to someone (Nectis). Felix was going to double-cross Sabaal and sell the goods instead
