## Summary
The party successfully stops the ambush by cactus spiders, although Hollia starts a conversation with Mara about thinking about being a man that causes her to avoid Mara. Once the spiders are taken care of, Felix tells us that he stole the shipment that Sabaal was trying to get and that it was Orevana's palm reading that gave him the courage to go through with the plan. The boxes are hidden in a partially-submerged cemetery nearby so Hollia flies us while following Felix's directions. While on the say, Orevana asks Peri about the flowers from her dream, then talks to Hollia about what yellow chrysanthemums represent (memory). Orevana also asks Hollia and Peri if they want to throw a mushroom party later. At the cemetery, Hollia and Orevana have some girl-time while wading through the mud since the others flew out ahead of them. Peri and Felix go into the mausoleum and grab one of the boxes - only it wasn't the box but some kind of sarcophagus! Peri quickly runs out with one of the two boxes as the mausoleum converges and stands up as some kind of golem. A fight ensues as we fought the creature as well as various undead cowboys. Felix was stuck inside the body of the mausoleum with the second box. Orevana rushed forward and ghost-stepped inside to rescue him and retrieve the box. On the way out, the box breaks open and spill out the weapons along with a smaller box, which she grabs remembering Sabaal mentioning valuables in the shipment. As the party gathers up after the fight, Felix is suddenly grabbed by some kind of invisible air elemental and flies into the air.

## Detailed Notes
**DATE: Highnoon 15**
- Cactus Spider Fight!
    - Giant swarm and one MOMMY spider
    - Hollia and Mara casually chat about whether or not Mara has thought about being a man (she hasn't); neither has Hollia
    - Orevana uses Hold Monster on Mommy and Peri uses a giant Guiding Bolt to get Mommy low; then Michael uses disintegrate to finish the job
    - Peri, Mara, and Michael easily clean up the swarm of babies
- After the fight, Felix tells us that he stole the package that Sabaal was trying to steal from Sterling Company
    - It's hidden in a mausoleum that's in a partially submerged cemetery near the river
    - It was Orevana's fortune that pushed him to go through with double-crossing Sabaal
    - Mara explains how we have a vampire on the ship to Martine and Felix - they're both a bit baffled but come along with us
    - Martine calls Felix "Goopy"
    - Felix leaned that Sabaal was planning on selling the contents of the cache to someone (he doesn't know who). Felix was going to double-cross Sabaal and sell the goods to the mystery buyer instead
- Hollia starts taking us to where Felix hid the cache
    - While on the way, she tells Felix he was smart to hide the cache where he did (he seems to get emotional over the compliment)
    - Hollia is purposefully avoiding conversation with Mara
    - Orevana pulls Peri aside to ask him about the yellow flowers from her vision (chrysanthemums)
    - Orevana, not knowing much about chrysanthemums, approaches Hollia to understand more. She uses the guise of having chatted about flowers with Peri. Hollia confirms that they are used in funerals and that the colors add meaning; White signals mourning, yellow leans more towards remembrance
    - Orevana asks Hollia if she's done mushrooms (no) & wants to throw a shroom party with the whole crew
- We arrive at the partially submerged cemetery (Cinderbog Cemetery)
    - Michael, Mara, and Peri fly over the water, leaving Hollia and Orevana walking arm-in-arm through the mud (and later Orevana gives Hollia a piggy-back ride). They keep chatting about how Hollia seems to be avoiding Mara - she confesses to Orevana that she's embarrassed about her questions during the spider fight
    - Felix directs Peri into the mausoleum to find the stash, but it's so dark that they have to feel around for it. They find a box in the water and yank it up - POP!
- Suddenly the water is flooded with glowing green light from the box that Peri and Felix opened
    - The mausoleum starts lifting up while dead spectral cowboys start emerging from the graves nearby
    - A monstrosity made up of coffins and mausoleum parts emerges (wielding a shovel)
    - Peri manages to grab one of the two caches and get out before the creature fully stands, but Felix is stuck inside with the 2nd box!
- We FIGHT! Some highlights:
    - Hollia turns ALL OF the undead - including the giant mausoleum creature
    - Orevana leaps onto the mausoleum's midsection and ghost steps inside. She grabs the second box and orders Felix to go into her bag
    - Peri uses his big staff magic to do a shitload of radiant damage
    - Mara peppers the mausoleum creature with eldritch blasts
    - Hollia trips and falls into the mud while turning more undead
    - Michael uses thunder whip to try to yank the creature into the air but it was too strong and resisted
    - Orevana pops out the bottom of the creature holding the box - it breaks open on the impact and she sees there was a smaller box inside. She grabs it, remembering Sabaal mentioning a cache of valuables
    - Peri summons Filly-Loo (the fey ferret) and it commands the monster first to drop its shovel, then later to grovel
    -  Peri blows up the creature in a supernova of light
- After the fight:
	- Orevana pulls Felix out of her bag of holding - he is unharmed
	- The group starts to gather after the fight
	- Suddenly, Felix's midsection is squeezed and he flies into the air. Orevana, having casted See Invisibility, can see that he has been taken by some kind of invisible air elemental
