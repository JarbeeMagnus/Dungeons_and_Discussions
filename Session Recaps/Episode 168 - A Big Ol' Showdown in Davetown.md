## Detailed Notes
- We’re at the mouth of the cave in Davetown
	- Michael is carrying a box
	- As we enter the cave, the light is different. Pink and blue lines start to appear on the walls and turn into some kind of trippy tunnel (groovy) and gentle piano music starts to play. The cave entrance closes behind us
	- Daaa'v says it’s all because of the psychic surgery he will perform on Michael
	- We proceed forward and the cave turns into a gold and turquoise hallway. He says it’s from his memory of a “coup on Zithu” 600 million years ago
	- The scene changes into a crazy cataclysm of destruction from 90 million years ago when he decimated an area to retrieve artifacts (in the Ulon Dwari sphere)
	- We find out D’aav has been repressing his emotions and prefers to be chill. He re-iterates that this is better than “endless ocean” and the scenery changes to show it
	- He says that the gods cursed “us” a billion years ago with endless life (he can’t die)
	- Mara suddenly finds the edge of the cave with her foot but she manages to re-balance herself. D’aav drops the illusion for us and we see we’re in the cave 
- We see D’aav - he’s a giant squid creature in the water. We also see the white creatures floating in the water on their backs
	- He tells us that Michael was transforming because he was in the “cloud”. He says it’s different from when he makes normal Daves
	- The party is split about what to do next. D’aav says the psychic surgery entails reaching into Michael’s mind and removing the things in his brain that caused him to transform
	- There’s a portal in the bottom of the mine - that’s why the chamber is filled with water
	- Orevana casts death ward on Michael just in case. Hollia casts a defensive circle around him as well. When D’aav reaches towards Michael, he gets hurt by the defensive circle. Michael convinces him to do the surgery through the pain - so D’aav continues
	- When the defensive zone wears out, Hollia does an actual attack against D’aav and he gets upset. Orevana attempts to restrain Hollia and asks D’aav to try again. He claims to want to help
	- The room changes into some kind of egg chamber for a few moments, but it flickers back to the room shortly after
	- D’aav successfully completes the surgery
	- D’aav looks into Orevana’s mind and says words (laid out in Article below)
	- We find out D’aav’s telepathic range is 1 mile, so we go outside that range so we can talk privately
- The party is divided about what to do about D’aav (Quest: Destroy Cult of Daaa'v)
	- Orevana, Michael, and Peri think we should kill the people of Davetown - Hollia vehemently disagrees
	- Mara and Hollia think we should kidnap one person to see what happens - perhaps with time, the connection would sever
	- We could also collapse the cave onto D’aav - though Orevana thinks it won’t be enough to actually trap or kill him
	- Michael thinks of an idea of how to defeat D’aav (to use portent)
	- There’s also that portal at the bottom of the cave - perhaps we could close it and banish/kill him 
- Michael contacts other plane:
	- Are we powerful enough to kill D’aav (yes)
	- Powerful enough to kill D’aav and the whole town (quite possible yes)
	- Will tin foil hats protect us (awful)
	- If we remove a mind-controlled resident of Davetown from the presence of D’aav, will the connection sever (if you take them away, yes)
	- Do they need to be further than 5 miles away (no) 
- We coast back to town to decompress. Back at The Ru, we find Jilly giving a tour to 2 people we don’t recognize
	- She introduces them as a security manager (Martine) and a bossman, a position which leads the deck hands (Kiro)
	- Jilly got their paperwork together and left it for Mara to review
	- Orevana plays Drakes (the "Go Fish" cardgame) with Kiro and Martine - Kiro turns out to be a very good card player
	- Mara and Hollia head below decks to talk to Zellhane. She talks about what a lovely time she and the others had at the hotel - specifically that the spa was amazing. They also ran into Jemzer and thought she was a great fit
	- Hollia writes up the events of the day into her journal and then gives Mara a foot rub
	- That concludes Day 4 (we get a good night’s rest) 

TD Date: Highnoon 11, 1069
Faerun Date: Eleasias 9, 1509

- We wake up to hammering while Togor starts working on the ship
	- We decide to take it easy today
	- We check for posters and find: Limewashing, some changeling impersonating a Charlie, and an artist class asks for bears to draw - Posters in Prosperity
- What we do today:
	- Michael goes to buy scrolls of fly and/or levitate
		- The shopkeep is in good spirits
		- Michael buys a Fly scroll (which does not, in fact, turn you into a fly)
		- After purchasing it, he goes to Annie’s Blue Rose to copy it into his spellbook
		- Once Peri leaves (see below), Michael and Tally chat nonsensically for a bit before Michael proceeds to ignore Tally 
	- Peri decides to go to a bar - Annie’s Blue Rose
		- He sees a riveting dance performance from a woman on a stage & she buys a round of drinks
		- Peri lays a pile of money on the bar and asks if it’s enough to get drunk. She takes a few and gives the rest back to him
		- He puts a second tankard down in front of an open seat
		- Peri nurses his drink & doesn’t allow anyone to sit in front of the other tankard. After a few hours, Michael joins him
		- Peri mentions he thought Tally would join him - which Michael hears a scoff above them in response. Michael confirms that Tally is here and Tally decides to join the conversation - he remains snarky with Peri and pretended to drink the beer while spilling it onto the floor
		- They talk about how Peri had a bad day and how he wished they killed D’aav. Tally mentions that Orevana also abandoned him but Peri defends her
		- Peri gets fed up and misty steps out of the bar 
	- Orevana and Hollia went to set-up a tent in the market for fortune telling and free diagnosis (25gp to rent a stall)
		- Orevana hocks for people to come in for various fortune telling, palm reading, connect with dead loved ones, etc.
		- Hollia makes her sign glow to get people to come in. She helps a guy with a dislocated shoulder
		- A few more people get checked by Hollia
		- Some people approach Orevana for future readings and to check on loved ones
		- A blob man wearing a cowboy hat comes by. She does a tarot reading for him and got a middling result that she swings slightly positive. He says he’s going to make the decision that he was wavering on and happily leaves
		- After a while, Peri shows up to get diagnosed by Hollia - he’s been feeling sad and useless since leaving Ravnica. He doesn’t feel like we’ve been doing enough to save people. Hollia reassures him that it’s a journey. They bond over hating Tally. Peri admits that he thinks he’s feeling hatred
	- Mara decides to take down any Davetown posters she can find
		- She finds one in a run-down part of town
		- Mara tries to convince a woman not to go to Davetown by telling her there’s a monster there (the woman seems a little upset about it)
		- “Davetown is no good! BADtown!” Mara looks for markings around town to get a feel for the town - safe places, “good” streets, “bad” streets
		- Mara meets with Hollia, Orevana, and Peri at the stall. She makes a dirty joke about Hollia “diagnosing” her and Orevana and Peri NOPE out 
- We all meet up in Annie’s Blue Rose
	- Michael is still there with Tally. When we arrive, Tally waves and floats off invisibly
	- Michael wiggles his hand and casts Fly on all of us
	- We all get drunk. Mara gives Peri a pep talk. Orevana leaves to go home with someone. Hollia accidentally flies and crashes while asking Mara to dance with her. Mara uses fly to be Hollia’s height while they dance
	- Michael attempts to trap Tally
	- After 34 beers, Peri finally gets drunk (and throws up outside). He offers another guy who was barfing next to him a position on the ship. The guy says "I'll need to sell my ship, but sure! I'll see you soon"
