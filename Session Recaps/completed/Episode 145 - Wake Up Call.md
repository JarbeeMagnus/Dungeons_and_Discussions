## Summary
We find Ljudo Jr whimpering in his bedroom with some kind of glass device installed into his spike/head. While trying to pull it off of him, we end up killing him and releasing some kind of eyeball parasite that had been inside. It runs to Donwec and blinks out of existence with him. Orevana scries on Donwec and sees him get an "intermediary device" installed in some sort of metallic organic room. Mara and Peri find a zombie butler downstairs and he informs them that there are approximately 50 guards outside waiting for us. As the team discusses what to do next, Orevana rips Ljudo's head off and uses Speak with Dead. We learn more about the deal with the Neogi and Hollia successfully removes the rest of the device from the spine of Ljudo's corpse.

## Detailed Notes
- We enter a room in the hallway into Ljudo Jr’s bedroom. We hear whimpering in the room next door (the closet). Peri hears him mumble about having “2 more weeks”
	- Ljudo is cowering with some kind of red/pink glass device attached to his back
	- While Michael tries to identify it, we go into initiative. During the fight, Ljudo was screaming, “*Get the intermediary out of my head!*”
	- We try to pry this device off/out of Ljudo Jr
	- Mara accidentally kills Ljudo Jr and a creature in a glass case pops out
	- The glass case creature steals Donwec and blinks out of existence
- Hollia determines that the glass device attached to Ljudo Jr is some kind of parasite. She thinks she could remove it if in an operating theater
- Peri heals the team while Orevana starts trying to scry Donwec
- Hollia takes Mara into the hallway and uses “breath of life” on her - via a big smooch!
- After a few more minutes, Mara looks around across the hall (guest rooms) and toward the stairs. She finds a zombie butler waiting in the living area downstairs
- Michael moves to one of the guest rooms to continue resting
- At this time, Orevana’s scrying finishes. She learns:
	- Donwec is in a dark room, lying face-down on a table
	- Outside the room, she can hear metallic clanking
	- After a bit, the eyeball creature comes into the room with another body device floating nearby. She watches as the eyeball creature installs the device into Donwec’s back
	- The room is made of metal that seems to be similar to the metal parts from the creature. The architecture is organic-shaped
- Mara goes back to the zombie butler. Peri trails behind her slightly
	- His name is Komornik, and he is the house servant. He asks if “his master”, Ljudo Jr, is alright. Mara tells him Ljudo Jr is alive (which he seems disappointed by)
	- Komornik also asks about Donwec. Mara tells him. He knows about the eyeball creature but does not owe any allegiance to it. He knows the location is somewhere in the encampment
	- He says there are 40 human guards and 8 giant guards waiting outside
	- He asks if we can kill Ljudo Jr so he can get a quick (and merciful) death and thus a 1-7 day break per his contract
- We reconvene and exchange information
- Peri searches the bedroom and closet for valuables. He finds some kind of Orzhov ceremonial totem and a small coin purse with 100 Zinos inside
- Orevana cuts Ljudo’s head off - we find that there were some kind of metal tube things going into his brain
- Peri kills the butler (two separate sighs of relief)
- Mara decides to cut the device off of Ljudo Jr to show Mephrosa - Hollia comes to help
- We use Speak with Dead on Ljudo Jr:
	1. What are the details of the deal, named “Project Firmament”, that you made with the Neogi? *My father made the deal 2 decades ago to provide bodies to the Neogi, who take them off of Ravnica and bring back the souls and the husks. The husks are given to the Golgari, who also transport the souls to Vizkopa Bank. Once we have completed Project Requiem, the Orzhov will be granted dominance over Ravnica by the Nulviir*
	2. Where do you go to meet with the Neogi? *They arrive at the Waystation approximately every 3 weeks. I have not met with them more than once*
	3. Where were you when you got the intermediary device installed? *The intermediary took me to the Monolith. It was there that it was implanted into me*
	4. Does the family have any secret exits that can be used to escape the citadel in case of emergency? *There are hidden tunnels under the citadel to move troops around the area. Teleportation circle, North tower of the citadel*
	5. What do the Neogi and Nulviir get out of Project Firmament/Project Requiem? *We provide the Neogi with living bodies, 5,000 per shipment. The Neogi take a cut for themselves. The Nulviir take most, we are given the rest*
- Hollia finishes removing the intermediary device
