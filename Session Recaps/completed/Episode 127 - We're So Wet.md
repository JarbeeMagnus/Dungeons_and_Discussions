## Summary
Pannikin brings us to Luka where he is given an unexpected delivery of an Orzhov coffin, requiring him to leave immediately. He directs us to an inn and heads off. The party is able to find the inn and stay the night, free of charge. The next day, we get some food at a Selesnya wannabe restaurant and head closer to the Reclamation Zone once Hollia divinates where Ljudo Jr. is. On the 3rd day of travel, Hollia successfully uses Divine Intervention and teleports us into a swamp. We are shortly found by a brain in a robot suit (A.R.P.) and he brings us to a tower nearby where Grizzut Zot Zot is waiting - along with Captain Zellhane!

## Detailed Notes
Pannikin sails us into some clouds, low to the ground. He always referred to his zeppelin as “Bronka”; she seemed happy to be in the rain. The party checks on Donwic to make sure he knows to keep his hood up. Most of the time the past few days, he seemed lost in thought. Pannakin brings us to where he will be dropping off the goods. He asked us to stay on the ship while he completes the drop-off. It's 3-4am when we arrive on the 20th. We descend to a dock as Pannikin uses levers to adjust Bronka into a smooth landing. The dock has large letters indicating: WARD 49 STRATEM 13 NORTH DOCK. A large cyclops loads and unloads boats along the canal. A krovad (bull-like creature) is being loaded in the background. Pannikin cursed under his breath as he notices an Izzet observo-sphere nearby. Mara offers to help and he insists that we stay inside the ship.  
  
Pannikin brings a cart down the loading ramp and approaches the pavilion near the observo-sphere  A dark, shiny figure leaves the building to talk to Pannikin (he may have called the figure “Sarouka”). The party is not able to hear what they are saying from where they are in the ship. After a quick chat with the shiny figure, few dock workers take a large, coffin-sized box out of the observo-sphere and puts it on the cart. Pannikin leads them back to the ship and tells us to do and say nothing as he has us step aside to load the box into the hold. There is an ornate Orzhov design on the box. Orevana takes Donwic’s hand to make sure he doesn’t make any sudden movements. Mara whispered “distract him if he moves” to Orevana. Pannikin tells us that he was not expecting to return with anything, but he needs to leave right away. He can’t take us any closer to the reclamation site. He tells us there is a hostel nearby called The Sad Man’s Tongue run by a Goblin named Miraji. If we go there and say Pannikin sent us, he’ll let us stay there.

Pannikin escorts us out of the ship and then went back inside to fly the ship away. We think he took the ship South.  
  
We head out of the dock area towards where he told us The Sad Man’s Tongue was. We leave through a gate and over a bridge that is 300ft above the ground. The canal was on a raised track. We go through another gate and wind our way through a town to find The Sad Man’s Tongue. A goblin is at the desk with a pen between his toes, writing a note. The goblin does not appear to have any arms. Mara tells the goblin that Pannikin sent us. He gets down from the desk and into a wheelchair. He scoots along and giggled, “*I do this because I thought it would be funny*”. He takes us down to the end of the hall and tells us the night is free of charge because we are friends with Pannikin. He asked us to stay quiet to not disturb the other patrons. There are a few other people in the room sleeping as the party gets ready to rest for the night. Kullic and Mara both wash up in the bathroom. Hollia casts a Glyph of Warning on her dress. Orevana takes a creepy doll out and sets it near her bed. Donwic asks if they are sleeping there for the night and Orevana said yes. He nods and lies down with his back to her. When she asked if he was okay he said that he was just tired. The party sleeps through the night and wake up in the late morning.

They go to the front room to talk to Miraji and ask for information about the map. He tells them that we are in the 49th Ward of Luka and the 13th Stratem. He explains that Stratems are layers to the city - we are in the top layer of this section, with 12 Stratems below. He also says that the lower levels tend to have more Guildless and they are “dirtier”. He asked us why we were in town. He laughed when Mara suggested work in the reclamation zone. He said even travel to the zone is dangerous with the infighting between Rakdos and Gruul. According to Moraji, the fastest way into the reclamation zone would be taking a barge on the canal, but raiders often attack ships. Moraji also says that we could take a locopede if we got a job with Lashka. He also said there are acid pools and undead from something that happened 200 years ago. He said that the story most people accept is that an old Izzet barrier broke down and destroyed the area. He said that the closest Lashka Conglomerate recruitment office isn’t far from the hostel. He said there is a restaurant nearby called The Glass House. Donwic seems distant during the entire conversation.  
  
The party leaves and decides to head toward The Glass House. The weather is overcast and lightly raining. There are various people walking by and many street food vendors. There is a hasta station nearby. A guy is sitting outside the station eating noodles. Mara says hello as they pass by and he gives her the stink eye. The Glass House has glass walls and fake trees outside. A group leaves and smell strongly of wine. Mara asked if the food was good and they said “*we’re just here for the fun*”. The decor inside is all leafy and flowery (all fake, of course). It feels like a Selesnyian Teahouse but slightly off. They are told to seat themselves. The staff are all dressed up. The "centaur" names Clotis comes to take our order, six of all three menu items - wine, a dainty sandwich, and an overpriced cookie.

Mara starts discussing next steps, so Orevana takes Donwec around the store to look around. She asks Donwec what’s wrong and he tells her how he’s been thinking about killing his brother. He destroys a dryad toy in the process, which Orevana purchases. She asks him if there is anything they can do to help and he pulls her into the bathroom to have sex. She goes along with him.  
  
Back at the table, Clotis brings seven wines, sandwiches, and cookies. The food is slightly moldy, the wine slightly sour, and the cookies are soggy. A short time later, Clotis returns with a stringed instrument and asks if we want entertainment. They said yes and he plays a song (badly). Mara tips him for his time and the party decides they will get street food after this. Hollia asks for better wine and tells Clotis she wants 25 zinos worth of wine. He asks to see the zinos first, which Hollia takes out of her portable chest. He scoops the zinos up and brings out a fancy Rakdos bottle. It is 25 zinos for a glass. Kullic offers to buy Clotis a glass after his shift ends, but his shift never ends so he cannot have the wine. The wine gives off a strong peppery smell and looks fantastic. Hollia pours the wine into a rune on the table and casts divination by asking, “*Where is Ljudo Jr right now?*” The wine pattern swirls around and forms shapes on the table. It forms the letter K, then O, R, E, N - which is in the middle of the reclamation zone. Shortly after, Orevana returns with a slightly disheveled Donwec. Kullic pays and leaves a tip. They get some food from vendors nearby (cheese pastries / mushrooms). There are canals all throughout the Stratems. They take an elevator to the 7th Stratem to take a public barge and take transfers to continue getting closer to the reclamation zone. They stop for food and rest as needed. On the third day, Hollia succeeds on her Divine Intervention to teleport them to Koren.

The party is suddenly standing in a dark, murky swamp. There is a tower in the middle distance (~300ft away). Everyone is surprised to be suddenly at their destination. Orevana even throws up. They notice the tower gives off a pulsing energy. Michael and Peri work together to try to find Ljudo Jr. Michael scries Ljudo Jr and sees him doing paperwork in an office. Outside it is clear and sunny. People outside are building buildings. The party is in a location that is currently dark and rainy, so they know he can’t be that close. Peri says Ljudo Jr is about 780 km past the tower. Suddenly, they hear a rhythmic robotic sound coming towards them, followed by a green light. They decide to hide to see what is approaching. They see a large spherical robot that appears to have a brain inside the top walking down the path. The robot pauses and picks a flower using a retractable arm.  
  
The party decides to follow, but Kullic’s leg gets stuck in the mud and makes a sucking sound. The robot turns and says “*Halt!*” and recognizes Peri and Mara. They say “*Hey, A.R.P.*” and he let us know that he and Grizzut Zot Zot were waiting for us. ARP invites them inside the tower and the party follows him. Orevana makes Donwic invisible and keeps him close-by. ARP types a pattern of runes into the door panel to open it, which Hollia memorizes. ARP tells them to dry off and get comfortable before he goes off and plugs himself in. The robot body opens and the brain comes out on a unicycle. Peri asks for the name of the tower, which ARP explains the tower doesn’t have a name. Hollia decides to name it the “Pulsating Tower”, which delighted ARP. He brings them upstairs to have some food. He explains that Grizzut knew that we were coming. At the top of the stairs, they meet a man. “*Just as the firemind told me you were coming*”. He also says that they’re going to fix Mara’s ship. From around the corner, a woman comes out from the shadows. Mara recognized her as Captain Zellahne.
