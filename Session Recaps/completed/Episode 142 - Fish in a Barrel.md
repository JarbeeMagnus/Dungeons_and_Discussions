## Summary
We split the party to gather as much information from within the Operations & Logistics Tower as possible. Mara and Belia go into the library and find various maps, schematics, and records related to Project Requiem and the domes where the djinn are being held. Peri and Michael work together on another fly-spy mission following Yunaska into the teleportation room. They learn that there are 4 portals leading out of the Logistics Tower and believe three go to the various MC domes (the forth is a mystery). They also see Yunaska return from a portal with a Guild Mage discussing a "surprise at dinner tomorrow". Hollia decodes the pocketbook Mara stole previously and gets a bunch of statistics about Project Requiem. Orevana and Donwec share a moment when he thanks her for helping him. Once we all reconvene, we scry on Grizzut and see he's being held in a room, unconscious, and the room brickwork matches the Logistics Tower - the group quickly finds him but we are interrupted by Yunaska and the Guild Mage.

## Detailed Notes
- We go into the lunchroom and Mara takes out the notebook she palmed previously
	- The notebook appears to have a lot of industry jargon and the words are encoded
	- Mara takes a few minutes to start decoding and quickly realizes we’ll need literal hours of time to fully decode/descramble the entire book
	- Mara hands the book off to a very excited Hollia (“IS THAT A CYPHER?!”). Hollia begins working on decoding, but even with her skill it will still take a few hours
	- Mara then approaches Peri and asks about going small and doing recon on the teleportation room (floor 9). Michael casts non-detection on Peri
	- Mara asks Donwec about what he remembers about his cousin. He says he doesn’t know her too well - but she was physically there when he had his ‘accident’
	- We ask Donwec to stay in the room and practice his swordsmanship
- NOTE: Michael has told the party about Grizzut
- ANOTHER NOTE: Mara gave the party handfuls of Zinos from the party fund to use as needed (for example, the toll between the 5th and 6th floors)
- Mara and Belia will go into the stacks (floor 7 & 8). Belia decides they should start with floor 7
	- At first glance, it looks like a typical library you’d find at a collage; the shelves are on wheels and can be moved back and forth to create space between certain areas. There are tomes on a desk along one wall shadowed in darkness
	- There don’t seem to be anyone else in the room, but a spirit whisks by at some point from one end of the room to the other
	- The shelves have exclusively tubes, so Mara approaches the desk of tomes
	- The first book has information on the infrastructure of the mining site
	- Mara and Belia start looking for anything that says “Project Firmament”. After 3 minutes, they hear footsteps behind them. Mara glances behind her and sees a Vedalken approaching them
	- He approaches them and sputters in disbelief. Mara says she’s a friend of Yunaska and uses suggestion to convince him to help her
	- They butter-up the Vedalken by complementing how organized the library is - he is receptive
	- Mara doesn’t know what to ask about, so Belia steps in and almost fully blows our cover when she said she’s from off-planet. She covers up the blunder by saying she’s been drinking all day
	- The Vedalken takes them to the 8th floor to review the schematics and records for *Project Requiem*
	- She learns this project has been ongoing for about 20 years. Target finish date is 9993 (currently it is 9986, almost 9987)
	- The Vedalken mentions that the records on these floors are ones that are available to engineers; higher important records are on level 10
	- We check on the MC domes: The domes’ architecture are identically laid out. Belia is able to give some direction on the set-up of how the domes work
	- She also gets information on how the Waystation is set-up (a map!)
	- Belia (finally) finds out that the Vedalken Recordkeeper (Mara later calls him “Scrollmaster”) Omersk. He explains that the Waystation “sends people home”
	- We also find out the Orzhov are working with the Golgari Swarm, specifically in the Waystation
	- NOTE: We already know that there are husks and souls being differentiated from the Volenco/Ljudo Jr conversation from the Bank Recon mission (Xivaskir 28). We’re presuming the Golgari get the husks and the Orzhov get the souls
	- The decommissioned pass is a tunnel between the Garrison and the Waystation that they collapsed about 15 years ago
	- ANOTHER NOTE: There is a decommissioned machine with a driller arm that we saw on the way in
- Peri and Michael are working on the recon mission on floor 9
	- Peri turns into a fly and lands on Michael
	- Michael casts invisibility on fly Peri
	- Michael approaches the guards and then looks confused and says “oh” and promptly turns around and hangs out in the staircase just out of sight
	- Soon, Yunaska comes down the stairs and enters the teleportation room. There was some kind of sound when she opened the door (some kind of tone)
	- Peri follows Yunaska into the room. There is some kind of “console” in the room with bowls of water and crystals throughout. The rest of the room is bare save the console and a teleportation circle nearby. This room seems to extend and curve around the whole tower. This room seems to be replicated three more times around the tower for a total of 4
	- Yunaska pivoted to the right immediately upon entering and walked into that teleportation circle (#4) and disappeared. There were operators at the console at the time
	- There are about 8 guards sprinkled about the room. There are a handful of other people operating the consoles
	- After some time, a centaur dressed as an Izzet Guild Mage emerges from one of the teleportation circles (#1). A human male Guild Mage emerges from another circle (#3) shortly after. They exchange crystals to be used in “Dome 1”. They also mentioned something about charging them. Peri gets the sense that the crystals are running low
	- Michael sends a message to Peri asking what’s going on after 30min
	- Soon after, Yunaska emerges from circle #4 talking to someone (an ornately dressed mage). “*Gather them and bring them to the Grand Hall tomorrow*” for a “*surprise at dinner*”
	- Peri follows them out of the room and un-flies behind Michael. They both descend to meet back up with the team back in the room
- Hollia, Orevana, and Donwec stay together in the room while Hollia decodes the notebook
	- Hollia discovers the following in the notebook:
		-  There are 32,000 workers in and out each day, with 12-hour shifts
		- She confirms the completion date is 9993 (*this is what Mara found, too*)
		- Target depth is 5 km (3.1 miles). Currently they are 2.2 miles down
		- "Avg worker viability duration" is a statistic that keeps popping up. After this period (14 weeks/100 days), they are sent to the Waystation
	- Orevana catches Hollia mumbling about how dry the book is and how she needs a drink
	- Donwec tells Orevana he doesn’t know how to thank her for the help; she says there’s no need to thank her and helps him practice various assassination moves. Hollia jumps in to give her medical opinion on the matter
	- Shortly after, Michael and Peri arrive followed by Mara and Belia
- We exchange information
- Michael demands that Belia create a “Michael action figure”
- ANOTHER NOTE: The counterveil anchors (what we need to take down the magic domes) are with Grizzut
- We conduct a séance so Orevana can add Scrying to her spell list. She scries Grizzut
	- He’s in a bare stone room, tied up on a cot
	- There are no windows or doors anywhere - it is fully enclosed
	- Orevana notices the stonework matches the command tower we’re currently in
	- Grizzut was also gagged
- Michael demonstrates the “door opening” sound from level 9
- Hollia and Peri team up to find Grizzut via Locate Creature and Find The Path
	- Hollia senses Grizzut is up above us
	- Peri’s path shows him that we need to go up the stairs. We go up to the 10th floor
	- The path continues past Yunaska’s office and down a short hallway to the right that ends in a dead end
	- We hear Yunaska’s footsteps and Orevana ghost steps into the wall. She ungags him and starts to untie him. He appears to be unconscious.
- Mara pretends that the group is lost as Yunaska and the mage round the corner (the mage is named Carodejnik)
	- Yunaska and the mage explain they want to REALLY surprise Ljudo and explains we need to go to another building. We will leave in the morning. Carodejnik advises we sleep on the 10th floor tonight
	- Yunaska says she can open a magical door for the group to sleep tonight that has a bucket for when we need to use the bathroom.. “The timing is crucial” regarding where we sleep tonight. Michael notices her run her fingers across a keyring and he tells Mara about it (NOTE: future stealing!)
