## Summary
We execute Operation Heal Donwec and were *mostly* successful. Dimwec/Donwec underwent excruciating pain as his scar turned black and blistery. After he fell unconscious, the party managed to get him out of the crossbow shop and to the Selesnya Conclave since Peri had a friend there. She was unable to help us since Donwec is from Orzhov, but luckily he woke up shortly thereafter. He didn't have much information about Project Firmament, but he was able to give us a few details about his father. We bond with him over the next two weeks until the WINTER SMASH RALLY begins! We hear the rules and win the first event - only our team, Do-Drak, and one hobgoblin remained once the first leg was over.

## Detailed Notes
At Geyb’s Crossbow Shop, Dimwec arrives with his 4 guards a few minutes late because they stopped at MushRomold’s and got everyone Mulchie Meals with Roachmilk Shakes. Mara fed piggy Michael her food. Dimwec brought his own crossbow, but was very interested in some of the other crossbows on the wall. Dimwec and Orevana both grab a crossbow to shoot targets at the same time. Orevana hits the dummy in the head and Dimwec’s shot goes wide. He cheers, thinking he hit the head - Orevana does not correct him and celebrates his great hit. The next shots had a similar outcome. They continue taking shots as the rest of the team proceeds with their plan.  
  
Kullic goes outside and runs into one of Dimwec’s guards. He pushes him back and Kullic said he’d been drinking all day. Hollia is taking notes on what is happening nearby. Peri says he needs to poop and walks out of the private shooting area. He farts loudly in the changing area and summons the ferret in his bag. The ferret jumps out of the bag and steps in front of Opatrovatel’ka and says “Sleep” before spores come out of his back. Opatrovatel’ka pets the ferret and goes into the corner and falls asleep. Mara watches Opatrovatel’ka closely and positions herself in front of the curtain in case someone unexpected enters the room.

With Opatrovatel’ka incapacitated, Hollia approaches Dimwec and asks to examine him to potentially improve his aim. Orevana urges him to let her conduct an examination. Hollia took notes as words in the air as she walked around Dimwec. She sees blue and purple symbols orbiting Dimwec, but doesn’t note anything else out of the ordinary. She casts dispel magic to get rid of the floating symbols - it does not work. Hollia tries remove curse and it does not do anything either. Finally, she casts Greater Restoration and Dimwec immediately grabs his scar and screams.  
  
Dimwec falls down and starts convulsing. Mara convinces the guards to let Hollia treat him. Hollia treats him for a seizure, but cannot do anything to fully stop it. Orevana holds his head as Hollia treats him and he is dripping sweat. Dimwec convulses for minutes. His scar blisters and turns black. He falls unconscious and his breathing is shallow. The guards want to take Dimwec to Orzhov doctors, but Hollia says no. Deyb pokes his head in and is concerned, but the team convinces him to get smelling salts and water/rags. The salts do nothing. Hollia casts remove disease and it doesn’t seem to help. Orevana casts Cure Wounds and the blistering stops spreading as quickly. She continues casting Cure Wounds and the blisters dry up. Hollia casts Death Ward, just in case. Peri casts Healing Aura and the blisters continue healing, but the blackness remains.

Michael jumps down off the table and does figure 8’s around the guards’ legs before going outside and hopping up on a chair. Opatrovatel’ka wakes up and tries to push Orevana and Hollia away. Hollia says “don’t touch me” and he touches her again. Hollia casts Harm and Opatrovatel’ka is immediately covered in blisters and curls up and dies. Hollia convinces the guards that Dimwec’s blisters are spreading and they better leave. The guards shout and run out of the building. Michael turns himself into Prince and goes outside to divert any Boros to another location. They believe him.   
  
They now need to leave the building without attracting any attention to Dimwec. Orevana casts Invisibility on Peri who picks up Dimwec. The rest of the party yell about disease and run out. Kullic immediately knew the Prince guy at the door was Michael and says “*C’mon Michael, let’s go!*” as they run out of the building. Michael casts non-detection on Dimwec right before the party decides to take a carriage to Concordance to talk to Peri’s doctor friend. Michael turns back into himself on the way. Peri’s doctor friend is named Arin and she works for Selesna. Concordance is a big beautiful field with buildings around. Peri hands Dimwec to Orevana as the party enters a structure in the corner of the field.

Peri finds Arin quickly and she is very happy to see him. He tells her that Dimwec is an Orzhov noble and she is very concerned about this. Kullic says that Dimwec and Orevana are dating and, while they were shooting crossbows, he started having a seizure. Peri pulls a quilt out and Orevana puts Dimwec on it. Peri drops 3 drops on wine on the quilt and it turns into a stretcher. They bring him to a nearby table so that Arin can examine him further. Hollia gives a clinical diagnosis of the wound, blisters, and blackness on the scar area (and does not mention casting any spells). Arin asks Hollia questions that make it apparent that she does not know as much about healing that Hollia does. Arin said that Tristani could help (trio of fused Dryads that is the Guildmaster of Selesna), but not since Dimwec is Orzhov. She suggests taking him to a Simic clinic, which Orevana does not agree to (her arm is Simic).   
  
Suddenly, Dimwec starts coughing and wheezes. He sounds very different when he speaks. He says he needs to go home to pack and go to his brother and that his name is “Donwic”. He says he wants to kill his brother and starts laughing maniacally. He also admits to hating crossbows ever since his brother shot him when he was six. Opatrovatel’ka used to take care of him; he had no animosity towards the thrull. He tells them that Ljudo Jr took the family’s only zeppelid. He continued the manic laughter in between sentences. He asks Orevana if they will help him kill his brother - the party agrees.

Over the next two weeks, they keep Donwic hidden. They dyed his hair and he wore common clothing. Michael kept a constant non-detection spell going on Donwic. Kullic sneaks around construction sites in the Blister Coils and sees that there are higher-up Orzhovs looking for Dimwec. The blackness and blisters on the side of his head does not change. He calls it poison, but he doesn’t want Hollia to heal him. He wants his brother to see him “like this”. Mara teaches Donwic knife tricks over the 2-week period. Michael says it would be poetic for Donwic to kill his brother with a crossbow. Donwic agrees and has the party purchase him a cross bow along with a gauntlet with a dagger that can pop out (Assassin’s Creed style). Orevana “runs interference” so Donwic does not find out too much information about the party or their intentions. She is still a noble, they are still her staff, and they live in the Garage because reasons. They totally bone.  
  
They are able to get some information on his family’s activities, but he didn’t have too many details since he was usually in his room or out doing PR for the family. He tells them that Ljudo Sr has been locked up in the manor house for the past three years - he never leaves. He is obsessed with being in the personal family chapel (he’s usually in there) and is constantly talking to himself but sometimes hears a thin, high-pitched voice answer him. Mara thinks perhaps that is the voice of The Emissary. Donwic does not know what’s going on in the 6th District, but that his father has property there that Jr was going to travel to, so he knows where Jr is. He confirms that he does not want to go back to living as Dimwec, as he lived like that for over 25 years.

**WINTER RALLYYYYYY**  
Donwic stays with Pivlic during the Winter Rally. Kullic explains that the Winter Rally is an illegal race that only happens when able (not annual). At the beginning of the rally, Pivlic makes a big speech at the beginning that the winner will get 10,000 zinos and 1 year of free food at Pivlichino’s. He outlines the rules, which are: No attacks against the Zeppelids themselves; Each stop needs to be completed (no skipping, teleporting, turning into mist, turning invisible, going through walls, no flying); Contestants cannot climb the outer area walls; No summoning creatures (no familiars); Magic to aid yourself or hurt others is encouraged; Dying is a very real possibility. The party’s pilot is Pannikin and Busalba is their engineer. They make quick introductions before the race. Do-Drak is there, notably with a team when he previously said he runs solo.  
  
The guns go off and we pull out in front. The Gruul team is launching missiles at us. Giants overhead throw boulders down, narrowly missing us. We arrive to the first event ahead of the other Zeppelids. The party gets a bottle of grease for arriving first that Michael takes. The challenge is called FETCH. They need to run through the arena to the end, grab the flag and make it back to the finishline before anyone else. Winner gets 10 points.  
  
The Rakdos team included flyers, so they immediately get disqualified. They proceed with causing havoc during the event until a wizard finally bamf’s them out. Other notable events from the fight include Michael’s giant hand summon, Hollia’s wall of daggers, and Peri turning into an elk to retrieve the flag. A giant ice creature appeared when he took the flag that did IMMENSE damage to the team. In the end, only our team, Do-Drak, and one hobgoblin survive to go onto the next event.
