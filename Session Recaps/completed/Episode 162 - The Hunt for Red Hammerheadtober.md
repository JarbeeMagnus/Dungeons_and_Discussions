## Summary
In the aftermath of the fight, Mara demands retribution so Michael Arcane Gates the party to the deck of the Aminatu. We find three Owuo priests doing some kind of ritual and kill them, but this seems to finish what they were doing and a strange man is summoned in the middle of them (Owuo himself). We continue fighting and Owuo kills Orevana. Hollia discovers her body and, in a panic, brings her back to life using Divine Intervention to retrieve her soul from the Raven Queen. Owuo then teleports us to Mechanus into a hut. He is shortly joined by Orkus - Owuo seems to be trapped by Orkus somehow. Orevana tries to appease Orkus but he notices she draws power from the Raven Queen ("another death god") and Owuo strikes Orkus on the head. The two gods fight and we slip out of the hut and down a large mountain of gears. We find a cave and rest for the night. The next morning, we are joined by a strange man in a Cosby sweater.

## Detailed Notes
- In the immediate aftermath of the fight, Hollia heals Zellhane and Jilly
- Mara demands to know where The Aminatu is, so Orevana casts see invisibility
	- She sees the Hammerhead ship above and upside-down about 200ft away
	- Orevana faerie fires the mangonel & people around it while Michael casts see invisibility
	- Michael creates a gate and we all (including Hollia) go through it 
- When we get onto the Hammerhead, we see three Owuo priests doing some kind of ritualistic chanting and black smoke coalescing in the middle of them
- We do some fighting and the three priests die and create some kind of man - we assume it’s Owuo. He demands to take Michael and called him a "Child of Chaos"
- Peri and Orevana fight Owuo and he retaliates by shooting some black smoke into Peri and the straight-up kills Orevana
- Orevana encounters the afterlife and speaks with the Raven Queen
- Michael finds out (by messaging the captain) that taking in the priests summoning Owuo was NOT his idea - “he forced us to take these priests, this wasn’t my idea!”
- Hollia goes to fetch Orevana to bring everyone back to the ship and discovers her dead body. Hollia, in a panic, uses Divine Intervention to bring Orevana back (a radiant book fetches Orevana from the death ocean)
- Owuo continues trying to take Michael back with him and he teleports the entire team onto Mechanus
	- Hollia tries to Word of Recall - it does not work
	- Mara calls for a lawyer - nothing happens
	- We are teleported into some hut, but still on Mechanus
	- Owuo speaks to some unknown entity and says “I have brought them to you, now release me!” and Orkus manifests inside the hut
	- Michael prays to Rakdos in his head (no response)
	- Hollia passes out from panic
	- Peri hears the many-tailed-fox in his ear
	- Orevana tries to appeal to Orkus by suggesting he throws a feast to celebrate. He looks closer and realizes she draws power from the Raven Queen (who he hates)
	- Michael then tries to appeal to Orkus by talking about Ravnica and how many souls are there for him to reap
- Owuo stands and cracks his staff on Orkus’ head and says he didn’t know we had the support of “another death god” and a fight between them ensues. We RUN out of the hut (including Peri jumping through the wall). We climb down a gear mountain with Orevana ferrying people one at a time as a giant bat. We get a few miles away and find some kind of gear room to hide out in
	- Orevana summons Leomund’s Tiny Hut and Hollia glyphs the door and puts 3 more on the ground toward the hut entrance
	- Michael turns into a piggy in the hopes of being harder to find
	- We fall asleep in the hut and rest for the night
