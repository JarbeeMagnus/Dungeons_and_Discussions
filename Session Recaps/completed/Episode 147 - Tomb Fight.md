## Summary
We defeat the spirit that was underneath Pracovisku Citadel and then investigate how to permanently get rid of it. Orevana uses her ghost tattoo to go down deep into the ground where she found human remains on a velvet pillow. Once blasted into dust, she came back up and we take a long rest. The next morning, we see the house is no longer looking haunted - and there are dozens of guards waiting for us outside. Peri and Michael work together to dimension door us all out without the guards noticing. We then proceed with rescuing Grizzut. Mara had previously stolen the key Yunaska used to open Grizzut's cell, so she gives that to Peri who turns into a fly to retrieve Grizzut. The plan goes without a hitch! We will proceed to the magic domes with Grizzut next time.

## Detailed Notes
- We start the night with immediate initiative - the spirit guy is not interested in discussion 
	- Once defeated, the ghost flies back into the sarcophagus. Orevana and Hollia approach and see a splash of ectoplasm in the bottom
	- Hollia discerns that the sarcophagus was placed here, so Peri and Belia push it off to the side. We find ice built up on the brickwork that was under the sarcophagus
	- We use a bonfire to melt the ice and try for a while to break the bricks (weapons to wedge out stone, eldritch blasts)
	- Orevana suddenly remembers she can go incorporeal. She shifts into the floor and finds a crypt with various shrines and a velvet pillow with human remains sitting atop. She blasts the bones to dust and returns to the party successful
- We take a long rest in Orevana’s Leomund’s Tiny Hut - we sleep a solid 12 hours
- It is now the **14th** - Ljudo Sr and the black feather lady (Marmaroth) will come on the 15th
- We go upstairs to check the windows for guards
	- It smells like breakfast
	- The books that had presumably been flung around the room have been tidied
	- Mara checks out the window to see about 20 humanoids and 4 giants outside in the courtyard. She also notices a statue of Ljudo Sr out front
	- There are another dozen or so humanoids out back plus a giant and potentially one other guard on the roof
- We encounter the butler again. He’s hauling a sack of piano parts. We chat with him and find out he’s meant to keep us here (along with the guards outside). He makes us breakfast and then we kill him so he gets another break (Hollia bonks him on the head with her mace)
- We do a séance so Orevana can get scrying. She scries Grizzut
	- He appears to be in the room we left him. He is writing notes on pieces of the bedsheets using a handmade pencil
	- Michael and Grizzut communicate via sending so we can let him know we are on our way
- We attempt to exit the Citadel without being noticed
	- We go up to the North tower to look out the windows. Peri finds a good spot for Michael to Dimension Door us behind the guards
	- We get through and head down a little ways to get out of eyeshot
- Getting to the Operations & Logistics Tower and Rescuing Grizzut
	- Mara summons a Homunculus to test the Dead Zone - it immediately dies
	- (Kayla did the math) It would only take about 30 minutes to tunnel through 0.5km. We double that for a more comfortable hole, and double again so Peri can take breaks. We take about 2 hours to get to the Operations and Logistics tower
	- We end up higher up than expected so we take more time for Peri to bring us down to the ground level
	- THE PLAN: Peri turns into a bug with the key to Grizzut’s room in his pocket (thanks to Mara!), then he turns back into himself to open the door, then turn himself and Grizzut into bugs and then they both exit
	- Ahead of the plan, Hollia puts a glyph on Peri and Michael casts non-detection
	- The plan went off without a hitch!! Also, Peri and Grizzut snuck into Yunaska’s office to get Grizzut’s stuff before they bugged out of the place
- Next time, we plan to assault one of the Magic Domes. We still need to figure out HOW
