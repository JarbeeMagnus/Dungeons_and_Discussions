## Summary
After sneaking the prisoners away from the Rakdos camp, we learn that there will be a caravan full of prisoners coming through this way and one of them is the Gruul leader's brother, who can summon wurms. We decide to rescue the prisoners. We know there is a caravan checkpoint nearby under a giant angel statue that toppled over, so most of the team head towards the checkpoint to kill any Rakdos cultists while Orevana and Peri make a large hole in the road to cause the caravan to get stuck. Once stuck, Michael snuck inside and then used Arcane Gate to get the prisoners out of the caravan. Mission success! We also found two anticepters for The Ru that provide magical coverage from the storms.

## Detailed Notes
- The party (+prisoners) sneak out of the greenhouse
	- We assume the prisoners are Gruul. There are 3 women and 2 men in the group. They arm themselves with makeshift weapons as we leave
	- The demons will go to the Southeast - the flaming skull told them to meet him at “the fallen angel”. The leader of the Gruul was a young woman named Shara
	- “*We hope you die in the purging flame of Bennok, the purging flame wurm*”
	- We walk for about 6 hours to the SE, the storm stays on our right
	- Sky Burn Clan has lived in the area for over a hundred years. We learn that Braz and Grea (the looter teens) are not from this clan. The Gruul were not responsible for the disaster 200 years ago
	- We are on the way to intercept a prisoner caravan
	- The storm in the center only came into existence 15 years ago
	- Mara (with the help of Hollia) convinces Shara to rest for the night so we can all be fresh for the fight tomorrow morning
	- Orevana summons Leomund’s Tiny Hut, but the Gruul reject her offer to sleep inside. Orevana, Mara, and Michael sleep in the Hut. Peri creates a lean-to using Druidcraft so he could sleep outside
	- We take turns taking watches throughout the night
- The morning greets us with hail and lightning
	- We are told that the “fallen angel” is a statue that toppled during the during the catastrophe 200 years ago
	- 4 hours later, we continue trudging through the heavy downfall when we see a large (400ft) toppled angel statue with a giant wing overhang
	- There is a clear pathway running west to east that we think the caravan will take us to the Fallen Angel
- Cyrna (Orevana’s raven) scouts the area and sees there are ~12 demons under the angel wing and the sled caravan is about 5 miles to the West
	- Our plan is to split the party: Orevana, Peri, and two Gruul go to dig trenches in the pathway of the incoming sled caravan while the rest of the party deals with the demons under The Fallen Angel
	- We plan to free the prisoners, specifically Shara’s brother Danton that can summon wurms
- At the Angel:
	- The party sneaks up towards the angel wing, using the rain as cover
	- They notice there is some kind of pole coming out of the angel’s “shoulder blades” with a copper chain dangling that has a glowing blue rod inside it (an anticepter) that appears to create a zone of safety from the storm - Mara wants this for The Rue
	- Hollia sends in an insect plague, causing the demons (that didn’t outright die) to go into hiding below the broken wing
	- Michael uses Bixby’s hand to chop the wing above the demons, which collapses on top of them
	- There was a Rok sitting on top of the statue and it starts flying away - towards where Peri and Orevana are digging. Mara blasts the Rok and then tries to intimidate it into coming back towards them by flying up into the air
	- The team easily disposes of the Rok before grabbing the anticepter and heading towards Orevana and Peri
- At the pathway:
	- Orevana polymorphs herself into a Giant Badger, then Peri enlarges her to dig large tunnels while he also molds earth to interrupt the path
	- We make a hole wide enough and deep enough that the sled gets stuck, plus Peri puts Plant Growth to slow down the sled
	- The 2 Gruul keep watch and will alert us when the sled gets close
	- Peri casts hallucinatory terrain to make it look like the road is fine
- The Fallen Angel team meets up with the digging team about 30 minutes before the sled gets close enough to engage. We hide along the pathway to wait for the trap to spring
- When the sled comes into range, we engage
	- Orevana makes herself and Mara invisible
	- Peri becomes a floating star dragon
	- Michael becomes invisible, then misty steps into the sled with the prisoners. He finds Danton, gives him a health potion, and creates a gate to get the prisoners out 400 ft away
	- We find another anticepter on the caravan
- We rescued everyone from inside the caravan - most of which were regular people and not Gruul
