## Summary
Orevana joins the party after Kullic's brother's spirit directs her to. The team, pretending to be construction site workers, have to deal with 2 separate spirit incidents. We learn that there's some sort of inspection the next day, so everyone will need to be on their best behavior.

----

## Detailed Notes
 
The party has found they have a natural talent for construction work in Ravnica after just about week of undercover construction work. On this particular day, the site supervisor (Zorica) had to leave the site and left Oleg in charge. He had his hands full, so he didn't notice that Gorman (AKA Crane-Nuts) had put a zombie in charge of the crane while everyone had lunch to artificially increase his productivity.  
  
While at lunch, the party was approached by various characters - a goblin woman threw sneeze on them and an elf delivered lunch to a guy behind them. The last person to approach was a half-orc named Orevanna, and she had news for Kullic: his brother, Marv, was dead. Marv's spirit told Orevana that the shackles that held him had been broken and that he had been in the undercity. Kullic went on a walk to clear his head and when he came back, a scream came from the other side of the building they had been working on.  
  
The party went to investigate (some faster than others) the scream and found Oleg crushed under a platform of rocks. Hollia tried to save him but the party was too late. Oleg's angry spirit emerged from the body, but Hollia was able to quickly dispatch both him and the zombie in the crane operating box that caused the accident. Gorman tried to convince the site that he had nothing to do with the zombie crane operator, but Orevana's zone of truth, Peri's vulture grapple keeping him in place, and Hollia's "tell the truth or you'll die" compulsion ability made him confess in front of Zorica when she returned. The body needed to be taken care of quickly and quietly, as Zorica disclosed that there was an inspection the next day by Dimwec Pracovisku (son of the construction company) and an Azorius permit inspector.  
  
Zorica "took care of" Gorman and left the disposal of the dismembered body to the party. They encased it in concrete in the washroom area.

Gorman's spirit snuck up on the party but they beat him swiftly with a series of magical blasts. Orevana got a contract setup with the construction company. With Hollia's help, it was signed without the spirit/body ownership clause.  
  
The party went to Keg Theory where Orevana disclosed that Marv's spirit had also said that she should stay with Kullic, as they could help eachother. Mara attempted to recap to Orevana what had happened so far (with great difficulty and interjection from Kullic). After getting thoroughly drunk, the party returned home to "The Garage" (as Mara lovingly named) to find Michael had set-up his fight club inside.
