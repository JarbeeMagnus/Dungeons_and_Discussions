## Summary
When we find the tower, we see it's been heavily damaged and ransacked. Grizzut explains that the looters (Rakdos cultists, we assume) took important equipment for the Izzet Guild Mage mission and tasks us to go get the missing parts. We follow tracks away from the tower and find a group of fiends with some Gruul prisoners. After some group invisibility shenanigans, we manage to kill off the fiends, rescue the 5 prisoners, and find 4 of the missing pieces of equipment.

## Detailed Notes
- We spend the night inside the ship at the Simic facility  
	- We let Tamelk know we’re leaving  
	- It’s very windy when we get out of the tunnel. It almost throws Belia and Michael off the deck  
	- Hollia tries to fly above the wind, but the height doesn’t seem to help. We drop back down after ARP alerts Hollia that the tower wasn’t that far away  
	- As we approach the tower, we see that it’s been heavily damaged  
	- We spend some time deliberating on how to approach the tower, so Grizzut and ARP jump off the ship together  
	- Michael and Mara feather fall down into the tower  
	- Orevana asked why we don’t land, so Hollia lands the ship  
- At the tower!  
	- Grizzut cries about the cultists taking the 4 “positronic field entanglers” and 6 “cascade emitters” and 1 “mana transplonder”  
	- Michael and Peri try to track where the cultists ran off to while the rest of the party goes inside to gather information from Grizzut about the things that were stolen
- Peri pinpoints the cultist tracks and discerns there were multiple smaller creatures, a few larger one (hooves & spider feet), as well as one large flying creature based on the wind marks of wings in the dirt  
	- The parts are needed to capture the mages for Nivmizit. Grizzut draws a picture of what is missing and Hollia takes notes with details  
	- Grizzut and ARP will build what they need in the ship as our base instead of the tower  
- We follow the tracks into the ruins nearby to try to find the Rakdos cultists  
	- After an hour, we find a bell in town that we will take back to the ship later as an alarm mechanism  
	- We see a giant flying creature in the shadow of the storm that reminds them of a dragon, but Peri and Orevana say that the only dragon in Ravnica is Nivmizit  
	- After another hour, we find bottles and other signs of debauchery. We find Jothor’s hand on the ground. Orevana picks it up with the intention of burying all of the pieces we find later  
	- We come to a ridge with broken greenhouses below. We catch the glimpse of small winged creatures flying between two of them. Hollia recognizes them as harpies  
	- Orevana sends Cyrna (her raven) out to get a layout of the area. We start heading down the ridge, sneakily  
- 10 minutes, raven back, but we hear a screech behind us (not a harpy) - it’s a giant flying spider  
	- We make everyone invisible, then we will meet back at the ridge after an hour  
	- We start in the greenhouse that the harpies left. We find it full of dead plants, which Peri takes a piece of. We find another piece of Jothor’s clothing  
	- (20min) We enter the other dome and the insect/flaming guy leave toward the south (storm is East, The Ru is west). We hear cackling and weird music up ahead  
	- Mara approaches the music and finds a demon peeing against a wall. Another one comes up behind the first and they both behave drunkenly  
	- Hollia finds 4 bison-sized demon creatures and10 demons
- There are 8 vrocks above (the “harpies” we saw earlier) and one giant playing a drum. We see a broken cascade emitter at his feet  
	- Hollia gets close to the giant and notices the rattle he was playing with is full of “junk” and she tries to jostle it out of his hands  
	- Belia finds a cage with 5 humans inside that are wearing clothes similar to what the looter teens were wearing near The Ru  
	- The demons said something about needing to get sleep before they go for a “caravan”. They also mention the name "Razgalgor" and that there are prisoners  
	- Mara finds the cage of people and a demon sits down nearby to take a snooze  
	- Hollia uses a wand to put lights in front of the Giant’s face to distract him. He puts the rattle down and swats at the lights. Hollia tries to pick up the rattle and leave with it  
	- Prisoners rescued, killed the demon standing guard, “Steal This”, Michael uses hand, Peri uses hypnotic terrain  
	- The backpack had 4 cascade emitters - we still need to find the rest of the missing equipment
