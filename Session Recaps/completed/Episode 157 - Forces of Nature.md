## Summary
Over the 3 easy days of travel, we bond with each other and the crew. On Flamerule 8/Seleszeni 10, we take our second Shore Leave to Silverymoon - this time with Mara! She has a bit of an emotional crisis when she realizes her 2 years of adventuring was actually 20 years in Faerun. We run a few errands, including purchasing some more magic supplies and getting ready to go out to The Dancing Goat that evening.

----

## Detailed Notes
- Hollia and Michael talk about his new airball spell and they plot potentially creating another new spell together with no purpose than to entertain them
- Orevana gets Marge to open up about what happened with the gas balls
- Marge opens up about some past trauma involving someone she loved choking her pretty bad
- Mara makes an apology tour
	- Orevana helps with releasing a spirit from a brooch she has
	- Mara and Peri talk about her getting therapy in Silverymoon
	- Mara finds Hollia “having some kind of seizure” in the corner of the cargo hold
	- Hollia thinks everyone in the crew should be given some “shore leave” - Mara suggests we take shifts
- At the end of 3 days, Orevana takes part of Hollia’s shift to fly the ship for a bit (she's getting okay with space!)
- Mara pays Jilly, Zellhane, Marge, and Belia backpay for their time + hazard pay (100gp each)
- Jilly requests strawberry pastries from our next shore leave (which we oblige)

**DATE: Redmesa 7, 1069 & Flamerule 8, 1509**

- We go back to Silverymoon
	- When we go to sign the datebook, Mara notices the date is 8th of Flamerule 1509 (she left Faerun in 1489) & had an emotional moment
	- Hollia goes off on her own to talk to the Glyph Scribe of the Vault (Tephania) and apologize for her previous behavior
	- The rest of the team go to The Shining Scroll to pay for the remaining supplies & buy scrolls. Michael takes a seat out front and tries to ritualistically cast a spell (which fails). Bessa has one bag of wind & the 9 bottled breath ready for us, which Mara pays the remaining balance on. We also buy additional scrolls and potions
	- Hollia waits outside for us to leave and then covertly approaches the store owner about “the stuff” which she discreetly purchases and rushes off to meet the rest of the team at the bridge - we later find out this was a diminution potion (see ep. 158)
- We go on a shopping spree to get new clothes and miscellaneous items before going to Hollia's mom and dad's house
	- Hollia gets flustered when she can’t remember Mara’s name when introducing her to her mother & runs up to her room
	- We have tea and snacks with Hollia’s parents
	- Peri & Hollia’s dad go into the back for him to pick out the stick for his staff (there were three options). Peri selects the walnut branch from the Star Court and asks him to carve it for him - something significant to Hollia/the dad
	- We decide we will go to The Dancing Goat tonight and Hollia’s parents will come to have a drink with us
