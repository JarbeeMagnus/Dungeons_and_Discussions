--The Night of the Fashion Show  (**Xivaskir 27, 9986**)
Dimwec picked them up outside the Inn around 4pm, like he said he would, with fruit snacks for the journey.   
  
Hollia found the naked Czarim statue (founder of Gruul) and got distracted and separated from the party.  
  
Orevana/Dimwec were early, so they went to a cafe down the road. Dimwec was not allowed to eat the food and instead had some kind of slop out of a thermos. At Orevana's prompting, Dimwec shot the hand crossbow that he had brought and narrowly missed a child. He rubbed his head in distress where the scar is, but Orevana was able to calm him down. The party shortly heads back to the venue where Viadka meets them and gives Dimwec a warm welcome. She finds out that Dimwec and Orevana are on a date. Viadka did not like Orevana's dress because it was poorly made, but she noticed the crossbow inspiration (Dimwec did not). Viadka believed Orevana's fake backstory before she's abruptly pulled away by the eldest Pracovisku sibling, Ludo Jr.  
  
Ludo Jr makes a fuss about not liking Viadka’s signage (black feathers as a V and not P for Pracovisku). Hollia and Kullic follow Ludo Jr and Viadka up the stairs to eavesdrop on the conversation. Ludo took some kind of eye drug, which Viadka disapproved of. Ludo continues to complain about going to “the weigh station” in the 6th district to “fix this” tomorrow morning. He will be talking to “Volenko” before he goes. Ludo also said “maybe I’ll take Dimwec and finish the job 30 years later” before grabbing Viadka inappropriately and kissing her neck (she does not stop him). Jr leaves and does not notice Kullic or Hollia making out in the hallway. Hollia does not like Kullic's feedback about her kissing skills.

Dimwec's thrall allows Orevana to sit with Dimwec during the fashion show. Mara takes someone else’s seat to stay closeby as her handmaiden. The team notices “Michael” sitting across from them. Michael told the party he was disguised (a white/silver/black, well-tailored suit and a fashionable hat) and that he's also upstairs using the bracelet.  
  
Real Michael casts dispel magic on fake Michael & it looks like a Morla outfit, but their face does not change). Mich/la managed to charm Mara with her gaze (on a second attempt). Michael uses a portal to engulf Mich/la (and 2 others) and dropped them outside. She turned into some kind of winged sexy lady. The other 2 fell to their death. Kullic managed to run out the front door to also see Morla fly away as a winged creature. Hollia, sensing something was off with Mara, removed her charmed condition.  
  
The fashion show ended to a lockdown of the entire building. Wojeks are on their way to investigate the murder of the two nobles outside.
