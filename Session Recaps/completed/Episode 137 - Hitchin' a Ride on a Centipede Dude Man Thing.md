## Summary
The next day, we successfully flag down the locopede and hitch a ride into Koren by disguising ourselves as Orzhov patrol. Once inside, we strip down and continue as if we are workers (Hollia forges fake paperwork for all of us, complete with a stamp from inside one of the nearby buildings). The area is set-up like a company town, so we just need to blend in. We learn about the rules for workers, like how there is curfew and set schedule that includes some kind of blessing ceremony in one of the cathedrals. Areas of interest we note include Pracovisku Citadel, Nadvlada Cathedral, and the Leisure Zone. We manage to walk around the Pracovisku Worker Housing area and both Orevana and Michael notice there are MANY spirits around. After re-casting Polymorph on Donwec (Slugwec), we head into the Leisure Zone.

## Detailed Notes
- The next day, we try to flag down the locopede as members of the Orzhov patrol 
	- Mara, Michael, Orevana, Hollia, and Peri (disguised as a half-elf) disguise themselves with fancy metal armor  
	- Grizzut makes himself, Belia, and Donwec invisible  
	- We flag down the locopede and Hollia convinces them she’s tallying slaves and workers  
	- They let us onboard about halfway down the locopede. Michael asks for food and they said they don’t have anything for us  
	- Michael tells horrible Izzet jokes to the guards  
	- After 55 mins or so, Grizzut gets Orevana’s attention to say he can only cast invisibility one more time  
	- Orevana puts on a show of doing a fortune telling session with the help of Hollia. They use their womanly charm to keep the guards’ attention while Grizzut recasts the spell  
	- After another 45 minutes, we start to slow down and feel movements of the locopede indicating stops at all three doors, just as Peri described  
	- Outside the locopede, it suddenly gets bright
- We step off the locopede  
	- The strip-mine seems like it’s like a company town - hundreds of workers surround us  
	- We bid the guards farewell and walk into the crowd. That’s when we notice the workers are handing in paperwork  
	- Hollia convinces one of the workers to hand over their paperwork so she could create fakes for everyone  
	- The paperwork mentions Project Firmament and that workers are on a 3 month contract (no less)  
- We split the party  
	- Grizzut sends a message to Mara (Sending) and says the invisible folks went into a nearby building  
	- Michael walks toward the building Mara pointed out but was unsuccessful in convincing the guard to let him inside  
	- Orevana sends Message to Donwec and gets information on who’s inside (it’s workers)  
	- Michael, Hollia, Mara, and Orevana get in easily with the forged paperwork  
	- Orevana steals one of the stamps so they can stamp the remaining party’s paperwork after they get inside  
	- We are directed into the building where Belia, Grizzut, and Donwec are waiting. Orevana stamps their forged paperwork and hands them out. She chastises Donwec for wandering off  
	- We are told there is a curfew and that there are 4 people per room. We find two rooms next to each other on the first floor  
	- There are younger, low level Orzhov staff stationed around  
	- Michael asks about getting air and finds out there is not a roof for us to access. Also, all entrances are guarded  
	- Belia goes outside to run around and Michael joins her
- Information Orevana gathered from the mousey-looking girl named Dusana:   
	- Top of the mine is off limits  
	- There is a recreation zone  
	- When leaving, they go to the Way Station to the North of the site to “go home”  
	- Pracovisku Citadel is on the other side of the Guildless area (we are currently in the construction worker zone)  
	- Every 10 days the workers get to go to Nadvlada Cathedral to participate in a blessing ceremony, overseen by the “Ministrant of Obligation”  
	- There is a curfew  
	- Work schedule is 4 10-hour days of work, then 2 days’ break, then 4 additional days  
	- There is also another Cathedral (Vernost) along the way to the leisure zone for workers to use  
	- After Orevana talks to Dusana, Hollia (who had been nearby taking notes) gave her one of her romance novels  
- Michael and Belia (outside) try to figure out how to get onto the roof  
	- They make it look like they are goofing off to the guards that are on the roof watching over them  
	- Belia does not make it on the first attempt. And again on the second attempt  
	- On the third try, Belia makes the jump. The only thing she sees is the lightning towers around the perimeter  
	- A guard yells and says she needs to jump down right away, which she does  
	- A group of workers give Belia’s low-five’s after she lands  
- Hollia manages to approach a hunky elf (after a few failed attempts). She makes small talk but he comes on strong and scares Hollia away. One of his buddies (Pavel) comes over and apologizes (love interest for later…?)
- We talk about plans  
	- We definitely want to investigate the Pracovisku Citadel tonight  
	- Potential for Donwec to overthrow Ljudo Jr  
- Michael approaches the guard at the door and tries to convince him to let us go for a walk. He says we would probably be fine if we’re back within an hour - but to be careful and we didn’t see him  
- On the walk:  
	- There are buildings down to the right where the locopede stables are  
	- Lots of people walking around, also smaller beasts of burden amongst them  
	- There is also potentially a path off to the side (2 miles) because there’s a gap in the ridge above us  
	- We walk towards the gap in the ridge in the hopes of locating the Pracovisku Citadel  
	- There is a waste treatment processing station nearby for the housing buildings  
	- There’s also a warehouse in the South with a wall nearby (gated) and that’s where the ridge splits. The gate has lettering that says “Obzedat Gate” *(note: the Obzedat are the ghost council that runs the Orzhov)*
	- The wall/fence has a door on each side (like a castle border). Peri deduces that the gate is where we came in on the locopede - but this is NOT where Peri flew in as an eagle (there were twists and turns while on the locopede)  
	- We are approached by a guard and told to move along  
- Orevana feels a spiritual presence in the area similar to that of the 10th district  - LOTS of spirits
- We continue on our walk, looking for landmarks  
	- We see a cathedral nearby (Vernost)
	- Michael’s third eye opens and he sees dozens of ethereal spirits with golden chains heading in and out of the cathedral  
	- The bells in the cathedral go off indicating it’s 3pm (note, this is Vernost Cathedral). A crowd leaves and we blend in with the crowd  
	- At the bottom of the basin, it looks almost like Disneyworld - this is probably the Leisure Zone  
	- Peri re-applies polymorph on "slugwec" (using his mouth) and we enter the Leisure Zone
