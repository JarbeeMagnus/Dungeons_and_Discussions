## Summary
Michael, having died in the last fight, has a vision that we all witness. He's surrounded by brought light and hears a strange man tell him that everything he needs for success is in his heart. The man, we later learn, is Boccob. He had given Michael some book called "Awaken the Giant Within" that Michael left in the University library. Meanwhile, Grizzut is communing with the Fire Mind and is passed out in the kitchen. While we wait for Grizzut to wake up, Hollia performs surgery on Orevana's tentacle arm and removes it. Mara also writes a manifesto for us to distribute to our Ravnica contacts. Grizzut wakes and we head to Nivix to turn in the guild mages and discuss what's been going on with Nivmizzit. We need to repair the ship (after that black tower shot us), check in with folks who may want to leave with us, and spread information about what Pracovisku has been doing. We try to get an audience with Nivmizzit but are not successful. We do receive a reward for bringing the dead guild mages back. The Izzet League also repairs and upgrades The Ru, so we head to the University to pick up Beffalor, Delandri, and Ellagorin (who has been fired). When we don't find them, we head to Keg Theory because we heard Ellagorin has been working with someone named "Yuri".

## Detailed Notes
- We start the session with Michael having the following experience: 
	- He sees shapes in the distance as he’s surrounded by bright light 
	- He hears a strange man that talks about the other spirits around and being in a cage of some sort. The man is wearing purple robes with symbols all over it 
	- Michael asks if the man is Rakdos, which he says no. He’s “the guy from the library” who gave Michael some book. He tells Michael that he’s going to share a memory with us 
	- “Everything you need for success is in your heart, Michael. Not a flick of the blade sooner than necessary” - the strange man says to Michael as he leaves 
- Meanwhile, on the deck of The Ru: 
	- Michael wakes up naked (save one shirt covering his genitals), covered in words in 8 different languages 
	- Hollia is snuggling up to Michael and covered in frost. Her breath can be seen. Mara and Peri take Hollia to the nearby bonfire that Orevana made in preparation for the resurrection 
	- We all see the conversation between Michael and the strange man in the purple robes 
	- Michael suddenly remembers the word “Boccob” and called him the god of magic 
	- “Awaken the giant within” is the name of the book 
- Grizzut is found in the kitchen, asleep and communing with the Fire Mind. As we wait for him to wake up: 
	- Hollia removes Orevana’s tentacle arm (after Mara’s suggestion that she won’t mind & pain numbing paste from Marge)
	- Belia and Peri attach a tarp (+strawberry vines) to keep water from getting into the cargo hold
	- Michael takes a nap 
	- Mara writes a manifesto and has Hollia proof-read it 
- We have contacts in the 10th that we want to: 
	- Ask some folks if they want to leave planet with us (the professor named Ellegorin Omavius, Kullic, Befallor & Delandri) 
	- Talk to Nivmizzit & the Selesnya Conclave & the Azorius senator about what we’ve found about Pracovisku
	- Repair the ship 
	- Mara also wants to potentially “paper the planet” with one-page double-sided fliers. She starts with 10 copies with Hollia and then she’ll make more copies at the Izzet 
- We take off when night falls. It’s dark and rainy for the first hour or so, then the weather clears up. The trip back was uneventful, but Hollia overexerts herself a little and swaps places with Belia 
	- When we get to the 10th, Mara decides we should go to Nivix and ARP points out a good place in a courtyard for Belia to land the ship 
	- We leave Hollia sleeping on the ship with a note on her pillowcase 
	- Michael checks his badge and it’s now LEVEL 5 (wtf?!) 
- We’re escorted to a lab (ARP’s?) and meet a racist human. Things escalate a bit and he calls security. The security guard says we are to stay in this lab. Orevana fails at charming the guy with Friends 
	- Shortly after, the guard who spoke with us earlier asks for the wizards. He leaves with the bodies and takes ARP to speak to Nivmizzit 
	- After about 45 minutes, the guard comes back with a chest on a cart 
	- We are not granted an audience with Nivmizzit, but are given a reward of Myzzium armor (half-plate & paldrons) & a pyro converger (flamethrower) & a bag of jewels (worth 6k gold) 
	- Izzet can repair our ship in the next few hours 
	- Mara inquires about Kullic but the guard doesn’t know anything. He’ll get back to us with info later 
	- Orevana wakes Hollia while Mara checks in with Zellhane to let her know about the Izzet repairing the ship 
- We head to the university 
	- On the way, we see the giant Pracovisku tower being erected has made substantial progress (Project Phoenix)
	- We take a subway since moving on foot would take a long time 
	- We want to gather Hollia’s friends, Magnum Opus, and Michael’s book. The professor’s nameplate was removed from his office door. The University cut ties while he was on sabbatical. Last they heard, he was working with a human and elf wizard and a guy named Yuri 
	- Michael finds the missing book inside one of the bookcases
