We met Morla at an abandoned stadium - along with 200+ other workers! The "main fight" would be between Michael and Do-Drak. Michael easily won using only his underwear as a weapon. ## Detailed Notes
The team went to an old stadium a few miles North of The Garage to find around 200 Pracuvisku workers in an abandoned stadium with various fighting rings inside. Morla explained that the big fight was going to be between Michael and Do-Drak (a large, 6-limbed crab man).  
  
While they waited for the big fight, the team split up and had smaller bouts. Michael found Do-Drak reading a book on the sidelines. Kullic and Peri 2v2'd another team and won, Orevana beat an old man using magic, and then Peri and Kullic went head to head (I CAN'T REMEMBER WHO WON).  
  
Morla had her speech, copied above, before the fight between Michael and Do-Drak began. Michael ended up doing a LOT of damage to Do-Drak using his own underwear. Do-Drak dramatically surrendered to Michael before leaving the arena.  
  
Mara entered the ring and asked to fight Morla, who had agreed to the match earlier. Morla was nowhere to be found. Instead, a little girl entered the coliseum looking for scraps. Hollia helped the girl, saying she would help her get home. On their way out, the little girl threw a green cube into the center of the arena where it exploded into a giant monster.  
  
Undead archers fired arrows into the crowds of people as chaos erupted. Kullic used his sword to jump around and kill some of the smaller enemies. Peri summoned pixies to polymorph the whole party (except Hollia and Michael) into t-rexes. They killed all of the undead dogs and archers before finally defeating the giant green monster.  
  
After the fighting, there were 52 total dead.
