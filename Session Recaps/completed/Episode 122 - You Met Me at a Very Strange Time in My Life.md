## Summary
The team decides to go to Rix Maadi and get information from Morla about why she's following us. We learn she wants to shut away Rakdos in Riverstix and wants to obtain (and probably kill) a baby whose parent used to be in the party. We go home to deliberate and decide to kill her but she doesn't show up. Orevana does a few scryings on Dimwec/Donwec and mostly learns he spends a lot of time at home, alone in his room and that his father appears to have anger issues (lots of yelling in the background).

----

## Detailed Notes
The team gets ready to go to Rix Maadi to meet with Morla - Orevana, Hollia, and Michael all dress up ahead of time. The circus was looking better than the last time the party was there, but not still 100% after the riots. Kullic easily finds Morla up in the rafters using his Finder’s Goggles. Peri shouts up to her “*Hey, hot stuff - we’re back for round two!*” A chain came down and all but Hollia climbed up.  
  
After the party climbed up, Hollia warded the chain against anyone besides the party going up or down. A young man, about 12, tries to get her attention by dancing in front of her. Hollia decides to dance with him, and he invited her to Hellbender later to which she replied “*maybe*”.  
  
At the top if the chain, there is a 20x20 foot platform with makeshift bedding that included a sack that had been stuffed and shaped like a person sleeping. A red, crescent moon shaped pendant hung above them. The party put on a show of trying to find Morla, who was clearly above them. Morla giggled in delight and only came down to parlay after the party asked her nicely. Her voice and accent were different than before.

The conversation between Morla and the party included many lies and half-truths. She claimed to be a Cambian (half-demon) with Rakdos being her demon father. Orevana and Kullic both think it's true. She said she is not working with Rakdos and is instead trying to lock him up. He uses a lava pool to travel to “Riverstix” - during this time the demodens that the party encountered before (Ig'drok, Soth'tanaz, Gozradal) guard the pool. Morla wants the party to kill Rakdos’ wardens. She believes this will lock Rakdos in Riverstix and will end the Guild Pact (she later says maybe it won’t). In return for doing this, Morla wants to keep (and probably kill) a baby whose parent used to be in the party and is going to be very powerful.  
  
Morla disclosed that she had been watching the party since they were in Rix Maadi previously, and has even been listening to them in the vents below the garage they’ve been staying in. Other things she claimed were: being pregnant with Michael’s child (lie), the red pendant was what allowed her to not be scryed upon (later discovered this was a lie), that Morla and Michael are bound to each other and that he signed a contract in his sleep (unsure), and that she only followed them to the Fashion Show because it was fun. Morla also gave the party cherry pastries. Michael tossed most of them over the edge, but Kullic took one before he did that.

The party tells Morla that they need time to discuss her proposition. On the way back, Orevana slipped and fell. Mara was almost able to save her, but wasn’t able to manage. Orevana landed on the boy Hollia was dancing with and killed him. Hollia squealed before checking Orevana for wounds and healing her. Three imps try to run off with the body, but Orevana chases them and hypnotizes them. She stuffs the boy’s body into her bag of holding.  
  
The party leaves Rix Maadi to discuss Morla’s proposition. The party decides not to take her deal. They invite Morla back to the Garage to “have more macaroni” where they will kill her. While they waited, Orevana and the party do a seance so that she can scry on Dimwec. The spirit was a priest and he happily gave Orevana the necessary knowledge to cast the spell. She successfully scries on Dimwec, who is taking a bubble bath. He hums an Orzov hymn for children. The windows are latticed windows, so she is not able to see his location.  
  
Michael waited all night, but Morla never shows up. The next morning, they spell out “No Deal” in macaroni before they leave to find Art Vandalay (one of Kullic’s contacts) as a way of getting to the 6th District. Upon their arrival, they find out the shop is closed and up for auction for Orzhov.  
  
Defeated and unsure what to do next, they do another seance to allow Orevana to scry on Dimwec again. This time, he’s in his bedroom, counting zinos at his desk. A man is yelling nearby and Dimwec rubs his scar in alarm. The yelling becomes a painful voice. The windows outside show a statue of a man with a hedge maze for 300 feet out. They appear to be on the 4th floor. There is an ornate fence at the edge of the property. Other, smaller mansions are nearby. In the distance, she sees Nivix (Izzet). It looks like the building is South of Midori Park somewhere.
