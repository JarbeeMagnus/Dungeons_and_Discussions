## Summary
The birth of "fight club". After waking late for work, we find out that Zorica is suspected of murder (true) and the team continues the construction site ruse. Orevana gives Dimwec a tour of the site and agrees to go on a date with him (he'll have more information because he's a Pracovisku).

## Detailed Notes
We arrive back at the Garage and see Michael being cheered for in the middle of the apartment.  He exclaims he can take on 3 at once.  
  
To his surprise, Michael takes on loads of damage from Piss the Goblin, some Dwarf and some other guy.  The Dwarf exlaimed "this is for Goran" as he swung for Michael.  Peri thought that was strange, especially since Michael wasn't there to even know what happened to Goran. He wasn't able to gain any ground and gets KO'd.  Morla, this weird woman, says something weird to Michael and ashes on his head.  

The group, still a little drunk from Keg Theory has a night cap while Michael tries to figure out what's wrong with him (so deep).  
  
We wake up and realize we're late for work.  The crew rushes off and sees that the dwarf from Fight Club is in the "foreman" spot?  He is directing traffic, Zorica is nowhere to be found.  Peri and Michael go to find her, after Michael finds something of hers to scry (A love note from O to Z).  As they take off, a couple of Wojecks cross paths with them.  One is Weslyn, and she gives Peri a heads up that she's going to a construction site (ours?!) so after exchanging pleasantries, M & P say that finding Zorica is not needed and head back.  
  
Orevana has taken the spot of foreman from Dwarfnutz, with help from Hollia and almost gets arrested by the Boros wojecks, since they came looking for Zorica and Orevana claimed to be her.  It was discovered that the mean Dwarf put in a semi-anonymous tip to the Boros about the murder on the construction site (construction workers considered to be Orzhav, so now murder is suuuper illegal).  The Wojecks still are looking for Zorica.

We hear crowd clamoring nearby and we see an Orzhov beast construct and on top of it, a man, a thrull and another person.  The thrull is tossing out the equivilent of copper pieces and makes a grand announcement "ARRHHUUU RAAAAA RUUU DIMMMWEIC ARRRRUUU."  The two humanoids introduce themselves and their intent to inspect the site.  Orevanna escorts them around the site. Dimmy hits on Orevanna like a preschooler and talks about crossbows and pokemon cards.  He handwaves looking at the site and gets a date with our new bard.    
  
Meanwhile Peri and Kullic "interrogate" the dork dwarf.  He admits to calling in the tip, says Goron was super cool and was his best friend.  Peri says maybe get a new best friend, choose better friends.  He's new to interrogation so he tried good cop dumb cop to get more information out of him.    
  
They break for Keg theory, and this is where it's hazy.  Then we went home.  Michael wants to do some more fight clubby stuff.
