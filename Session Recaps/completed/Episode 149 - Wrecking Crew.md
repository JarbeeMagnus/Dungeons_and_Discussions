## Summary
After the fight, we take a look around. Orevana discovers the priest we fought was actually a vampire - and he was still alive! She and Hollia quickly kill him while Mara and Peri continue looking down the hallway into other rooms. We don't find a manual for operating the contraption that is holding the djinn, but we do manage to open a magically locked door that leads to the teleportation circle. Unfortunately, it's been rendered unusable from a large crack running through it. Michael and Orevana manage to start freeing the djinn by fiddling with the control panel. We free the djinn and do NOT let Grizzut take it captive. As it flies into the air, a giant black tower appears and starts firing lasers at the djinn. We take to the air (in various ways) and enter a fight with a bunch of Orzhov soldiers and mages. Peri uses reverse gravity to keep us suspended during the fight and Hollia teleported herself back to The Ru by using Word of Recall.

## Detailed Notes
- Grizzut wanders around taking stock of the room with its gadgets and gizmos
- We learn that the stained glass window only works on live creatures
- Orevana casts Gentle Repose on the wizard bodies
- Mara shuffles the wizard bodies into her bag of holding (with the help of Michael’s telekinesis)
- We take a short rest
- Mara goes to a door in the corner that does not open. Neither she nor Peri can open it
	- There is no keyhole or lock that we can see
	- Peri casts detect magic on the door. He detects abjuration magic
	- Hollia, who has been studying the room, determines the door has an arcane lock and probably has an alarm
	- Michael dispels magic on the door (which Peri can confirm). The door immediately opens
- The door opens up to a small hallway with 6 doors (small rooms) and a teleportation circle at the end of the hallway. Mara and Peri go inside to loot the rooms. They mostly find books
- Orevana takes the priest’s bag of Zinos and holy symbol. She notices that the body is strangely cold and clammy. He also has very sharp teeth. Orevana calls Hollia over to take a look and Hollia confirms it’s indeed a vampire (and it’s still alive!)
- We make quick work of the vampire, covering Hollia in blood. Peri and Mara soon emerge asking Hollia for help with the books (Peri with an armful of books). Michael cleans Hollia off and she takes the books into the corner to pretend she’s in a library. When she doesn’t find a manual in the stack, she goes back into the rooms to find one. She does not, but instead finds a trashy romance novel
- Mara and Peri go back into the hallway to check out the teleportation circle - it’s got a big gash in it and seems to be inoperable
- Meanwhile, Michael joins Grizzut at the control panel and successfully releases the remaining flux charger and gelectrode. The storm inside the glass orb in the center of the room fades and shows a long-nailed hand and the face of a djinn. He doesn’t seem to be able to hear us. He starts trying to break the glass
- Grizzut wants to capture the djinn, but we don’t want to give him up to the Izzit League
- Michael messages the djinn and it stops, indicating it heard him, but Michael only gets screams in response
- Orevana finds levers that appear to operate the machinery and starts pulling them at random. One of the things holding the orb up bursts
- Mara and Grizzut start frantically placing the Counterveil Anchors around the djinn
- Mara blasts the orb with eldritch blast, which inspires Orevana to do the same. She hits where the glass is breaking and seems to help open it up more. Water starts seeping out and Peri manipulates it to help break the orb. Another round of eldritch blasts bursts open the orb. The djinn pulls himself out of the orb and flies around the room for a while
- The djinn flies out the roof and a horn blares. A flying tower (?!) appears and starts firing red lasers at the djinn
- We fly out the top of the building (Peri beast shape holding Mara and Belia, Orevana polymorph, Hollia summoned celestial, Michael wings carrying Grizzut then Bigby’s hand when the wings run out)
- We see black fungus growing in the dead zone as we fly out and land in the Pracovisku Worker Housing area. There is a crowd of Orzhov soldiers below, some of which are shouting and pointing at us. A group of soldiers (and two wizards) ride up to us on gloamwings
- A meteor shower almost murders the entire party and an EPIC fight ensues. Where we end off:
	- Hollia is on The Rue after she became conscious
	- Orevana, as a bat, is holding Grizzut
	- Peri used reverse gravity and he, Mara, and Belia are floating at the top of it
	- Michael is a dragon
