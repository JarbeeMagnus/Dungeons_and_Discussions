## Summary
We successfully enter the Operations & Logistics Tower under the ruse of being plumbers/investigating a "strange white substance" and claim to need access into the heavily-guarded Requiem Gate. We discover that the Commander of this project is none other than Donwec's cousin, Yunaska. She allows us into the "Project Requiem Site" and we find dozens of people digging what appears to be a large hole. Mara suspects it is related something she's seen before called a "soul dump hole". On our way back to the Logistics Tower, we learn that Grizzut has been caught sneaking in one of the MC towers. We take a long time wrapping up and are allowed to sleep in the tower.

---

## Detailed Notes
- The Requiem Gate is the most fortified gate we’ve encountered and doesn’t seem to have any statues and adornments that we can see from a kilometer away
- It’s currently early afternoon (Zuun 11)
- We plan to smooth talk our way into the Logistics Building to investigate a white substance we’ve been seeing in other waste treatment sites
	- Orevana and Hollia will take the lead
	- There looks to be a small stable on the roof of the Logistics Building
	- Suddenly, a shape rises up above the ridge nearby - the Pracovisku airship is leaving
	- We easily make it past the guards at the door
- Inside the Logistics Building
	- There is a central “concierge” desk in the middle of the room
	- Other notable features of the room: a large staircase, engineers and managers working on paperwork/blueprints at multiple desks around the lobby
	- An indentured spirit came out of a portal near the staircase and asked for “geological and chemical reports for sites r3-r6” and that (where the spirit came from) was the records room - we want to investigate this later
	- Peri overhears a conversation about the Pracovisku ship taking off but the receptionist didn’t have any other information on it (note, they’re a lower level employee)
- A ghost spirit is summoned to bring forth the Facilities Manager (Hask) to talk with us
	- He is a minotaur and initially claims there are no problems. Michael unleashes a horrid stench which causes Hask to take us down the stairs to the base of the tower
	- Michael continuously casts prestidigitation to continue making the mayo dung smell which causes Hask to leave the room while we investigate
	- We spend 30 minutes to leave traces of mayonnaise around a bunch of pipes to claim it’s some kind of contact disease originating from the Requiem Site
	- After we leave, we find Hask sitting in the stairwell looking nauseous
	- Mid conversation, Hollia brings attention to the mayo on her hand and then she casts contagion to sell that it’s a disease (her flesh starts rotting off her bones). She claims she was exposed yesterday and that the disease takes 24 hours to manifest
	- The guards start to quarantine the stairwell and fetch a priest from upstairs. He believes us and says that “Commander Pracovisku” needs to know
- We convince him to take us upstairs
	- There is a landing between the 5th & 6th floor and encounter a skeleton holding a book and guarding the upstairs
	- A guild mage meets us on the landing and says he’s been instructed to clear the area and a special team will be arriving in about an hour to investigate further
	- We convince the Guild Mage that we should be the ones to investigate right away because Hollia can cure everyone if we find the source.
	- *RETCON FROM 141* - when we go upstairs, the Guild Mage gave us Zinos to leave in an offering bowl for each of us
	- We go upstairs and find floors 7 and 8 hold the records. 9th floor has a door (mystery) with guards. 10th floor has a bunch of desks and magical displays all over the room
	- The commander is Yunaska Pracovisku and Donwec mutters her name questioningly under his breath. She noticed he said something, but not the details
	- “My uncle is coming here in 4 days’ time” - we suspect this might be why the ship was leaving
	- Yunaska gives us clearance to go through the Requiem Gate
- After we exit
	- Grizzut sends Michael a message saying he’s been caught and that he’s in MC3
	- Donwec confirms that Yunaska was also horrible to him as a kid. We tell him he needs to be cool in the future because he almost blew our cover. He now wants to kill everyone. We will be able to kill them all in 4 days when his dad comes in
	- We get through the gate and find a giant hole in the ground (estimate 2km deep). Mara stops in her tracks, as she has seen something similar on another, distant planet
	- Michael hear an old man laugh in his head - he thinks it’s “Orkus”
	- We are escorted by a guard who drives us into the hole
	- About halfway down, we see more focused digging areas at the bottom
	- Orevana casts see invisibility and Michael opens his third eye. Orevana senses a large amount of spiritual energy and Michael sees many spirits around, some engaging in the work they had done while alive
	- At the bottom, we see they are dumping dirt and rocks into a channel below the floor of the pit
	- Mara suspects this will be some kind of “soul dump hole” like she saw before, but this one isn’t activated yet
	- At the bottom, Hollia puts on a show of destroying “the source” of the contagion
- We return to the tower
	- Hask has donned a gas mask. When we arrive, he sends a spirit upstairs to notify Commander Pracovisku
	- We go downstairs to clean up the mess and take a LONG time to finish. We secure a room on the 4th floor for the night
	- Notably, there are no windows or balconies that we can see
	- We take a long rest and WAKE UP LEVEL 13!!!!!
