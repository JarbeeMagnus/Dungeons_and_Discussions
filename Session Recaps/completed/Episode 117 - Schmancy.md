## Summary
Morla meets the gang and we tell her who we are and what we're doing (saving the universe). Peri goes on a date with Weslyn and it goes well. Orevana goes on her date with Dimwec/Donwec at a fancy restaurant while the rest of the crew were either her "servants" or snuck in to watch. Viadka interrupts the date to make sure Donwec is going to her fashion show the following week. He decides to invite Orevana to go with him.

## Detailed Notes
We join our heros in Keg Theory with the rough carpenter Morla. Orevana and Kullic sit with Morla as she waits for Michael to join them from the bar - ashing into Kullic's drink before telling him he should get rid of the guards at his church. Mara and Michael learn from Yuri that Morla leads a new gang called the Slag Sisters. When Michael joins the table, he and Moral talk about Fight Club for a bit. She said that the next fight night will be in 3 days. Kullic finally got to understand Mara's struggle as he tried explaining planes and planar travel to Morla. Michael proceeds to tell Morla everything about him and the gang. She sat stunned until he finished. She gets up to as if to leave but instead full kisses him on the mouth while handing him soap. Orevana tells the group that Morla is some kind of fiend.  
  
Peri, who had been suspiciously missing this whole time, was found in the bathroom fixing up his hair and washing up in preparation for his date with Weslyn. He uses the soap that Michael had and took a shitload of damage as his skin turned black and a swarm of insects came out of the blackness and bit him. As he cleaned up (again), Orevana pulled out the documents that Mara found in Zorica's office. It was general construction plans for Project Phoenix - the series of re-builds after the Rakdos riots. It also included information about a new (very tall) basilica called Strazit Sanctuary and information on a project in the 6th District called Project Fermament (1500).  
  
Michael proceeded to turn himself into a pig before realizing he couldn't change back to humanoid until he rests. Both Peri and Orevana had dates coming up so they and Mara and piggy-Michael went shopping for new digs.

Peri got a nice new shirt, Orevana a sexy little black dress and crossbow pin, and Mara got a piggy sweater for Michael and a fancy wee bag to carry him in. Hollia and Kullic stayed at Keg Theory and bonded at the bar.  
  
Weslyn, Kullic, and Hollia exchanged pleasantries as they waited for Peri to come back. Peri tried to run off, he was so nervous. The date went very well, especially considering that Weslyn didn't know it was a date at first. Peri kissed her cheek as she left, then proceeded to joyfully lift-hug the whole team (minus Kullic).  
  
The next day, the gang went to work at the construction site. A new foreman had been appointed by their company named Sava. A hawk appeared for Orevana with an invitation to Indigo along with a note that said she could bring servants. She chose Kullic and Hollia to come along on the date, so the three of them got dressed up and took the carriage to Indigo. Mara, Michael, and Peri also decided to go to Indigo, so Mara and Michael (now *Michelle*) disguised themselves before hopping into the basked that Peri carried in Roc form as he flew them all to the restaurant. Orevana checked in with the other team via her raven familiar.  
  
When they arrived at Indigo, Orevana, Kullic, and Hollia were brought to Dimwec's table before being separated - Orevana at the table with Dimwec and his thrull, Kullic and Hollia to the guard alcove hidden behind a fake wall. Michael drew fight club symbols at the maître d' but she was not responsive. Mara and Peri were not able to find Orevana's table, so the three of them decided to just enjoy the food. Kullic and Hollia chatted with Dimwec's guards ("I AM a lady, THANK YOU for NOTICING" -Hollia) and discovered that Dimwec's brother shot him as a child with a crossbow, which may contribute to his obsession with them.

Orevana was enthusiastic about crossbows the whole evening and Dimwec seemed pleased with the crossbow pin she brought for him. Opatrovatel’ka (his thrull) was between the two the whole evening and wouldn't even let her help him with the pin. Mara came by with ice cream after Peri pointed out the table but Opatrovatel’ka would not let Dimwec eat it because Mara didn't look like a waiter. Orevana convinced Dimwec to send food to the guards in the alcove.  
  
Viadka Pracovisku, Dimwec's sister, showed up and asked if he was coming to her fashion show next week, completely ignoring Orevana. Opatrovatel’ka gave a neutral-positive review of the date to Viadka before she left the restaurant. Dimwec invited Orevana to the fashion show with the intention of leaving as early as possible so they could do crossbow-related activities. She eagerly agreed and they walked out together, arm-in-arm. Orevana managed to kiss Dimwec on the cheek before he left. Mara, Michael (no longer Michelle), and Peri met Orevana, Kullic, and Hollia in front of the restaurant. While discussing how they were getting home, Hollia used Word of Recall to teleport the whole squad home.  
  
TLDR: Michael, Peri, AND Orevana GOT LUCKYYYYY
