## Summary
We navigate the Sargasso clouds trying to get out. Our air pocket will last about 120 days as long as it's not fouled. Peri is a rock during this time - he gives Hollia "herbs" in secret to help her divination, he creates a plant sanctuary in the back of the ship for Orevana, and he helps direct us using his navigation skills and Zellhane's map. Orevana, meanwhile, is spending much of her time in the cargo hold, as it's too overwhelming to be on deck. On one of the days, while Michael is flying, we hit some kind of no-magic dead zone, so the ship is stuck. We end up getting ambushed by a group of scavvers and a giant asteroid named "AHHHH". We were only able to convince AHHHH to pull us out of the anti-magic zone because of the comprehend languages Hollia put on the ship previously.
