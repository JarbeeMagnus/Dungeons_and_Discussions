## Summary
After the murder of 2 guilded people, Boros swarm the museum. When going through the "Verity Circle" (Zone of Truth), the team had a few hiccups. Hollia was pulled aside for mentioning her dress was from Faerun, Peri for un-polymorphing from a bee to himself in front of the guards, and Mara for talking about Neogi and Nulviir. Orevana leaves with Dimwec/Donwec holding piggy Michael. Kullic also gets out but slips that "the pig isn't a pig", resulting in a chase scene (Michael is able to get away). Hollia convinces the guards to let her talk to Mara and Peri in the wagon outside and, once close enough, teleports the three of them to the University Library using Word of Recall. We all make it out unscathed!

## Detailed Notes
The entire museum goes on lockdown after Boros discovers the bodies outside. Michael tells the group that he thinks Morla is a succubus before misty stepping into her seat. Red mist covered all exits and side wings so that all guests (except Kullic) were trapped inside. Viadka had a complete breakdown near backstage, insisting that her show “is RUINED”. Orevana and Dimwec successfully console/comfort her after her guards let them through. Mara and Opatrovatel’ka (who we learned carries a dagger) establish that neither like each other. Michael also came with and made the models swoon after unbuttoning his shirt.  
  
Hollia was seated near the stage and overheard that the people that died were a half-elf woman from the Selesnya Conclave and a dwarven man from the Simic Combine.

Kullic had run outside before the red mist trapped everyone. He checked on the two bodies and confirmed they were VERY dead. Boros arrive on the scene quickly and check the bodies. Wojak Selena recognized (and swooned over) Kullic while he recounted what had happened, saying there was a third person that came out of the portal that had turned into a demon and flew away. The Wojaks drilled Kullic about his decision to run outside, perhaps hoping to associate him with the crime, but he stuck to his story (three people portal-ed, one flew off). During this questioning, Roks started to arrive carrying more Wojaks and winged Boros angels came shortly after. A blue spirit emerged from one of the bodies that went off with some of the Boros. A blood-red spirit emerged from the other body, which Kullic was able to dispatch using the Boros ghostbuster device. The Boros enter the building by splitting the red mist in the main entrance with Kullic in tow.  
  
Peri, who had earlier turned invisible and then polymorphed into a bee, watched everything unfold from the ceiling.

An angel psychometrist named Lieutenant Ebrissa entered the building that appeared to be in charge. He meditated on the stage to divine what happened earlier. His eyes glowed and followed the earlier scene in a vision. Hollia had the hots for Ltd Ebrissa.  
  
Mara and Michael left Orevana, Dimwec, and Viadka to “get snacks” and figure out how to handle Ebrissa seeing the truth of what happened. Kullic also left the area Boros told him to stay in. They all meet up with Hollia but they aren’t able to figure out how they could stop the angel or his vision. Luckily, it didn’t appear he was able to identify who created the death portal.  
  
By now there were 30+ Boros in the building. They set up a “Verity Circle” for those exiting, which the team realized was a zone of truth. Viadka was stopped when she tried to leave and threw another tantrum. Meanwhile, Michael turned into a teacup piggy and Orevana gave him to Dimwec as a gift. Dimwec was delighted at the thought of butchering the piggy for bacon.

Hollia went first and passed until she mentioned her dress was from Faerun, so Boros pulled her aside for additional questioning. Peri turned from a bee into himself in front of the guards. Shocked, they manacled him and brought him into the Circle. Peri said he knew someone involved - a demon named Morla. Boros want to bring him to Sundholm for further questioning, so they bring him into a covered wagon outside. Mara enters the circle next. She started ranting about the Nulvir and Nyogi. She said Morla is a succubus and that she ran “Fight Club”. Ltd Ebrissa casts silence on her before she is cuffed and brought into the wagon by Boros. Viadka leaves via the Verity Circle with no issue. Dimwec, holding Michael, also goes through with no issue, though he accidentally admitted to wanting to “dress him up”. Orevana passed through the Circle with no issue. Kullic also passed the questioning, but told Boros that the pig “wasn’t a pig”. They went outside to investigate.  
  
Across the street, Dimwec is holding Piggy Michael while he waits for his carriage. As the guards approach, they ask for the pig and compel Dimwek to hand it over. Michael jumped out of Dimwec’s arms and ran under the nearest wagon to sprout wings and start flying away. An angel pursues him down an alley and catches him. He turned back into Michael and then captured the angel in a resilient sphere. Then, Michael disguised himself as the Angel that he captured claiming “the pig went this way” and ran down another alley. This was convincing and Boros ran down a side alley before Michael slipped away.  
  
Hollia slipped out of the building and convinced the guards to let her talk to Peri/Mara. Once close enough, she BAMF’d the three of them back to the Library. Kullic is able to slip away as the guards were chasing after the pig.

Orevana is unable to convince Opatrovatel’ka to let her come home with Dimwec. She kisses him goodbye (which was awkwardly reciprocated) and manages to get some hair for future scrying. Kullic, Orevana, and Michael reconvene with the rest of the group at the University.
