## Summary
The party spends a few days in the Leisure Zone until the Rite of Obligation is set to happen. During the ceremony, we notice that the Orzhov Workers are separated from the Guildless. The Workers have done this multiple times, but the Guildless look sad, weary, and dirty. The ceremony happens and is full of panache - there's a choir and a priest that mentions “The Black Dawn” and “Agyrym's ascension across Ravnica”. After the ceremony, we manage to break off from the Worker group and disguise ourselves as Orzhov guards/Izzet engineers and check out one of the Waste Treatment facilities. With the help of Hollia's forgery skills, we manage to get into Orzhov Garrison North, which is a more secure & guarded area. We plan to proceed deeper into the site to get intel on Pracovisku Citadel and attempt to get into the Operations & Logistics Building under the guise of investigating the pipes.

## Detailed Notes
- The past few days in the Leisure Zone:
	- Mara has a heart to heart with Hollia about drinking too much
	- Peri pours over the map to commit it to memory and try to understand the layout better
	- Mara and Peri gather containers so that Peri can create 2 gallons of mayo for future use (3 jugs and 12 bottles, spread among the party)
	- Orevana does all of the leisure activities: brothels, the Orc bar, and going to a massage parlor with Hollia. She also probes Donwec on what his plans are after the assassination (none). She confirms he won’t turn on them afterward and determines he still thinks she’s a Kartashov
- We leave Donwec in a brothel while we go to Nadvlada for the blessing ceremony (Rite of Obligation)
- Grizzut is investigating how to get into the domes
- On the way to Nadvlada
	- Obzedat Gate & Viadka Gate both have MANY guards, including giants, knights, and spirits; Viadka Gate has people running off to the left inside it (a pathway or something?). Both gates have statues of the gate’s namesake nearby
	- Michael does not see any tunnels nearby
	- There are at least 1k troops in the Orzhov Garrison South
	- The tower nearby (MC2) has purple lightning running out of it into the storm clouds nearby
- Nadvlada Cathedral itself/Guildless Housing zone
	- The Guildless Housing has a ridge going along the outside. The “houses” look more like prisons, with guards and soldiers all around
	- There is not just one mass going on, but rather a stream of services. The workers enter through the front and the Guildless enter through the back
	- The cathedral itself has its back facing the prison area
	- The Behemoth is an old (200-300 years old) machine that has been crushed by fallen rocks
	- There is a large concentration of spirits inside Nadvlada Cathedral that emit a strong feeling of grief
	- We are in the throng of workers going in from the right and then out from the left; from this angle, we can’t see where the Guildless are leaving
	- The inside of the cathedral is decorated with gold filigree. We go upstairs and get into position. The Guildless are downstairs at the front of the cathedral. There is also a choir nearby
- The Rite of Obligation ("blessing ceremony")
	- It starts with the choir singing a song for a few minutes
	- The priest then does his schtick. He mentions “The Black Dawn” and “Agyrym's ascension across Ravnica” - which does not ring any bells for Orevana
	- The Workers appear to be bored; they’ve done this time and time again. The Guildless in general are sad and weary during the ceremony
	- The ceremony ends with more choral music. The Guildless exit through the back
	- We don’t hear or see any kind of magical spell put over the Guildless
	- Orevana sees many many Orzhov spirits in the rafters
- After the Ceremony
	- Mara pretends to have an upset stomach and Hollia throws up nearby to make it more believable
	- Peri has explosive diarrhea and almost hits Michael. A few indentured spirits come to clean up the poo
	- Belia sneaks off and watches the rest of the group to see what the next move is. She rejoins them when nobody else sneaks off
	- Orevana becomes invisible and sees that the Guildless are lead back down into the prison area via a gate - the only entrance/exit to the area. She also notices the entire housing area has a rampart and guards ALWAYS watching
	- Orevana manages to go into the South Garrison zone and enter the Command Center in search of an armory. She finds one and steals 3 sets of armor before she is interrupted by a spirit
- Our Plan: Check out the Waste Treatment plants
	- Be guards: Michael, Orevana, Donwec with the stolen armor; Mara disguised as a guard; Hollia paints her golden armor to match; Belia sneaks; Peri polymorphed
	- Hollia forges us documentation so we can go into the waste treatment centers to see if there are more direct paths to other zones
	- We approach the gate, present our inspection papers, and are let in
	- We don’t find any pipes going out of the area, but we now understand how the waste treatment centers work
- The next morning, we will pretend to be inspectors to get through gates. There is one in the Utility & Staging Zone - which is on the other side of the Pracovisku Citadel!!
	- Since Mara’s Disguise Self only lasts an hour, so she will instead disguise herself as an Izzet engineer with Grizzut’s help. We also disguise Belia as an engineer, too
	- In the meantime, Grizzut will investigate the djinns for each MC (MC = Manical Chamber)
	- We decide to check on Waste Treatment Plants on the way, specifically the ones in the North and South Orzhov Garrisons
	- Donwec has to keep his composure as he sees statues of himself and his mother at the respective gates
	- Pracovisku Citadel is up on a hill
	- The Waystation and Ljudo Gates have the most guards and are better fortified than the others
	- As we are walking, we see Ljudo Gate open and a group of Guildless being marched toward us - they look dirty and like they’ve been digging
	- In the Utility & Staging Zone there are giant stables with gigantic oxen-like beasts inside. The “Assembly” area is an organized waiting area for Guildless being lead through the "*Requiem Gate*"
	- The Operations & Logistics area is higher up and overlooking the Utility & Staging Zone. We notice some of the pipes run into the Operations & Logistics - we “found a mysterious white substance that we need to investigate”
