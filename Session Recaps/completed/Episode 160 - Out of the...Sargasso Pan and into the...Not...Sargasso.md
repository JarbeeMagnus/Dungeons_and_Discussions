## Summary
We navigate the ship toward the "lighthouse" AKA the flow beacon from Zellhane's map. During this time, Mara and Hollia decide to "behave" in front of the crew, Orevana builds some trust with Marge, and Mara and Peri reconcile about the heated discussion they had in the Fey Wildspace. After a few days of easy travel, Hollia and Peri take the rest of the crew to Silverymoon for some shore leave. Peri gets his finished staff from Ealon and he is VERY happy with it. The two return to The Ru and Hollia picks up the crew the next day with Mara. The next day, we stumble across a derelict ship that appears to belong to the Elven Imperial Navy. We investigate the ship, which is frozen with dead elves aboard. An orange and red light flashes to get our attention and some invisible being puppets the dead bodies to get Peri's attention. Once Peri responds, the lights turn green and a kitsune appears on The Ru and says they will "see us later". Orevana gives the dead elves their last rites with the help of a fireball from Michael. A few days later, we see Kinshala Sphere in the distance and start closing the gap (it's HUGE, so it takes a while). Another few days pass by when we are approached by a hammerhead ship. They signal to talk and we meet Urn Nadiif, Mr.Brightspark, and some of their crew. They claim to be part of the EIN and review our papers. Orevana and Peri pick up on some weird vibes when Nadiif tries to get us to come aboard his ship first to get various items that would help with air fouling and then later to look at their map. We decline and go on our way.

## Detailed Notes
**Seleszeni 11/Flamerule 10/Redmesa 9**
- We start with Hollia and Mara having an intimate conversation
	- Hollia was journaling when Mara approaches her
	- They decide that they should “behave” in front of the crew
	- Hollia brings up the Feywild and how Mara and Peri had a shouting match and explains how that’s how she feels all of the time - but she was taught how to manage her emotions through journaling
- Mara checks in with Peri & apologizes for their shouting match (there were no hard feelings on either side). They then find Orevana and ask her to check up on Marge. Marge was pretty normal in that she was pissy about everything - apparently the kitchen was knocked around so much it dented the pots & pans
- Back at the front of the ship:
	- The windows at the front of the ship remain broken. Jilly gathers all of the pieces so that there aren’t shards of glass strewn about
	- Zellhane thinks the lighthouse that Algus pointed us towards is actually the flow beacon & that we shouldn’t get too close to it
	- When Mara mentions shore-leave, Zellhane looks terse. Mara suspects that it’s probably difficult to have all the fighters (except Belia) off the ship for long periods of time
	- The plan is to go to Kinshala Sphere only to refresh the air bubble. We’ll properly stop at Thunder Junction to pick-up provisions & crew

**Seleszeni 12/Flamerule 11/Redmesa 10**

- We travel for a day and a half to get closer to the “lighthouse” which is actually the flow beacon (Redmesa 10, 1069)
	- Orevana spends time trying to help improve Marge’s perception of the group by helping her in the kitchen. She seems to make some progress (Marge made some small talk about cooking)
	- Jilly sees the beacon, which looks a lot like a black hole. We get closer and see that it’s some kind of eclipse with a void - and emits some kind of vibration. As we revolve around it, we see that it’s not a sphere but like a thick plate. The angle of the plate indicates the direction we should go (perpendicular to the plate face)

**Seleszeni 15/Flamerule 14/Redmesa 13**

- A few more days pass and Hollia takes Ellegorin, Marge, Jilly, Belia, and Peri (to pick up his staff) to Silverymoon
	- Ealon excitedly reveals Peri’s new staff - and he LOVES it. The meteorite Peri uses as his spell focus also fits perfectly into the crook of the staff, so Peri uses vines to properly affix it into "Peri's Magic Staff"
	- Hollia introduces the rest of the crew to her parents. They all enjoy muffins from Hollia's mom, Della. After a few hours, Hollia and Peri jump back to the ship - the rest of the crew will be picked up tomorrow
	- Orevana and Michael have another Drakes (Go Fish) tournament. Orevana barely wins the best of 5
- After another 12 hours, we pass through the clouds of the Sargasso and into a pocket of clean air. We see stars in the distance. Zellhane points out a brighter star off the port side that is the sphere of Witchlight. We orient ourselves toward the Kinshala sphere (opposite of Witchlight)

**Seleszeni 16/Flamerule 15/Redmesa 14**

- Mara and Hollia go to Silverymoon a few hours early to get some alone-time before they have to bring the crew back
	- Ellegorin had a shopping spree at the Lady’s College. He also met with Magus Odizar during his shore-time
	- Marge purchased new knives and spices
	- Jilly spent her time at the bath house and shopping for nice dresses & perfume
	- Belia also had a good time hanging out at the college and stirring up trouble with the tree-critters

**Seleszeni 17/Flamerule 16/Redmesa 15**

- Orevana is piloting the ship, Michael is on watch. He sees a light and Zellhane (and Jilly) say that it’s a derelict ship
	- Zellhane wakes Mara from her nap & asks if we should approach or not. Mara decides we should go check it out
	- As we approach, we see that it’s a damselfly ship
	- Jilly and Orevana both saw some kind of red/orange light flash periodically
- We do a séance for Orevana to be able to scry into the helm room. Michael mage hands a coin with light cast on it so she can see better inside the ship
	- The helm of the ship has been sawed off and there are frozen splatters of blood and a body behind it, also frozen
	- After a few minutes, Orevana hears a light laugh and a flash of red light up the stairs
	- A few minutes later, the body of an elf tumbles down the stairs into the helm room. The elf had a uniform with an emblem of a gold S atop a white field (Zellhane confirms it’s the emblem of a passenger service called Sindiath Line that had ties with the Elven Imperial Navy). The elf’s mouth suddenly opens and says, “I’d like to talk to Periwinkle”. Another elf body floats down and makes a joke about how long we’re taking
	- We relocate to the top of the deck to shout to the entity and re-scry so Orevana can respond back in case the entity can’t shout back. We notice the red color flashes when it talks
	- Peri turns invisible and uses Mara as a puppet as he shouts back “hey, what do you want!” The red light immediately changes color to a softer, aurora borealis color
	- Peri is visible again and then turns into his star dragon form. The blue/green light radiates around, then a red/orange column shoots out of the ship and a small fox with four eyes and many tails appears on deck and says “See you soon!” and poofs away - NOTE: the sea turtle’s last conversation was with a many-tailed fox!!
	- Michael says the being was some kind of magical kitsune that is fey-like
	- Orevana asks Michael to burn the bodies (he uses airball followed by fireball) and she says their last rites. Michael salutes (incorrectly)

**Seleszeni 20/Flamerule 19/Redmesa 18-21**

- Hollia gets us back on course and after about 3 days (Redmesa 18, 1069), we encounter a MASSIVE crystalline sphere (Kinshala Sphere!). It’s bigger and grander than what we encountered leaving Ravnica (which was bland and gray). We approach and it keeps getting bigger and bigger as we approach and we finally get "close" to it after another 2.5 days (Redmesa 21, 1069)
	- Zellhane explains that different spheres have different “rules” to get inside. Kinshala is one where you just approach and then teleport onto the other side
	- Orevana is in the cargo hold with Peri. Hollia is asleep. Michael and Mara are on deck as we approach
	- Michael, with the spyglass, sees another ship come up from underneath/behind us and shouts “we’ve been flanked!” The ship is using some kind of light in a pattern. Mara calls Zellhane up to decipher the code. She say it’s a hammerhead ship that is signaling it’s a Sindiath Line ship working with the Imperial Navy and is asking us to pull over. Mara calls for everyone to be prepared for the worst and we all go up on deck
- 10-15 minutes later, the hammerhead ship comes up on the portside
	- We see the flags of the Imperial Navy and Sindiath Line
	- They ask (via signals) to send a little boat over and to check our logbook
	- There are 7-8 people on the boat that floats over to us. They are wearing the same uniform from the bodies of the damselfly ship. One person (the helmsman) is wearing white robes. There is also a Giff and some hot elf named Captain Urn Nadiif of the Aminatu (the name of the ship)
- Captain Nadiif thanks us for letting him come aboard. He explains that we are in the middle of a war zone with the Scro Empire and that they are looking for pirates. He introduces the Giff as Mr. Brightspark
	- Brightspark reviews our logbook from Zellhane
	- Captain Nadiif intervenes when Hollia tries to ask Brightspark a question. He makes introductions to our whole party & asks about where we’re headed and why
	- He mentions that the Golarian Sphere is being overrun by the Scro (Mara is concerned about Absalom Station, but he doesn’t have any info)
	- We also mention that there are Neogi and Nulviir in the Sargasso, which he appreciates
- Captain Nadiif asks if we need anything and we inquire about some kind of spacesuit or two and he says we could spare a few Gadabouts he could spare (some fish that you put over your head). He also offers to let us look at some 3d map they have
	- Peri and Orevana think the offer is too good to be true and we decide to decline their offer (there was some kind of facade that slipped for a moment)
	- Peri, Michael, and Hollia notice the helmsman of the small boat had an amulet that has a ladder on it. Hollia knows of no Elven god that worships a deity whose symbol is a ladder (or any God that uses a ladder, for that matter) - she doesn’t share this with the group, just notes it internally
