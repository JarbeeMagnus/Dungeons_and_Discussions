## Summary
After the team brings the remaining prisoners from the caravan to their hometowns to rebuild, we learn from Grizzut more information about the Guild Mages and Djinn and how to capture them. We then decide to approach the storm via the path that the prisoner caravan was taking. At Fallen Angel, we find a large amount of giants, knights, and thrulls dressed in Orzhov colors - they mention there are reinforcements coming tomorrow. We decide to leave them be and continue the recon mission. We find an outpost just inside the storm that appears to be a gated tunnel going into a giant plateau. Peri is sent in to investigate and finds another gate in the tunnel and one at the end, all heavily guarded. On the other side of the tunnel, there are clear skies and what appears to be a mining facility. Peri brings this info back to the party, who had been testing the limits of the anticepters and the harsh storm - they were barely working and we pulled out before we broke the anticepters. We plan to leave The Ru hidden nearby (with Zellhane & Jilly) and try to get into the eye of the storm (the party + Donwec) by joining the reinforcements that we learned about earlier.

## Detailed Notes
- We plan to drop off Grizzut and Belia at the tower, then we will take the remaining folks (~100) to their hometowns to rebuild. 5-6 towns  
- The anticepters will allow us to travel through the storm (we now have 3 of them!)  
- Jilly and Zellhane appear to be having a reaction to the wasp stings. Hollia removes any poison from them and they appear to immediately feel better  
- Grizzut excitedly says he finished the 3 “counterveil anchors”  
	- He said the 6 mages we were looking to capture for him should be with 3 djinn  
	- The djinn are causing the storm 
	- When the counterveil anchors touch a djinn, the djinn will be neutralized  
	- Grizzut does not know how long the djinn will be neutralized  
	- We plan to follow the path the sled was going to take and do some recon
- We decide to check out the pathway between the Fallen Angel and the edge of the storm to look for paths and tunnel entrances when Jilly and Peri notice lights down by the Fallen Angel  
	- Hollia gets us in closer and Peri sees Orzhov giants with pikes and a few other medium-sized Orzhov knights. They appear to be investigating the aftermath of our fight  
	- We send Peri closer to investigate as an invisible bat  
	- The group has an anticepter to protect against the weather. There are 3 giants posted along the edge of the anticepter range   
	- There are also 6 knights, 8-9 small thrulls, 4 winged thrulls (1 in each corner), and various (12-15) indentured ghost servants  
	- There is a tent and, while Peri is watching, 2 humans come out talking. One is a cleric (the one in charge) and the other is a mage. They said they were waiting on reinforcements that should hopefully arrive tomorrow. Otherwise, they will have to hold down the fort and wait for the construction workers to come through the area  
	- The bodies of the Rakdos demons we killed were burned up in the corner
- We do nothing for now and continue the recon mission  
	- As we get closer to the main storm, the weather starts to get harsher (even with the anticepters). The ground also gets more ruinous and overgrown as we continue  
	- Within a couple of miles from the storm wall, Peri sees another group of lights - it appears to be another outpost  
	- We see there is indeed an edge to the storm  
	- Peri goes back in, but this time as an invisible giant eagle because the winds are harsher  
	- There is a plateau about ½ a mile from the edge of the storm with a huge fortified gate in it. There are about 12 giants standing guard at the gate. In the area there are also about 25 knights  
	- While Peri is spying, Hollia attempts to poke the storm with the edge of the anti-weather field to see how it holds up. It seems to work, so Orevana checks in with Peri. He decides to go into the tunnel. After about ½ miles he hits another gate guarded by another 12 giants. One side of the gate is open, so Peri continues  
	- Back on The Ru, Hollia continues testing the limits of the anticepters by dipping in and out of the storm. Even with the anticepters, the roar of the wind and storm are deafening as the storm interacts with the bubble. After about a minute, when Hollia pulled out of the storm we noticed the light coming off of the anticepters started flickering and dimming. They went back to normal after we leave the heavy storm
	- Peri continues flying through the tunnel and gets to the third (and last) gate. The weather on the other side is clear and sunny. The eye of the storm is 6 miles (10km) wide and is open at the top like a tube. There is a mining facility just inside the entrance. There are three domes shooting lightning into the storm - we presume the djinn are housed in those domes. Best guess is that the storm is probably about a mile wide and 10-15 miles tall. After looking around, Peri tells the team about what he saw
- We now need to decide what to do next  
	- Our main objective is to get info on what Orzhov’s connection is to the neogi and nulviir and how it relates to Project Firmament - where are the souls, what are they doing  
	- Side objectives: Neutralize the djinn, capture the wizards, allow Donwec to kill Ludo Jr  
	- Our plan: hide The Ru in one of the giant ruins nearby and cover it in branches. Peri can use druidcraft to really cover the ship up  
	- We decide to take Donwec into the storm with us and leave Jilly and Zellhane with the ship  
	- We will go back and pick up Grizzut so he can accompany us into the base, then we will proceed with the plan: We wait on the side of the road for them to pick us up using deception - we can pretend to be a patrol and say that we need a ride back inside
