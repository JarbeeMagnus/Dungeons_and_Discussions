## Summary
The boys are issued severance notices from Pracovisku Development, so we go to the office to pay fines and for everyone else to officially quit. We check in on Kullic's church and then head to talk to Madam Gajic about getting to the 6th District (travel is currently halted due to Gruul and Rakdos fighting in the area). She sends us to Pivlichino's where we learn about an upcoming Winter Rally - we can hire a pilot after that, since the fastest will be in town for the event. The Rally is in 2 weeks, so we decide we can try to heal Dimwec/Donwec in the meantime. Orevana arranges a date for the following day at a crossbow shop.

## Detailed Notes
Early the next day, the party hears a knock on the door. Orevana answers to see a flying thrall with a golden mask who hands her an envelope. Michael, Kullic, and Peri are given severance notices due to not going to work last week. The fee was 825 zinos per person with compounded interest per day. They owed 2475 total so far. If not paid by the end of 4 weeks, a contract reaper will arrive to collect the zinos or the person serves in the afterlife. They decide to go pay the fine and, while at the office, Mara, Hollia, and Orevana officially quit.  
  
With time to kill, the party decides to check on Kullic’s church. He finds out that the Slag Sisters have been doing a good job guarding and that nothing bad has happened. After that, they  go to Keg Theory to have some lunch and drinks. They ask about the 6th District but the only thing they find out is that people are taking jobs in the 6th District that are paying 150,000 zinos for 3 months of dangerous work.  
  
After lunch, it’s late enough for them to go to Indigo and meet with Madam Gajic. They do the usual ritual of ordering the Clam’s Priasm before being escorted to a back room to talk with Gajic. They learn that Gajic will not meet with us at home or her home - we will always have to do the ritual at Indigo to see her. Gajic said the Pracuvisku’s live on Oligarch’s Row, and that we can’t go in unless we have Orzhov blood. When pressed further, she said “they’ll know if it’s Orzhov blood”. Gajic also said she has not seen a thrull become a ghost. She believes there is fighting in the 6th District and that's why travel is cut off. She said she only has one contact that may be able to arrange travel for the party: Pivlichino.

Kullic made comments about Gajic not being helpful, which she was very upset about. Mara smooths things over and Orevana asks Gajic about her day. She mentioned she has painful sores on her back that are because she is of a noble Orzhov bloodline; all noble Orzhov bloodlines have problems. The party thanked her for her time before moving on to Pivlichono’s.  
  
Upon their arrival, the party is seated on the first floor with the rest of the rabble. They waited for Pivlic to meet them at their table for 30 minutes but he does not show up. They move to the second floor, where they find Pivlic excitedly talking to a group of people about the upcoming Winter Rally (a zeppelin race / fight). They approach Pivlic and he is happy to see them (“*Madam Gajic has been telling me about you!*”) and gives them a token to go upstairs to the third floor where they can speak more privately.  
  
On the third floor, they are surprised to see Do-drak is also there, reading a book. They approach him and he seems annoyed, but polite. He happened to be in town and confirmed he hadn’t heard from Morla since his arrival a few days prior. He admitted he was told not to kill the person he was fighting when he was recruited for Fight Club. He asked Orevana about her arm and if it was from Simic. She confirmed and he bought her a drink. He said that he used to be a troll before Simic got ahold of him. They both “were given” their “upgrades” and seemed to bond over it slightly.  
  
Hollia asked him about the book he was reading and he gave her the book - a romance about an elf and a harpy called “A Less Rotten Love”. This is different than the last book the party saw him reading, which was a compilation of ethics essays called “Against The Walls of Agyrem”. Hollia accepts the book and trades him a romance book in return called “Hellcats”, which he seems excited to receive.

The party asks if he would be willing to work for them and Do-drak said he would for the same pay he would get from the mines. He also said that he would need to be back in 2 weeks for the Winter Rally. They think that is agreeable before they go back to their table to wait for Pivlichino. The party asks if he would be willing to work for them and Do-drak said he would for the same pay he would get from the mines. He also said that he would need to be back in 2 weeks for the Winter Rally. They think that is agreeable before they go back to their table to wait for Pivlichino. He finally arrives to discuss how he can get them to the 6th District. Since the Winter Rally is in 2 weeks, fast zeppelin racers from all over Ravnica will be in town. Pivlichino said we could hire one of them to take us to the 6th District, specifically someone named Pannikin Flystalker, a viashino (lizardman). The location they’ll be going to (the reclamation site) is in the East Side of the 6th District. Pivlichino wants to send goods with the party to drop off in the West Side, which they accept. They have yet to determine the final price.  
  
Orevana goes back to talk to Do-drak about the Winter Rally. He explains that the race has a series of pit stops where there are fights. She tells him they are going to compete and if he’d be on their team. He laughs and says he’s always a solo fighter to keep it fair on the others. He has competed for the last 10 years and won 7 of them. She asks about him working with them after the race and he agrees as long as he is paid (note: it’ll be 1k zinos per week).  
  
The team goes back to the garage to talk. They have 2 weeks before the race, so they want to pursue the “healing Dimwec” avenue to hopefully get more information about Pracu dealings. They do another seance, but this time it is so Orevana can cast Sending. The spirit is a bard - he gives her Sending and did a dance before he vanished.

Orevana arranges a meeting with Dimwec for the next day at a crossbow shop called “Geyb’s Crossbow Shop” in Plaza West. They will meet at 2 because he is going to a chapel (bank) at noon. Orevana and Peri bury the boy that she landed on outside before they, and everyone else, go to bed.  
  
The next morning the team does another seance so Orevana can cast sending again in case Dimwec gets lost or is horribly late. She tries to summon the bard from before, but instead the spirit is Jamiroquai the goblin. They head out to Geyb’s Crossbow Shop about 30 minutes early. The shop is HUGE and has dozens of people inside. They start scoping out the place. Orevana speaks to an employee who happens to be the owner’s brother, Deyb. She finds out there are 4 private ranges that can be rented, so she reserves one for when Dimwec arrives (Bay C).   
  
The plan is for Peri to summon a fey to charm Opatrovatel’ka to leave. Once alone, Hollia will look over Dimwec to determine what might be wrong with him and, if able, heal him. Then the party will have an ally on the inside that can help them. Dimwic will arrive in 20 minutes, so they don’t have much time to prepare.
