## Summary
We meet up with Befallor, Delandri, and Professor Ellegorin at Keg Theory. After some drinks, we return to The Ru back in Nivix and review the repairs and upgrades the Izzet League are installing for us. The next day, the party splits up to hand out the manifestos Mara wrote. Peri and Hollia tell Weslyn what's going on in the hopes she could bring that info to the rest of the Boros Legion. Orevana and Belia update Gajic on what we learned about the Pracovisku family. Michael and Mara speak to Senator Rinsma to get the Azorius Senate on-board. Once regrouped, Captain Zellhane goes over gravity wells, flow beacons, what to expect in the Sargasso, and how long it will take to get out of the Ravnican Sphere (11 days). Just before daybreak the next day, we are awoken to Orzhov trying to impound the ship so we get the hell out of dodge. Over the 11 day journey to the edge of the Ravnican Sphere, we learn the basics of spelljamming. Orevana scries on Donwec and sees he has been taken over by the intermediary device and further scrying fails. Hollia says she might stay with us instead of returning to Silverymoon with Befallor and Delandri. Peri's meteorite starts singing and glowing as we approach the edge of the sphere. He also notices the stars are partial spheres (to his surprise). Orevana gets a POUNDING headache that climaxes into some kind of vision right as we leave the sphere. Also, the Izzet lightning cannon that was installed had metal wires/plating around the ship - it wasn't to power the cannon but something else as it dropped off the ship and turned invisible right as we crossed the sphere.

## Detailed Notes
- We arrive at Keg Theory and it’s full of Orzhov workers getting meals after a long day of construction (we don’t recognize anyone)
	- Hollia notices Delandri’s profile nearby. She moves to the other end of the bar so she can see him but not hear him, but Mara wants to get Yuri’s attention right away
	- Peri lifts Mara by her belt to try to get Yuri’s attention. He uses thamaturgy to help her get his attention. He smiles when he sees us and says we have a room downstairs (previously the Rakdos sex room); he says our “friends” are down there already, waiting for us
	- When we get downstairs, we find Befallor talking to Professor Ellegorin Omavius (AKA Magnum Opus) over a table full of books and paper. The professor also has a homunculus sleeping under the table named Dhazo
	- Ellegorin excitedly gets information from Belia to chronicle her as part of “Gatewatch 2.0” (he doesn’t ask about Orevana or Peri, and we don’t bring his attention to it)
	- Befallor complains about not getting an audience at Nivix for the past 6 weeks (Michael asks if she’s level 5, to which she takes a swig). Both Befallor and Delandri want to leave Ravnica ASAP
	- Ellegorin asks about planewalking which we say we’ll explain things to him later. We sent the NPC’s to Nivix to check on The Ru
	- Shortly after, we’re served food. While we eat, Delandri quietly discusses Ellegorin’s incompetence with Hollia. Orevana interrupts and explains that "Agrym is where Spirits go" before going back to converse with the others. Delandi continues the conversation with Hollia and says that Ellegorin’s memories have been “smudged”. She thinks perhaps a Greater Restoration or Remove Curse might help him
	- Hollia writes a word on his forehead but it doesn’t do anything
	- Mara asks Yuri for provisions and gets 4 casks of ale and 2 casks of water. He tells her we can find food at an Orzhov supermarket nearby but they’re already closed
- We bring Ellegorin, Befallor and Delandri (and a very drunk Hollia) back to The Ru to sleep for the night
	- The Izzet repairs to The Ru include fixing the leg joint, adding conduits along the outside connecting to a disk on the belly that connects to a lightning cannon up top (this will replace the mangonel), a double-ballista, changing out the wings (they’re rectangular now), a new hoist for the cargo bay (metal chain instead of rope, along with a platform), and a new round eye to replace the broken left one made out of metal-glass (it’s clear)
	- The strawberry vines were taken down
	- Mara and Hollia get the rundown from the Izzet repairers. Peri asks if they can install some kind of telescope/looking glass to the ship as well
	- Mara gives Izzet a list of provisions to gather tomorrow - things like toiletries, food, water, hammocks
	- About 6 Izzet guards are stationed around the plaza at night
	- Hollia cuddles and whispers to the ship; words fly out of her before she passes out - she hallows the ship
	- We rest onboard The Ru for the night
- We split up into three groups to hand out manifestos:
	- Peri and Hollia go to talk to Weslyn: She’s happy to see Peri. She says she’ll do what she can to bring info to other Boros
	- Mara and Michael speak to Senator Rinsma:
		- Mara preps Michael on the way. Rinsma tells them that his daughter is still in hiding
		- He's thankful that we shared this with him (he takes lots of notes)
		- They also shop for some clothes 
	- Orevana and Belia go to talk to Madam Gajic: 
		- They’ll shop a bit for some clothes while we wait for Indigo to open
		- Orevana tells her all about the Pracovisku plotting, dead sons/cousin, and the matriarch is back. She hands copies of the manifesto
		- Gajic gives us some bottles of wine, including some Casarta (expensive)
		- Orevana also tells Gajic that we’re sharing this info with Boros and Azorius and that she may want to distance her family from the Pracovisku's (since there is evidence that the Pracovisku family is the only one responsible for infringing on the Guild Pact)
- The ship has lights with various settings (high, med, low, off)
- Zellhane wants to go over the map with us
	- Ravnica sphere - we need to find the crack out of the area (hummingbird “stars” inside the sphere that would help point the way)
	- Peri knows this 11-star constellation (Kolibrik). It's strongest in the sky during the Spring equinox (in a few weeks)
	- Orevana knows astrological associations with this constellation (which aren’t important)
	- It will take about 11 days of travel to go from where we are on the planet to the edge of the sphere (this is while going 11mil miles per day - WOW)
	- She explains gravity wells to us
	- Zellhane also explains Flow Beacons. They are ancient artefacts that emit light. And if you get too close (within about 20 miles) you disappear and are never seen again
	- The Ravnica Sphere is floating in what's called a Sargasso: a massive shapeless mass of gasses and chunks of minerals. There are magically dead pockets. The phosphrus gas pockets are extremely flammable. Electric beams. And creatures that inhabit the zone - Scavers, Goon Balloons, Space Clowns, Vampirates
	- Overwhelmed, Orevana sleeps in a Leomund’s Tiny Hut on the ground outside
- Just before daybreak, Orevana wakes and hears Orzhov guards wanting to impound The Ru. We decide to get the hell out of dodge. Peri’s rock vibrates in his pocket
- We have an 11 day journey to the edge of the Sargasso
	- We’ll swap out piloting the ship each day so we all get practice - Hollia will be flying one shift each day, the rest we swap around (except Peri cause the chair is metal)
	- Orevana will scry on Donwec, starting on day 2 and YIKES. Other scrying attempts on Donwec fail after this
	- Orevana tries scrying on Grizzut (she finds a small screwdriver of his on the ship to assist) - this scrying works (he's in some kind of lab). Kullic is in the background!
	- Belia will run gym classes for us each day so we stay IN SHAPE
	- Mara and Hollia have a heart to heart. Hollia decides she may be staying with us after all (instead of going back to Silverymoon with Delandri and Befallor)
	- Jilly and Zellhane go over Spelljamming basics with us - including jumping off the side of the ship to practice getting back on
	- Peri’s rock starts emitting light as we continue - stronger each day. Day 7 or 8 he whispers “what are you trying to tell me” and hears some sound coming out of it (when placed at his ear)
	- Orevana starts getting very light headed as we travel further out, becoming persistent. It becomes a migraine on the last day, so she spends it alone in a dark room with cold compresses - it doesn’t help
	- The hummingbird constellation gets noticeably closer - Peri is ECSTATIC and spends all of his time on deck. He notices they are only semi-spherical (not full spheres)
	- Orevana cries out when they get close to the edge and then passes out. She wakes up after a few moments with the same nausea and migraine
	- Peri involuntarily wild shapes in and out of various constellations. His spell focus starts singing and glowing brightly. The crack between the stars has an edge to it that only he can see. As we pass into the crack, the blackness becomes scintilating light. Like an iridescent metal sheen - crystalline
	- Hollia pumps us full of temporary hp (and the npc’s too!) When we pass through the crack, suddenly the metal plate from the laser cannon spins off the bottom of the ship and becomes invisible - UH WHAT?!
