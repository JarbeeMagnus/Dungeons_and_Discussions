## Summary
We have a big fight on the top of the reverse gravity field with a Neogi and Umber Hulk. Hollia races toward us in the Ru and nose dives into the center of the eye of the storm. Three Neogi ships show up and they and The Monolith (the floating black tower) fire at The Ru. Hollia expertly dodges most of the attacks. We deliberate trying to free another djinn but then think better and leave Koren.
