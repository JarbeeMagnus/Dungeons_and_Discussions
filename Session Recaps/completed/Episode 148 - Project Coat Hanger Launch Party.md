## Summary
We get a run-down of what Grizzut encountered right before he got captured - he gives us a map of the MC tower and indicates we need to trap the djinn in addition to the Guild Mages. When asked for details, he confirms that the djinn will forced into labor at the water stations for the rest of their lives (which we don't approve of). Using Peri's tunneling magic, we make our way from inside the Tailings Storage (just a bunch of rocks?) to just outside the MC entrance. Hollia bluffs us inside where we found a pair of elevator shafts that are powered by air elementals. We take the small elevator up to where we expect to find the djinn. We manage to kill a priest and two wizards, and disconnect two of the three mechanical stations that are keeping the djinn trapped.

## Detailed Notes
- Note: It’s late afternoon
- Grizzut tells us what he found in MC3
	- He used an elevator from the Command Center to get inside
	- He saw a glass orb inside
	- The guards were expecting him, so he was captured right away
	- The Counterveil Anchors will allow us to trap the Djinn, but we would need to carry them once the Anchors are engaged
- What if we release the Djinn? They could get revenge on their captors
- Grizzut said if we bring the Djinn back to the Izzet, they would be “put back into the water stations” - so basically forced labor
- Our plan is to dig into the Tailings Storage under the Requiem Gate, then into the worker housing to get to MC3
- MATH FOR ANYONE WHO CARES:
	- 1km = 3280.84ft
	- 3280.84ft/5ft per turn
	- 656.168 turns to travel 1km * 6 seconds per turn = 3937.008 seconds of tunneling
	- 3937.008 seconds/60 seconds per minute/60 minutes per hour = 1.09 hours to travel 1km 5ft at a time via cantrips (not counting any breaks or whatever)
- We get into the Tailings Storage area and we manage not to alert any guards
- Another tunnel to get near the Command Center of MC3 behind another building
- Hollia convinces the gate guards to let us in (“We have orders to get orders”)
	- The next two gates let us in with no issue - there was some kind of light signal used for letting us through
	- We find two elevators - one large door being loaded by goblins with stuff and one smaller door that Grizzut used last time
	- Michael checks above the elevator and found an air elemental harnessed to the elevator (he named it Sam). Michael briefly thought about freeing the elemental but changed his mind
	- We move to the small elevator and prepare for a fight
	- The room has two flux chargers attached to some kind of machine with a ball in the middle. We kill a priest and two wizards that joined the fight midway. The third mechanical station has a "gelectrode" attached
