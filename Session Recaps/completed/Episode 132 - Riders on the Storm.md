## Summary
We pack up the ship to embark on a journey into the storm to the next tower when suddenly Grizzut gets a message from ARP. Apparently, Rakdos Cultists ambushed them (Jothor is dead) and he is hiding nearby at a mana storage tank facility. We fly out with the lightning rods to protect the ship - and charge a battery Grizzut says we need at the next tower. After going through a lightning storm, we encounter some flying snakes (Peri talked them out of bothering us), Kartashov mining troughs, and a town that we think had been flattened by some kind of worm. Grizzut gets another message from ARP that he had been found by someone. We go in ARP's direction and come across a Simic laboratory called "Yimmugo" that is run by a Vedalkan biomancer. While discussing Orevana's arm, she agrees to let him replace it on the condition that Hollia stays with her. While waiting, the rest of the party doesn't get any interesting information about the facility.

## Detailed Notes
After a long rest, the party prepares to depart in the morning. The winds are between 40-50mph. We decide to fly below the storm (5-6k ft) (sky-eaters, no ability to get bearings) but higher than the tree canopy to try to evade any spider tendrils. We plan to fly dead reckoning (straight line) as we finish preparing for departure. Peri and Hollia figure out how they will be communicating during the flight since he will be on deck deflecting lightning. Jilly and Donwec load the ship with cargo. Orevana talks to Donwec about how he’s been acting and he is evasive about it. She assures him that Ljudo will die no matter what happens and they both leave the conversation there. He goes off to rejoin Jilly in loading the ship. Mara goes to talk to Marge. She offers the ability to drop Marge off wherever she wants once we’re off planet, but Marge would have to cut the pessimism until then. Marge seems receptive to this deal. Grizzut dictates some of the items that ARP needs at the next Tower - including a battery that he wants to charge with the lightning from the storm.  
  
Suddenly, Grizzut gets a message from ARP saying there is trouble at the base - that there are Rakdos minions, Jothor was captured, and ARP is hiding nearby at a mana storage tank facility.

We decide that when we get to the mana facility that we will scope out the scene from the air before committing to a plan. Peri, with the help of Zellhane, navigates out our flight path in the storm. Hollia expertly pilots the ship through the rough winds. We fly 60ft above the tree canopy for the first hour, then we go up to 500ft above the treeline and speed up. Michael goes to the galley and asks Marge for snacks to which she said “no”. Michael left and Marge eats jerky out of a sack. Michael walks by Mara and says, “we’re going to have to kill Marge”. Donwec throws up into a bucket.  
  
A few hours later, we start rapidly approaching a thunderstorm. Hail belts the remaining front window, but it does not break. Hollia notices a shadow up ahead and lets the team know. Orevana sees a giant snake out to the right, then there are suddenly three. Mara makes the call to fly higher and faster. Hollia brings the ship up to 2500 feet. We  soon feel a strike of lightning nearby, but Hollia is able to dive out of the way so that the lightning only hits the rods (not the hull). Michael checks the battery and it looks like it started charging. We hear a bump on the bottom of the hull. Hollia calls out “Leech on the belly”. Peri attempts to convince the creatures to leave us alone using beast speech - it seems to have worked and the creatures leave us alone after that. We fly through a web of lightning and take some damage; the wind continues to whip around the ship.

Peri goes out to the deck and ties himself to the ship. He uses call lightning to direct the lightning to the rods to first charge the batteries, then move any bolts away from the ship as we fly through the bulk of the storm. Peri and Zellhane confirm we are on track.  
  
Hollia drops us from 2500ft to 500ft so we can see the ground to navigate. On the ground, we see large troughs full of rusted metal with Kartashov Industries on the side. Mara says, “It’s your fields,” and Orevana just nods. ARP sends another message to Grizzut that the place he retreated to has subterranean creatures. We are 88 miles into the 400 mile journey. We ascend back up to 2500ft to speed up again. We should be at his location in about 8 hours. Belia uses the coffee jug and Jilly sees it and demands her coffee back. Belia reluctantly gives it to her.   
  
Michael retreats to rest, but we fetch him when we see smoke coming up out of the forest from an abandoned town below us after about an hour. Hollia slows down and Mara uses Zellhane’s spyglass to check it out and sees a leveled village. Hollia brings the ship closer to the buildings to check it out. The buildings appear to have been burning for a few days. We see bodies in the rubble, so Mara yells down but gets no answer. Peri uses thamaturgy to try to yell again. No answer yet again. It almost looks like some kind of giant worm crushed the town based on the tracks going in and out of town. We decide that ARP’s safety is more important than checking out what happened, so we fly back up into the air and speed up towards the middle of the storm. Two hours later, we see three geysers coming out of a water reservoir nearby. Another hour passes and Grizzut gets another message from ARP. He was discovered and said something like, “*Oh dear, oh dear, oh how very curious, biom-*”.   
  
Peri and Zellhane navigate us toward where we think ARP is.

Hollia flies the ship to an opening in the ground and casts light on the hull of the ship. Mara and Orevana go out onto the deck to keep watch. Grizzut casts locate creature but doesn’t pick up on ARP’s signal. As we travel, Hollia sees light reflecting up (water 500ft below) and Orevana sees worn out lettering inisde the shaft spelling “yimmugo” followed by a simic symbol. Hollia brings the ship closer to the water (about 100ft above) and we see light blue light nearby. We see a dark shadow appear and start rising out of the water - a platform to land on.  
  
A figure comes out on the platform from the wall nearby and calls up “*Welcome to Yimmugo*”. He says he’s with our friend ARP and that he said we’d be coming. Grizzut says he can sense ARP in that direction. Michael asks if there will be tea and snacks to which the figure replies yes. He sends up a few winged creatures to help us land - they unroll the ladder and hold it at the bottom so it doesn’t sway as we get off the ship. Orevana tells Donwec to stay on the ship with Jilly, Marge, and Zellhane. We disembark and see that the figure is a Vedalken. Orevana tries to keep her white arm hidden as they entered the facility. The Vedalken introduces himself as Tamelk and explains that he is a biomancer and that Yimmugo is a simic laboratory. Hollia casts water walk on the party just in case.  
  
We enter what appears to be a submersible that is of Izzet and Simic design. We travel down a hallway into a living area. We find ARP holding a tall cocktail. Hollia takes a sip out of the straw and describes the flavor to him since he can't eat or drink. ARP explains that Jothor was killed by Rakdos cultists. He said there were at least 20 at the tower. Michael makes his cape look like chitin, which Tamelk comments on. Orevana is cagey and tries to leave. Tamelk offers her a drink and Mara steps in to lower the tension.

Tamelk tells us that there are 78 aquatic research subjects in this facility and that he has been here for 40 years. Momir Vig himself commissioned the facility (head of Simic). He’s here watching the animals since they are trapped here. Orevana asks if we can see the subjects. He excitedly says yes and brings us into a viewing chamber. A few creature hybrids swim by them. Michael asks if they are hive beings, but they are not.  
  
Orevana asks if he is an active Biomancer and he in turn asks about her arm - she did not successfully hide it. He takes a look at her arm and remarks at how advanced it was. Orevana asks about changing the color to be more discreet and he said that he can swap out the entire thing to something she would like better. Orevana decides to let him operate - as long as Hollia is with her at all times. Tamelk asks to keep her previous arm as payment for his services, which she agrees. Michael, Mara, and Belia go to into the living area to wait. ARP laments about missing his body so Mara steps in to pretend to eat for him (it was very wholesome). Peri checks in with ARP and asks about one of his students named “Psy”. ARP confirmed that Psy and other students disappeared (“*a successful experiment!*”) and he can’t disclose any more information on the subject. Peri said Psy was a friend of his. Peri also asks ARP if his dagger (or something else) could be imbued with magic (he previously had a staff that was broken saving a bunch of people). ARP said he could help with that back in his lab after all of this is over, “*provided we live!*” In the meantime, Mara does some snooping around the living quarters. She tries looking for a secret door at the bookshelf, but doesn’t find one. She doesn’t end up finding anything notable. Michael reads a book about green and blue mana. After a few hours, Orevana and Hollia rejoin the group with Orevana sporting an almost-flesh colored electric tentacle arm replacement.
