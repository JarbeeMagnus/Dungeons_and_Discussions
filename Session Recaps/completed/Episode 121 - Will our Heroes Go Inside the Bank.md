## Summary
We want to follow Ljudo Jr and Volenco into Vizkopa Bank because we know they'll be talking about Project Firmament. The party spells-up Mara and Peri to follow each of them. Despite entering 2 different ways, Volenco and Ljudo Jr. met inside and had a super secret conversation - too bad Mara and Peri were insects listening in! We reconvene back at the garage to find that Morla made mac and cheese and left a note saying she wanted to "talk business".

----

## Detailed Notes
Our heroes are all gathered at the University around 10pm and they realize they won’t be able to rest if they are to intercept Jr’s meeting in the morning. Mara admits to have taken Orevana’s clothes after they had all changed into their Fashion Show attire. The team went to Vizkopa Bank to assess the situation and put together a plan. The bank had 3 possible entrances and had a steady flow of carriages going in and out throughout the night. Gargoyles were perched on the bridges and at the doorways as well, so the team decided that quiet and sneaky was the way to go.  
  
Orevana and the group completed a spooky ritual to allow her to cast Locate Creature in case they needed it to find/track Ludo Jr. Michael could hide 2 people from divination detection, Peri could cast Pass Without Trace on 2 people, and Orevana could polymorph 1 person, so the group decided Mara and Peri would go in as flies - 1 with Volenco and 1 with Jr.  
  
Volenco arrives first, so Mara is spelled up to follow on Volenco’s cape. Volenco enters the bank using the left door. She has three black stones embedded in her right hand that she used to enter the building. She had to give a zino to the vampire blood statue in the lobby and was annoyed when it made her give it another one (the team assumed it was because Mara was with her and it’s 1 zino entry per person). When Volenco checked in with the receptionist, she said “The current counter sign is reticent”. Volenco goes deeper into the building with her receptionist as they review her itinerary for the day (it included reviewing "Project Phoenix" reports).

Volenco approaches a heavy lead door at the end of a long hallway where a spirit head asked for entry. She responds “reticent”. She enters and goes down a staircase. There are assembly line conveyer belts at the bottom of the stairs along both sides of the hallway that are not yet on. She goes down another staircase where there is another door at the bottom that also uses the passphrase “reticent”. She finally enters a depot that had carriages loading and unloading chests.  
  
Pracuvisku arrived about 15 minutes after Volenco. Peri beast shapes and gets spelled up to follow him as a fly. He perched on the carriage as it approached the bank from the side. The carriages that are leaving the bank match a previous encounter that the party had (a worm fight?). This entrance is set-up as an airlock with a donation bin for entry. Inside the bay, Peri sees Jr shake his head as he left the carriage, as if he had just taken eye drugs. They are in the same depot as Volenco/Mara.  
  
Ludo Jr and Volenco go into an empty bay area to talk. Before beginning their conversation, Volenco casts Private Sanctum. They discuss very important matters (convo is posted in the game-art channel). Volenco dismisses the spell before they go their separate ways.

Both Mara and Peri follow Jr to his carriage to leave. Mara flew back to the group, while Peri stayed on Jr’s carriage. The group realized he must have stayed with the carriage, so they hail their own carriage and Orevana uses Locate Creature to keep tabs on Jr. They end up at Augustin Station. Many of the ships in the station are attached to zeppelids. Jr’s airship has a “Pracuvisku Development” sign on the back and the attached zeppelid was crown shaped. Jr left to go to the 6th District, as he mentioned in the meeting with Volenco. Peri returned to the party in a sneaky fashion and startled everyone.  
  
Mara checked on her spelljammer and it has still not arrived. She also asked her favorite receptionist on getting a ship to the 6th District and she learns that travel to the 6th District is currently closed due to fighting between Gruul and Rakdos.  
  
The party decide to go back to the Garage where they found some things had been touched (but nothing was taken). Orevana admits that Mara taking her clothes when they left was a good idea. Michael had soap under his pillow and there was pasta on the stove and a bottle of nice wine with a note on it from Morla (see game-art channel). She wants to “chat business” with the group. Michael threw the wine, food, and soap off the balcony before anyone could taste (much to Kullic’s dismay). Michael successfully scried on Morla where she appeared to be at Rix Maadi. She was playing with a set of 6 dolls and an imp. They looked like they were play-acting fight club.  
  
The party has a few options for getting to District 6: Kullic knows someone named Art Vandalay who could give them a ride, Orevana could convince Dimwec to take them, and Peri could wildshape into a zeppelid and fly them. The team also wants to talk to Morla about her note.
