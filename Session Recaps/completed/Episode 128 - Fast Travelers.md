## Summary
We meet the survivors of The Ru and Kullic is spontaneously pulled away by Nivmizzit for an urgent task. Grizzut explains he was tasked with finding us and getting into the eye of the storm. After learning about what happened getting to Ravnica, we set out to the crash site of The Ru to fix it up (leaving Donwec with Grizzut). On the way, we find an abandoned power station with a flesh abomination inside that we quickly destroy. Upon finding the spelljammer, we see two Gruul teenagers looting it.

---

## Detailed Notes
Mara softly hugs Captain Zellahne who explains that she recently broke her leg. Mara asks if everyone survived and Zellahne’s face darkens. She said they lost most of the crew, but she thinks the ship is still intact. Zellahne calls out “Jilly” and a dwarven woman comes downstairs. She says hi excitedly and then runs back upstairs to get the others. Suddenly, Grizzut starts to convulse and a deep voice comes from his open mouth. Nivmizzit talks to Kullic through Grizzut and asks him to join them on another front. Kullic says yes and then immediately disappears after a flash.  
  
Soon after, we meet the rest of the crew. Jilly returns the stairs with a green tiefling (“Marge”), giant insect-like humanoid (“Rakcha”), and a small squirrel-like woman who is introduced as Belia. There is also a cyclops upstairs that Grizzut says is named Jothor that they briefly meet later. Grizzut goes back to the other end of the lab to gather things and prepare for their departure. We find out They have been here for about 4 weeks. Jilly seemed confused when Orevana asked how many of them work for Simic; they are all from off-world. Jilly explained how difficult it was for them to find Ravnica. They managed to find a cartogropher that had even heard of the place. It was an old flamboyant man named “Al Zharad”. He was the only one they could find after weeks of searching that could tell them how to get here. Jilly further explains that, upon their arrival, they saw that Ravnica is covered by an energy field that has one crack that they were able to get into by following a barge that looked like a big box. Three Neogi mind spider ships came out of the clouds and chased them. They flew into storm clouds and then got hit by some huge creature. They managed to recover and crash landed from about 50ft in the air. They lost some people in the crash, they lost others to creatures that attacked them, and others still to an acid swamp.

Soon, ARP and Grizzut found them and brought them to the tower to wait for us. The Firemind instructed Grizzut to find them. The ship is approximately 6km away. There is likely stuff in the area to fix the ship up - this used to be an industrial zone.  
  
Before they leave, Hollia fixes Zellhane’s leg, Belia’s tail, and Rakcha’s thorax with help from Peri. Grizzut also pipes up that he has been able to see Donwec the entire time (he was still invisible); we have to introduce him to everyone. We tell them that he is a Pracovisku and he says he’s the youngest son, but nothing more on that. Mara explains Project Firmament and why we are here. Grizzut said he thinks the Waystation is the center of the storm that we will be going to at some point. The storm is in the center of Koran. “*Recover the ship and go to the eye of the storm*” is what Nivmizzit told Grizzut to do. We follow Grizzut upstairs to the roof after ARP hops back into his mechsuit and goes outside. ARP flew his mechsuit to the roof and met us up there. Jothor (the cyclops) goes inside ARP’s open suit and they fly away. “*We’ll see you at the next tower!*” Grizzut yells out they leave. The other tower will be closer to the eye and will be our base of operations.  
  
We leave with Belia, Rakcha, and Zellhane to find and recover the ship. The tower is not scryable, so we leave Donwec with Grizzut so he’s safe. The weather clears up a little and we are able to walk through the swamp in relatively good weather. We see acid pools as we travel that look like oil spills. The forest opens up after about an hour. Ruins start to become more common as we travel closer to the crash site. Soon, we come to a river that is about half a mile wide that has a single bridge crossing over it. There is a powerplant on the other side of the bridge. Young drakes hunting birds nrarby are grabbed by ghostly forces when they land on top of the powerplant. We cross the bridge since it’s the only way across.

There are skeletal remains of a giant creature scattered around/inside the river below. The stench of the acrid river is nearly unbearable. We get to the building and Michael climbs down to one of the catwalks with Hollia and Rakcha. They go around the corner and see that the catwalk has a sudden drop; the rest of the party watches the doorway in case anything comes out of the front door of the powerplant. When everyone is back together, we go inside the building. Peri casts daylight, Hollia’s shield glows, Michael’s sword lights on fire, and Mara has one of her daggers light up as well. Needless to say, we had ample light. We hear the humming of a machine in the darkness. Then we find Techrohet’s prayer book (one of the crew that died here). Orevana casts Detect Evil and Good and initially does not detect anything. As we start to cross the room, she detects an abomination.   
  
Everyone except Mara and Orevana step on spider eggs and unleash swarms of lightning spiders on us. While we were fighting, a mouthy creature emerges from the shadows - the abomination Orevana detected. We defeat all of them and another mouth monster that came out of the shadows as well. After the fight, the party wants to try to find Techrohet, so Peri shouts down the stairs and we hear a faint, muffled reply. We decided to descend the stairs. The stairwell is covered in slimy goo. An orange flickering light is coming from the bottom of the stairs. It also gets hot as we descend. We soon see that the orange light is coming from a machine that is surrounded by flesh. We can see faces of people in the mound of flesh. Hollia eviscerates the flesh and completely eliminates it. Hollia, Michael, and Bellia realize it’s some kind of magic battery. We find some daggers, jewelry, and Techrohet’s mace in the area below.  
  
We leave the powerplant, and continue traveling until we find The Ru completely crashed in the forest.

Bellia sees a flicker of light from inside the ship and points it out. Michael, Mara, and Peri all gain the ability to fly (Michael becomes angelic, Mara’s cloak sprouts raven wings, and Peri wild shapes) and rush towards the ship. Bellia runs up branches to get inside. Hollia and Orevana stay outside the ship to watch. Mara and Bellia get to the ship first and find a teenager covered in dirt with a bone circlet. The person ducks and hides when they see Bellia / Mara. After a loud bang, Orevana and Hollia see two tribal teenagers slide down a rope with a satchel full of stuff from inside the ship.
