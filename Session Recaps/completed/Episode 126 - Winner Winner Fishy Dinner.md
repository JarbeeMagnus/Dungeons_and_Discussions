## Summary
We start off at the after-party of the Winter Smash Rally. The party  gets wasted and Peri convinces Mara to buy us all separate hotel rooms (though Mara cuddles piggy-Michael and Orevana & Donwec share a room). Mara and Michael awake the next morning with a blood and pasta-stained map of the 6th District, courtesy of Morla. While on the journey, Pannikin gives us the run-down of what it's like in the 6th since our map is slightly outdated. The party also discusses possibilities on how to get into the Reclamation Zone since access will be highly restricted. While stopping off in the 8th District to refuel, we decide to check out the cargo hold to see what we're shipping - the contents of one of the VERY locked & glyphed containers included expensive champagne and a secret compartment with drugs, jewels, and Simic-labeled genetic material. We store it all back before Pannikin gets back to the ship.

## Detailed Notes
All other opponents were killed save for Do-Drak (who notably didn’t come to the afterparty. At the party, Pivlic comments that it was the bloodiest Winter Rally he’s ever hosted. Pannikin confirmed with the party that they will be leaving in 2 days. Orevana, after some handsy celebrating with Donwec, attempts to find Hollia someone to hook up with at the party, but she was unsuccessful in finding someone Hollia was interested in. Soon, Donwec looks like he’s going to be sick and Hollia fixed him up before he throws up at the party. Mara starts bringing up Weslyn with Peri so he summons the cute ferret to distract her. The cute ferret charms Mara into paying for the hotel rooms for everyone. Michael was able to cast nondetection on Donwec before everyone retired to their own rooms to sleep all day. Mara cuddled with Michael piggy, Orevana and Donwec shared a room, and all of the others had their own private room thanks to Mara’s generosity.

Mara/Michael wake up to a map of the 6th District in cold pasta in the middle of the room. The map has a note from Morla and the paper is marked with a pasta stain on one side and a blood stain on the other. In figuring out where to land, Orevana asks that they avoid Rakdos and Simic territory at all costs. The party relocated to an Inn closer to Wayport to eat and rest before traveling the next day.  
  
Pannikin meets them in Wayport early the next day. He explains that we will fly through the 8th District to get fuel, then we’ll go to the 6th District - the trip will take three days total. Pannikin is not staying in town so the team will need to find their own way home. **Note**, Hollia’s word of recall only works on 6 people (we have 7 with Donwec).  
  
Pannikin shares with the party what he knows about the 6th District. He tells the party about various Orzhov families in the area - the Lashka Cartel “owns” much of the district, there is Kartashov presence here (Orevana’s “family”), as well as Tezirek, Pracovisku, and Botasavo. He also explains that Rakdos and Gruul are constantly fighting in the area and that they also run brothels inside Luka (the main town area). Most of the people here are Guildless that are just working for the Guilds. Pannikin also said our map is old - some of the Gruul / Rakdos territory is different now. Izzet, Simic, Orzhov are the ones that run most things. Boros are more mercenaries / guards - no Wojecs, insead there are Hazda (like sheriffs) that have little support from the other Guilds.

When asked about the reclamation zone, Pannakin said there are “Sky swallowers” in the zone. Orevana thinks this could be leviathans that eat flying things - so flying to the area would probably be a bad idea. Orevana goes over the plan with Donwec to make sure he doesn’t blow their cover.  
  
While Pannikin is refueling, the party tries to break into the cargo hold. Kullic breaks the door open, then Mara lockpicks one of the many strongboxes inside and is hit by a glyph on the box. Hollia moves one of the glyphs onto another strongbox. Then, Michael / Kullic see there is still an arcane lock on the strongbox, so Hollia uses dispel magic as well. Inside the box are 3 bottles of high end champagne (Casarta). Under the booze is a hidden panel with jewelry, drugs, and a glass tube filled with a green liquid with a Simic symbol etched on the side of it. Michael casts identify on the tube and discerns it is a magical bodily fluid (genetic material). The team re-locks the box and Hollia put a new glyph on it so the box is fully reset before they leave the cargo hold.  
  
Options that the party considers for getting into the reclamation zone: Orevana uses her guise as a Kartashov to get a carriage to get near reclamation zone; Go to the reclamation zone disguised as workers; Allow Donwic’s nondetection to run-out and see what happens. The party decides to just find a place to stay and gather intel before making a decision.  
  
The plan after landing: Kullic will gather info in general, Hollia will find soldiers to ask, someone else should investigate the brothel(s).
