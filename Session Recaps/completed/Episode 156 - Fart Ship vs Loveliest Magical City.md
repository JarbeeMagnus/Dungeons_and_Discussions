## Summary
**On The Ru:**
In an effort to get clean air, Mara maneuvers the ship near the Neogi caravan so that our air bubble touches theirs. She is ultimately successful, but the Neogi ships notice her and open fire. She barely manages to escape, calling upon the "Lord of Dis" to aid her. Once out of the fray, she realizes the nearby "comets" were actually heads that were looking for her ship. They find The Ru and escort them to M-ZOK, the "the Void Lord". He's some kind of giant robot thing and he demanded a sacrifice. Mara hastily offers Marge, but the rest of the crew immediately beg her not to. Mara summons an imp to offer instead and they quickly leave the area.

**In Silverymoon:**
Orevana, Michael, Peri, and Hollia enjoy a lovely time in Silverymoon. They meet Hollia's parents, request Ealon (Hollia's dad) to create a new staff for Peri, hang out under the stars, and in the morning they have a nice breakfast and go to the hot springs for a bit before Hollia returns us to The Ru.

Mara explains what happened while the rest of the party was gone which results in Hollia and Orevana storming off. We have a few days of easy travel ahead of us - a good opportunity for team-building!
