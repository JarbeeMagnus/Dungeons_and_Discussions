## Summary
Orevana has a nightmare about Rakdos and tells the others about it. Shortly after, we enter an asteroid field and a group of light motes flash at us in the distance. They get closer and fly around the ship before disappearing. Later, we hit a zone of heavy gravity and seem to attract pebbles and clouds. We are then hit by a large jellyfish and a giant asteroid. The "jellyfish" seems to have been a ship - there is bedding, supplies, and a couple of bodies on board. Marge sees movement on the asteroid and we discover it's a group of teenage Goon Balloons. We fight them and kill too many, which fouls our air. Orevana consoles Marge, who seems shaken up by the fight (something about being strangled previously). We leave the gravity well a few hours later, but we still have foul air and no timeline for getting out of the Sargasso.

## Detailed Notes
- Orevana wakes from a nightmare about Rakdos. She tells Mara about it and the two go to tell the others
	- Orevana hurriedly goes into the helm room with Belia while Mara and Hollia chat on deck. Peri shortly joins them
	- Hollia suggests beheading Orevana to “reset” any trauma to her brain. They did not think that was a good course of action
	- Peri suggests giving Orevana mushrooms to induce more visions - he’ll talk to her about it later
- Suddenly, while Belia is manning the helm, the ship slows from maximum speed to tactical speed
	- The characters on-deck see that we’re now in an asteroid field (looks like graphite)
	- Peri tries telling Moufy to stay close to the ship (it doesn’t really work)
	- A group of what look like stars flutter by in the distance. One notices us and approaches. It flashes at us a few times and Hollia responded with using the Light spell to flash back at it in the same patterns
	- After a few moments, the light floats under the ship then re-emerges behind the ship and floats toward the front. Everyone on deck feels a cool breeze as it passes and the scent of flowers. It blinks three times and then shoots off
- During the next shift, we hit a zone of heavy gravity. The ship seems to leave the zone but we still feel heavy. This lasts a few hours. Small rocks and pebbles get pulled onto the ship and the clouds nearby start coming closer to us
	- A giant jellyfish looking creature crashes into the ship followed by a giant asteroid
	- Michael uses Bigby’s hand to slow the asteroid down enough that it doesn’t damage the ship
	- Michael then uses the hand to pull things out of the “jellyfish” - an ogre with 6 eyes and a chest full of bedding, food, other supplies, a small pewter raven statue (figurine of wondrous power), and a dagger shaped like a trident
	- We all suffer a level of exhaustion
	- Orevana slowly makes her way to the deck
	- Another few asteroids collide with us - one of which Marge sees movement on. Orevana immediately eldritch blasts the asteroid. Some muffled voice said something that we couldn’t quite understand
	- Michael, on the bottom of the ship, waves at a couple of sphere dudes. He captures one in a ball. They called each other Lincoln and Ashlyn. Braidyn, Keely, Brynn (Belia notices they look like Goon Balloons)
	- Orevana, seeing movement on the large asteroid, immediately blasts three of them and they all die and release a green puff of smoke
	- We do a fight with them and kill too many - the air becomes fouled
	- We knock out a bunch and manage to get them (and the asteroids) outside the air pocket to kill them all. Hollia also disintegrates the “jellyfish” carcass
	- Marge has a panic attack during the fight and Orevana consoles her
- A few hours later, we feel another lurch and gravity returns to normal

OREVANA'S NIGHTMARE: *You push up past the spires of the Tenth District, rising over the streets and bridges where thousands of people are locked in battle. As the fading daylight washes the city in bloody hues, the gargantuan demon Rakdos looms over the carnage, crushing a Boros angel in his clenched fist. Throwing his four-horned head back in fits of glee, he tosses the mangled corpse and turns to look up at you, his lips curling into a sneer. His searing gaze follows as you soar higher. His face slowly drains of emotion as the long shadows of sunset drape him in dusk. For a moment you feel free. The demon steps out of the gloom, but his visage is now horribly maligned. Two sharpened horns flank an elongated skull emerging from peeled and decaying flesh. His fanning wings are torn and hanging in tatters from the bones. “Return what is mine!” he bellows, raising his rotted claws up at you. As he is about to envelop you in his grasp, the whole world shatters like glass around you. The terror is suffocating and you barely catch the sight of your reflection: you are a jet black raven staring in horror at ten thousand versions of yourself looking back from the shards of the collapsing world. Everything is black for a long, long time. Slowly, the soft sound of rain on stone fills the emptiness. Wait, is that a pen furiously writing on paper? No, a chain being fed down a hole. A wound into the belly of a prison. A blade reflecting on itself. A hooded figure standing in the darkness. A brother? A messenger? The sound of ravens croaking. You wake up with a sense of deep loss mixed with the pounding joy of finding your way out of an unending maze.*
