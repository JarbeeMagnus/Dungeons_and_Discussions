## Detailed Notes
- There is a bulletin board that catches our eye with a few small jobs including finding a lost dog, rental carriage driver, potion tester (Quest: Posters in Prosperity)
- We buy some cowboy hats, thunder weapons, magic ink, and Michael buys a Magic Missile scroll
- Peri unveils his new (long) hair & beard 
- We go to The Voyager Grande
	- Hollia gets into trouble when she dips her toes in the outside fountain
	- We get drinks at the bar and Mara rents us 2 rooms for the night
	- Mara gives us all 100 gold to play with (and Michael gets 1000 gold)
	- We play some blackjack (Onyx Eyes) and meet a dwarven medic named Jemzer Balladris - she might join the crew!
	- Michael wins thousands more gold and gets compensated a suite in the hotel for 3 days. He lets Zellhane, Marge, Jilly, and whoever else wants it to stay in it 

TD Date: Highnoon 8, 1069
Faerun Date: Eleasias 6, 1509

- The next day, we have brunch and check on Henry (the missing dog)
	- We get a brush from the dog owners, as well as secondhand information of him, to scry for the dog
	- Michael scries and sees that Henry is in an alley. He gives Hollia the hair and she uses Locate Creature to find the dog
	- We find Henry along with 4 other dogs that the owner says were their last dogs - a happy ending!
- After finding Henry, we go to buy a bathtub - we find a bronze clawfoot tub and pay 600gp for it. We check in with Zellhane et. al and send them to the suite back at the hotel
- We go to check on Sterling Company regarding the hammerhead ship - they tell us to come back tomorrow when the daytime officer is working
	- Mara asks about Davetown and the officer says it’s a fool’s legend that is decades old. People go looking for this town, go missing, and never return
	- Mara also inquires about the metal dogs she's seen and he says they don’t sell them (though a few rich people have them)
- Hollia sets up a tent in the cargo hold and she and Mara sleep in it. Orevana sleeps on the deck. Peri communes with nature to learn about the planet (see article below)

TD Date: Highnoon 9, 1069
Faerun Date: Eleasias 7, 1509

- Hollia uses Legend Lore on Davetown. She learns that it started as a mining town started by a man named Dave. Once the gold vein dried up, everyone but Dave left. Others have gone there and never returned - they either get rich or something terrible happened to them
	- Mara is very interested in investigating Davetown - but we don’t know where it is
	- We check-in at Sterling again about the Hammerhead and vampirates we turned in. We were told there is no reward for the vampirates. However, there was a magistrate hearing on the ship yesterday that they expect to get notes on this afternoon. They’ll make sure there’s a note for us if we check after the sitting officer leaves for the day
	- We mention Davetown to the Stirling officer and he says it’s fake. He does mention that it’s south of the city (maybe a few days?)
	- Funcle Tally pops up behind Peri and says we could use his “flying saucer”. We decide "fuck it" and all climb in and take it South going 4mph. After about 10 minutes, we fly over the other spelljammer port (the main one). It’s crowded and full of ships
	- We go up high to look for hints of where Davetown is but don’t get high enough to be able to see. Peri turns into an eagle and flies 1 mile into the air to find hints. He sees a grain elevator about 40 miles away that may be what we’re looking for. We head in that direction!

Peri's Commune with Nature:

Locations of portals to other planes of existence

There are about 2 dozen small portals Peri can detect that lead to the quasi-elemental plane of steam located under the city, in the cracks between plateaus.

Location of one Challenge Rating 10+ creature (DM’s choice) that is a Celestial, an Elemental, a Fey, a Fiend, or an Undead

There is a very powerful undead creature Peri can detect that is located somewhere in the central area of Prosperity (near the edge of what you can detect from where you are, so this creature is nearly 1.5 miles away from the docks). The tallest limestone skyscrapers are located here, but without direct line of sight, it's hard to say which one it might be in. 

The most prevalent kind of Beast

In general, birds. Specifically, swallows. Peri can detect thousands of nests dug into cracks in the huge limestone pillars and in the crevaces between plateaus.
