## Detailed Notes
- **RETCON:** Rewind time back to when Mara woke Hollia & destroyed Hollia’s old clothes. Also, Mara drew a glyph on Hollia’s thigh with her tongue (which I forgot to mention last time)  
    - Hollia teleports them to Silverymoon and we do a TIMESKIP (eyebrows)  
    - Marlia takes a co-ed bath. They talk about Peri’s comment about Hollia and Orevana being "girlfriends" (and that misunderstanding)  
    - Hollia talks with Mara about “wish day” and “liking great clubs” - she apparently tried to seduce Delandri in the past?  
    - Lots of awkward comments from Hollia during this time  
    - Hollia also mentions how she had NO FEELINGS for women or halflings altogether until one day (overnight) she fell HARD for Mara. Hollia theorizes a God stepped in to change her desires  
    - Hollia promises to keep Mara safe  
    - Before leaving the tub, Hollia's mom comes home
- Peri and  Orevana get high on the ship - and shenanigans ensue
- After 6-7 hours, we pass through the Kinshala Sphere (Peri and Orevana are sober now)  
    - The stars are humanoid warriors holding fire braziers  
    - Going through the sphere was like a teleportation spell where we enter one side and then out the other
- Orevana and Hollia have a quick conversation  
    - Hollia asks if Orevana wanted to come back to life or not because it was difficult to do (it’s usually not) (Orevana died in ep. 162)
    - Hollia admits to having tried to kill Orevana multiple times when they first had met (which Orevana forgave)  
    - Hollia mentions experiencing a 50-year dream of agony with the Raven Queen
- Another day passes  
    - Hollia/Orevana talk about dreams they had that seemed to have sort of shared  
    - Hollia’s was about tending to the dead in various battlefields with one eyed ravens on each body. Orevana’s was about feeling like roiling sand above an empty battlefield until a quill comes down from the sky (Hollia’s quill) into her hand
- We have a team meeting:  
    - Hollia asks the team for help on learning how to do basic tasks/skills. Orevana agrees & in return Hollia will teach her how to read Faerun common
    - Skills include pitching a tent, cooking, doing hair  
    - Group project: NEW NAME!!
- Over the next **12 days of travel** to Thunder Junction
    - Orevana works on training Crabitha & chats with Nadiif &Mr. Brightspark about their endeavors. She also wants to learn local songs from everyone else  
    - Hollia spars with Mara to practice protecting herself & shatters her confidence because Mara couldn't land a single hit. She later practices with Peri and gets slapped  
    - Hollia offers to heal Zellhane of her scars (which involves skinning her alive, one bit at a time). After a few days of deliberation, Zellhane agrees  
    - Hollia tries to get cooking lessons from Marge but is turned away  
    - Tallison shows up for a bit, but he goes back down below the ship after a bit of jabbing from Orevana
    - Peri summons his fey ferret (Filly Lou) to get info from Tally. Tally mentions he doesn’t get along with any of the courts & says he will not hurt Peri or “Peri’s friends”

**Thunder Junction Landing Date:** **Highnoon 7, 1069**
**Same Date in Faerun is: Eleasias 5, 1509**

- FINALLY approaching Thunder Junction  
    - As we approach a free floating planet, we are pelted with what seems like sand - it gets into our lungs  
    - We arrive at an arid desert; there is a line going across the desert with some kind of vehicle going across it (train?)  
    - The train approaches a town that we decide to approach as well and we find a place to land. Mara, Hollia, Michael, and Peri use cool powers to get off the ship and Orevana uses the ladder  
    - We are greeted by a man in a hat named Bill. We landed in his cattle field, just outside of the town (Bitterstone). He says Prosperity is to the West (follow the train tracks)  
    - Michael walked into town and Mara, Hollia, and Peri follow him. They want to take the train but miss it & the Ru has to circle around and pick them up - We follow the train toward Prosperity and see giant white pillars on the horizon  
    - There is a bustling city within and around the pillars  
    - A couple of people riding griffins approach us and ask us to follow them to an overflow zone (they’ve had a lot of ships come into town the past few months)
    - Once we’ve landed, a dock-worker approaches us to get our information & docking fee  
    - We learn the authorities around here is called the Sterling Company. They are dressed in white & have security dogs (Sterling Hounds) they use around town & also human-sized automaton (Sterling Deputy)
    - Bertrum (“Virgin”) Graywater is the leader of the Sterling Company  
    - Mara and Orevana bring a guard over to take custody of Nadiif and Brightspark. They ask that we stay available (in town) and Orevana asks her to get us a discount on the daily parking fee  
    - We ask about the sudden influx of ships and find out they are refugees from the war (see Scro Empire) and that the war has expanded from where we thought  
    - “Star Mariner’s Friend” and “Horizon Atelier” are repair services we could hire. We also ask about places to go in town and are recommended The Voyager Grande which is a Saloon, Hotel, & Casino (& spa) - the biggest on the planet
- We get quotes:  
    - $3,250 and 5-6 days ($720) to fix the Ru from “Star Mariner’s Friend” and NO improvement  
    - $2,200 and 10-14 days to fix from “Horizon Atelier” (owned by Togor) +1day to get the bathtub installed and $900. Armored shutters/steel doors +$1827 for windows +$1604 for doors. 18 days total ($2160) - total price $6500, plus the cost of a bathtub  
    - We’ll go with the Horizon Atelier pricing because we really want the ship improvements
