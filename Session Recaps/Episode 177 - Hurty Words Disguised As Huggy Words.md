## Detailed Notes
- (RETCON) Hollia, noticing how out-of-it Ellegorin is, casts lesser restoration on herself and him. Ellegorin immediately goes to bed
- (another RETCON) Right before (and partially during) the mushroom party, we write a passive-aggressive Sterling-gram to Sabaal
**DATE: Highnoon 16**
- Mara delivers the message to the Sterling office at the dockyard
	- She asks about if there is a flower shop nearby
	- She purchases a bouquet for Hollia and skips back to The Ru in a GREAT mood
- Michael meets Mara on deck and finds out about the message
	- He seems surprised and a bit upset. He checks the gattling guns to make sure they're loaded
	- Jilly pops out of her room and chats with Mara for a bit. Zellhane 
	- Mara checks with Togor on the timeline - he says they're making good progress. He thinks they may even get done early! (*Note: the current estimate is Highnoon 25*)
	- Mara places the bouquet of flowers beside the sleeping Hollia
- Jemzer calls up from below - she brought pastries! She also won big at the casino last night and wants to show us the nice room she won and have dinner all together tonight
- Hollia heads to the kitchen to find a vase for the bouquet. Marge grabs her arm and begrudgingly gets a jar for her for the flowers
- Cut to the Aminatu. After another round, Orevana handcuffs Nadiif to something and heads over to The Ru by herself
	- We discuss what to do about Nadiif
	- We won't be taking Nadiif with us when we leave
	- Ultimately, we decide that it's up to Orevana what we do
- Michael, feeling panicky, sneaks over to the Aminatu to place an arcane eye
	- He hears multiple voices below and uses his sending stone to let Mara know about it
	- Hollia, Mara, and Orevana start heading over to the Aminatu
	- Michael sneaks to the helm to be able to see and hear the conversation. He sees Nadiif handcuffed to a post and two sailors talking to him. Nadiif is begging them to break the handcuffs
	- Michael wiggles the ship and they all panic. Every time they reached for the manacles, Michael would wiggle the ship
	- The girls see the sailors running off the ship and Orevana yells at them to stop. They explain that they heard someone yelling and went on board to help
	- Mara and Hollia stay on deck and chat about cute things while Michael and Orevana interrogate Nadiif
	- Nadiif is not apologetic about the innocents he's killed and generally resists sharing information while in a Zone of Truth
	- Orevana takes the ring she gave Nadiif and leaves him alone with Michael
	- Michael tortures and kills Nadiif with disintegrate
- Orevana announces that she needs a drink. Michael wants to hire a bunch of Giff to guard The Ru. So we go off to where Brightspark disappeared to
	- We decide to hire Togor to strip down the Aminatu for parts (except the anti-magic eye)
	- He lets us know that we'll want to move the ship before ripping the helm out
- Orevana and Michael have a drink (and she gets shit-faced). Shortly after, Mara and Hollia arrive. There is a misunderstanding about Hollia "booping" Orevana
	- We wait for Giff to arrive - they do after a few hours
	- Michael asks about Thicc and the all laugh heartily
	- They are interested in standing around our ship in exchange for some guns. But then Michael gets distracted about getting a bar set-up
	- The Giff agree to be bouncers for us for a few days in exchange for the guns (which Orevana persuades them are really nice)
	- Michael buys two kegs of beer as well and leaves with them
- Drunk Mara, Hollia, and Orevana go in search of "kitchen supplies"
	- We run into a man that Mara accidentally calls "ma'am" and he tells us about purchasing a bucket
	- We keep looking around and run into him again (he runs away)
	- Hollia uses divine intervention to Locate Object to find pans
	- We purchase a full set of copper pans for Marge while Hollia wanders off to purchase paint
- Michael projects himself to purchase a Hold Person scroll and then uses that projection to be busy & have two copies of himself
- Mara and Orevana bring Marge the new pots and pans (which makes Marge emotional, but neither Marge nor Orevana notice)
- Hollia paints her shield to say Gatewatch (in Mara red) over an open book
- Orevana maintains her drunkenness and takes some time to do her hair and get ready for dinner
- Togor stops by and asks about where we want to install the anti-magic eye. Mara decides to replace the ballista with the eyeball
- The Giff arrive and we negotiate what guns we are giving them
	- We give them 2 thunder pistols and rifles each (5 of them), 2 mounted repeaters, and a hundred cartridges (so now we have 20 thunder pistols, 10 thunder rifles, 900 thunder cartridges, 2 mounted repeaters)
	- They get set-up under the ship
- We go to the casino with Jilly, Ellegorin, Kiro, Marge, and the hidozee (Pomo, Munu, and Sogo)
	- We find Jemzer arguing with a tall, thin man. Apparently he joined our crew - it's Ferrun Suul Garsk. He and Jemzer seem to have a history, but they both insist that it won't be an issue
	- Michael sends his copy to eat dinner while he guards the ship
	- Michael and Orevana go to the high-roller table to bet 5k gold using his portent - but it fails so Orevana loses the gold
	- While she wanders the high-roller area, she runs into Trystimar and she goes back to his room with him
	- Everyone but Kiro, Jilly, and Ferrun stay in the suite
	- While chatting, Jemzer lets slip that she won 75,000 gold
	- Michael's projection wanders the casino, trying to figure out why his portent didn't work. He hears Sabaal behind him say "Ah, I see you've come to deliver the key"
