## Summary
The party shares information we separately gathered regarding the "job" that Peri heard about previously. We notice that Hollia wasn't acting like herself and piece together that she takes on aspects of people she resurrects - something to look out for in the future!

We do a seance on Nadiif and discover that, although he was jailed for the murder of the spa attendant, he was quickly released and escorted by Goth Mommy to a special un-scryable area of the casino. We gathered that he is important to the job and that she isn't happy about it.

We go to a nightclub named Sapphire for Orevana to seduce information out of the bartender there (Tulgan). While chatting him up, Michael executes a plan to kidnap Tulgan with Bigby's Hand and for Orevana to save him. The plan worked! However, Tulgan took it poorly and seemed to think Orevana was working with a rival gang called the "Brass Vipers".

The party, wanting more info out of Tulgan, decide to follow him. Mara disguises herself as Bilver (an annoying gnome we encountered inside the club) and Peri disguised himself as Bilver's busty "wife" to complete the charade. Through Mara's suggestion, they find out that the "job" involves the hammerhead leaving the casino and flying out of town before sunrise. Mara convinces Tulgan to go home instead of telling "Sabaal", who is the leader of the gang and owner of Sapphire.

What will the party decide to do next?!
