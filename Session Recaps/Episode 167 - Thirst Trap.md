## Detailed Notes
- We start the session with the party (sans Orevana) appearing on the deck of The Ru
	- It’s night-time (close to midnight)
	- They find Belia on the deck among various papers & scrolls. Mara gives Belia a recap of what she experienced in Davetown but Belia is distracted by the papers - which appear to be a starchart! She had happened upon a familiar face walking by and heard that her mentor (Focaron) might be nearby. She says she wants to resign to find him, but is then distracted by Michael
	- Belia asks if Michael is okay and everyone notices he’s extremely pale. He is gasping for air. Hollia heals him and Peri creates water above Michael’s head. It makes him feel better but Michael still has a sheen of what looks like slime on him. He tries to remove it via prestidigitation and dispel magic but nothing changes
	- Hollia determines it’s not a disease. Mara takes out a Scroll of Remove Curse and hands it to Hollia to cast. It also does not change anything about Michael’s condition. Michael thinks he may need to be in the bathtub and Peri fills it with water. Michael immediately feels better and climbs out of the tub triumphantly
	- Peri mentions Orevana and the party suddenly remembers Orevana is still in Davetown!! Mara runs to the dockmaster and finds out they’ll lose their spot if they fly away (he gets offended by Mara trying to bribe him)
	- Peri says he can turn into an animal and can find & fetch Orevana
	- Michael tries to turn into a werebear and turns into a slimy, hairless, pale werebear (ew)
- Cut to Orevana and Tally
	- Orevana and Tally do a seance to get the Locate Object spell to try to find Michael’s jacket. They get close to town and she does not find it - so she deduces the party must have bamf’d back to The Ru
	- Tally offers to fly Orevana back to town and she uses polymorph to turn into a ferret so she’s not as heavy (and it’s cute)
	- While traveling back, eagle Peri crosses paths with Tally (+ a ferret). He tries to sneak up on them but Tally notices him and thinks some eagle is attacking us so he uses magic on Peri who turns back into a firbolg and falls to the ground and is knocked unconscious. Orevana turns back into herself and casts cure wounds
	- They decide to sleep in Leomund’s Tiny Hut & sent an animal messenger to Mara to let her know their status
- Back on the ship, Hollia and Mara talk about what they’re going to do next - the changes Michael is going through appear to be "natural". Michael asks another plane to:
	- See if his condition can be healed - “why would you change your current condition?”
	- Then Michael accidentally uses his second question to ask if that’s the answer he’s going to give - “yes”
	- Boccob, is that you? - “I’m not Boccob”
	- And then is it possible to stop my condition - “Yes”
	- Will the spell “heal” remove my condition? - “not this time”
- Mara stays up all night to let Hollia and Michael rest 

TD Date: Highnoon 10, 1069
Faerun Date: Eleasias 8, 1509

- In the morning:
	- New contact other plan convo w/Michael
		- “Will cure disease stop my transformation?” - “no”
		- “Will killing Dave stop my transformation” - “interesting possibility”
		- “Would you look good as a potato” - “irrelevant”
		- “Do you know how to stop my transformation” - “not my purview”
		- “Do you prefer diet coke or diet pepsi” - “I prefer the blood of the innocent”
	- Orevana, Peri, and Tally arrive and make coffee while waiting for everyone to wake up naturally
        Hollia uses Breath of Life (Greater Restoration) on Michael - nothing changes about his condition 
- Michael realizes he fell into the water and determines that he was coated in "Dave sperm" ("you’re PREGNANT?!")
	- Mara thinks the mushrooms in the cave may have something to do with it?
	- Orevana apologizes for abandoning the team and it goes really well (although Tally is not forgiven)
	- We realize Belia is missing - Peri finds a note from her saying she’s left to find Focorum
	- We decide to go to the Dock house to make sure we can take the Hammerhead - we find out someone is claiming ownership of it. A 7ft tall cactus (Cactus Cowboy) and a well-dressed (handsome) man (Tristimar Hawkins) take the ship!
	- After Mara gets upset, Orevana gets information about the men and finds out they are high-ups in some kind of ??? Mob
- We go to charter a ship out to Davetown
	- We find a hummingbird ship (The Donglimmer) whose captain is Captain Curl - an eccentric halfling
	- We agree to his 50gp per day rate (+5gp for use of the bathtub)
	- Peri and Orevana hang out in the cockpit with Captain Curl & Peri uses reduce on Orevana and Disguise Self on himself so we’re not squeezed too much
	- Hollia tells Mara they need to have a private conversation sometime later
	- After Mara asks him to speed up, we get near Davetown
	- Orevana leaves one of Hollia’s romance books with Captain Curl so he’s not bored while he waits
- We plan for the fight
	- We will seance to get Orevana Rary’s Telepathic Bond for the group
	- We will also disperse potions of greater healing
	- Suddenly an image of Daaa'v appears above us and he talks to us. He apologizes and says he was hangry and that it was all an accident
	- Orevana casts Rary’s Telepathic Bond as a ritual. There’s a banner in town with “Oops, Our Bad - Sorry!!!!!!!” and the whole town is singing that they were sorry
	- Daaa'v says the mushrooms help chill him out and that he’s “left to the place of only water” a few times and came back. He prefers it here
	- We end the session as we approach the mouth of the cave
