## Detailed Notes
- Hollia and Michael chat on the way back to the ship
	- Michael casts fly on himself and Hollia (she’s still drunk)
	- Peri and Mara are also still drunk. Mara uses her wings to fly and hugs Peri to pretend he has wings. They giggle about it
	- Hollia asks Peri if he’s feeling better. He rambles about inside and outside thoughts, but Hollia isn’t able to follow
	- Peri asks about Orevana and they remember she went home with “that old cowboy”
	- Hollia asks Michael and Peri to watch over the ship (which they misinterpret as literally holding it down) so she can go to Silverymoon with Mara
	- Mara asks about what the group thought of the new guy, Kiro. Mara got “bad vibes”, Hollia got “no vibes”, Michael got “shiny” 
	- They realize they didn’t clean the tub and sing “Tainted Tub”
	- Hollia struggles to get up the rope ladder and she decides to take them to Silverymoon from the ground
	- Michael pees off the ship as Peri flies upside down, pretending that he’s floating away. Peri tells Michael “Nice dick!” 

TD Date: Highnoon 12, 1069
Faerun Date: Eleasias 10, 1509

- The next morning, in Silverymoon
	- Mara wakes first and sneaks downstairs for breakfast. Mara and Hollia's mother talk about how clumsy Hollia is. Mara also talks about how Marge seems to overpepper Mara’s food because she “may have wronged her a little bit” (cut to Episode 156 where Mara negotiated giving Marge to some space robot)
	- Hollia’s mom is planning on making to-go packs for everyone later
	- Mara goes outside with some coffee to enjoy the weather, eating breakfast
	- Shortly after, Hollia wakes, gets dressed, and joins Mara outside. “Mara Underhill. I love you”. Mara responds with “good morning, Hollia’ - the last time Hollia said this was ages ago at The Dancing Goat
	- Hollia says she’s too nervous to eat breakfast. She proceeds to tell Mara she’s ashamed of how she went through a gate before Ravnica and she froze and her party almost got slaughtered because of her. On their return home, the gate took them to the iron city of Dispater (instead of where they thought they were heading). And she’s copied the four realms of damnation. Something about flags hanging on iron buildings. Mara catches on and realizes Hollia recognized the mark on her chest
	- Mara admits she had to make a deal and that the rest of the group doesn’t know. Dispater made her an offer (shortly after her best friend died) to take revenge. It’s how she gets her power, but when she dies her soul belongs to Dispater. At the time, she didn’t care about what happened to her. Things are different now, with the group and with Hollia
	- Mara apologizes and Hollia hugs her. Mara notices Hollia looks shocked and talks about how everyone goes somewhere when they die. Hollia sternly says she’ll need to know the terms of the contract so she can help her. Mara doesn’t remember the details but the contract was etched into the flesh of some creature Dispater summoned (Quest: Get Mara's Soul Back!)
	- Hollia can only resurrect souls that don’t belong to someone else. So she wants to figure this out so Mara can be resurrected if things go wrong. NOTE: Dispater is the ruler of Dis, the second of The Nine Hells. Mara calls him “Lord Dispater”
	- Hollia’s mom suddenly comes home and realizes Hollia was crying. She gives the girls space to keep talking in the backyard
	- Mara is emotionally vulnerable with Hollia about wanting to keep everyone in the group alive as best as she can. She also apologizes about the “l-word” thing and says she needs more time, to which Hollia understands
	- They go back inside and Hollia finally has breakfast. Hollia’s mom make the to-go snacks and they head back to The Ru
- Cut to Orevana, who is served breakfast from her conquest. She heads back to the ship shortly after
- Back on The Ru
	- Togor and crew are working on the ship
	- Michael decides to clean the tub and Peri does some morning stretches (no hangover!). Needing to make room, Peri decides to rehome a few of the bonsai trees. He speaks with plants to tell them what’s going on and they seem pretty chill about it. Peri goes to the market and asks around for people to take the trees. Some creepy man approaches and puts the bonsai trees into some weird under-robe pockets and runs off with them
	- Peri comes back to find Mara and Hollia on the ship with Marge. Marge only greets Hollia and leaves to go back to the kitchen, where Michael blocks her path. Michael doesn’t budge and Marge goes to the bar. She runs into Orevana and is a bit of a jerk. Orevana joins the party and Mara tries to confess ‘something’ to the party when she’s interrupted by a brisk “HELLO” from below
	- Some mechanical gnome approaches the ship and asks to come aboard to do work on the ship at the behest of Togor. His name is Twiki
	- Mara tries again to tell the group about her deal with Dispater. Orevana (who also made a deal, but with the Raven Queen) is confused about why that’s a big deal
	- Mara also admits that she asked for another favor when The Ru was almost destroyed - to which Hollia storms downstairs (see Episode 156
- Orevana suggests we go to a spa
	- Michael (with Mara’s help) disguises himself to be able to gamble (“call me Zorro”). Peri turns into a horse for Michael to make a grand entrance
	- Mara goes below deck to find Hollia and bribe her with a trip to the spa - along with a kiss
	- We go to The Voyager Grande! Horse Peri is valeted to where the horses go
	- Orevana, Mara, and Hollia get directions to the spa. Zorro stays at the concierge and asks about “private games”. The concierge directs him to the public casino area that we've been to before and he goes inside
	- Mara and Hollia have no idea what to get, so Orevana takes the lead. They get what’s available (2 aloe wraps, 1 sand-scrub massage, and 3 mud baths)
	- Zorro goes to gamble and wins 5k gold, then intentionally loses 100gp to try to seem like he's not seeing the future. He tips the dealer and then heads upstairs to the spa. He notices someone following him

Now we go into paragraph mode…

In the stables, Peri is brought to a stall and is served a bucket of oats and chopped carrots. There are quite a few other horses so it’s cramped. As he munches on the food, he hears a woman berating someone nearby.

The woman says, “Why do we even bother, let’s just cut him loose!...This is a waste of time, I need to go across town. Fetch my horse!” Peri sees the Cactus Cowboy we saw previously who is part of the ??? Mob and took our hammerhead ship! She continues, “I don’t want to deal with Sabaal’s bullshit.” The cactus man finally speaks and says, “Sabaal wants him to stay.” She retorts, “we work for Sebal but we’re not his bitches,” and she rides off.

Peri turns into a fly and follows the woman (“goth mommy”). Fly Peri follows her back to the Voyager Inn and enters the main entrance. She goes up another flight of stairs and into a grand hallway. There are glass tubes along the walls and she goes into one (elevators). There are a few Sterling Deputies in the room as well. She takes an elevator up and Peri decides to leave it alone. He finds a privy and transforms back into Peri. He asks “where is everyone” as he leaves and a crazy dwarf walks by and says “EVERYONE’S AT THE CRABS TABLE, COME ALONG!”

Back at the spa, Zorro is escorted to the lobby where he waits for the girls. Hollia slips and falls as she tries to get out of the tub. Mara gets upset and gets a free drink for both of them - it’s all good now. They pass Zorro in the lobby and Zorro/Mara have a conversation via message. Zorro leaves to go to look for Peri in the stables, but when he leaves the spa, he finds the guard that had followed him along with a silver deputy. Zorro goes into a nearby bathroom to hide. Mara and Hollia get their cactus wraps applied while Orevana enjoys the last half of her mud bath.

Cut back to Peri, who is at the crabs table. There is a table with a track and 4 crabs that will race each other. He bets all of his money (250gp) on crab #1 and he loses it all! Peri tries to get a line of credit and his dwarf friend loans him 5gp (which he also loses on #1) - but the dwarf wins from his 100gp bet! So he loans Peri another 20gp on #3. The dwarf wins again on #2. Peri is impressed but decides to parts ways with the gambling dwarf in order to find his friends, but he can’t remember where everyone went.

In the bathroom near the spa, Michael changes his appearance and casts invisibility and non-detection on himself and then walks out. He looks around for a window to try to get out of the building.

In the mud baths, Orevana hears someone get into the mud bath next to her and they chit chat for a bit. She recognizes his voice as Nadiif and pries him for information on who he knows - apparently he’s close to one of the power players in Prosperity (not the head of the Sterling Company). She finds out that he used that power player to get his ship back (the Aminatu)

Michael wanders around inside the spa and finds a way to use Gate to get onto the roof and then immediately casts invisibility again. He sees expensive spell-jamming ships inside and on top of a building behind The Voyager Grande. He sprouts wings and flies back to The Ru invisibly.

Peri hangs out in the lobby of The Voyager Grande and is approached by staff to see if they can get him anything. He asks them to help him "find his friends". He then proceeds to describe the party (badly). Confused, they offer him a soda water which he happily accepts. He gets a little burpy.

Hollia and Mara are in the wrap room. Hollia comes up behind Mara and slowly removes Mara’s strips and give her a massage. The attendant asks Hollia to stop because only staff can massage guests. Hollia’s eyes flash and she slowly puts Mara’s wraps back on. They are both ready to leave so they rinse off and leave to find Michael. Orevana is asked to leave and Nadiif notices it’s her and not some stranger. He yells and runs out of the bath but he slips and falls. Orevana helps him up (and grabs a clump of mud to try to get a hair). He tries to hit her with a spell but it’s deflected to an attendant (who promptly dies). Orevana then casts hold monster and yells for the guards. They come in quickly and arrest Nadiif. He asks Orevana why she’s here (“to relax”) and she winks and waves at him as he was pulled out of the mud bath area. Mara and Hollia see him on the way out and Mara freaks out. Hollia resurrects the attendant who is scared and starts crying. Orevana comforts her and there’s an announcement that the spa would be temporarily closed. Hollia is shaken and Orevana tries to comfort her too, but Hollia runs out soon after (note: the barbarian orcs from her childhood were covered in mud). Hollia and Mara chat for a bit and Orevana joins them (clean and dressed). The three of them leave the spa and go into the lobby, intending to look for Peri in the stables and Michael in the casino. They find Peri in the lobby with 9 empty soda water glasses near him. They catch up with him and we all exchange information.
