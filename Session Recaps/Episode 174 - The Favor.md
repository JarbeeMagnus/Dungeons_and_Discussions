	- He tells us that the shipment the others were supposed to intercept was a shipment of weapons headed for Fort Harmony. Bertrum Sterling (the owner) has a vault there and often ships valuables hidden in sealed caches among other shipments. Sabaal wanted to intercept and steal the valuables within that particular weapons shipment - but it never arrived
	- While he wants to recover the goods, what is a higher priority is to find one of the members of the team that didn't show up for the mission - Felix Fiveboots. He wants us to Scry on Felix so Sabaal can find him and "speak" with him since it's an odd coincidence that Felix didn't appear on this failed mission
	- Orevana conducts a seance with the team + Sabaal (Michael and Orevana hold Sabaal's hands) in order to get the Scry spell from a spirit
	- Orevana notices that Peri has sweaty hands
- The Scry showed Felix (the blob man Orevana did a palm reading for in Ep. 168) hidden in a barrel underground in some kind of abandoned cellar. Felix was talking to himself about feeling like he was being watched and if Martine had seen "the signal". Orevana tells Sabaal about the location, but does not mention Martine in case Felix was referring to our new crewman Martine Dawnstrider
- We go back to the ship and Orevana tells the others that Martine may have more information. Everyone is concerned about Felix, so we decide to try to find Martine to get more information
	- After asking Jilly about her whereabouts, we learn that Martine is currently in her hometown, Panner's Gulch. It's about 15 miles NW of Prosperity
	- Hollia uses divine magic to cast Sending to warn Martine that Felix is in trouble and asks where she is. Surprised, Martin responds and confirms she's in Panner's Gulch
	- Peri communes with nature to help figure out where Felix is since Orevana heard water outside the cellar during the Scrying
	- Orevana grabs a map of Thunder Junction to help us navigate
- We take the Aminatu to Panner's Gulch
	- Nadiif makes the ship invisible while Hollia pilots us
	- We land in Panner's Gulch successfully and Hollia gets directions to Dawnstrider Ranch by lying to a Sterling "post office" about being a courier looking to drop of a private package
	- We get to the ranch and meet Martine's mother, Cathrise, who is not too happy about her daughter going off into space. Orevana convinces we're cool and she tells us Martine just went off into town not too long ago
	- Since we had just walked from town, we knew Martine lied to her mother about where she was going, so Peri turns into an eagle, flies into the sky, and easily spots Martine up toward the river that runs by the ranch
	- We quickly return to the Aminatu and pick up Martine
- We learn that Martine and Felix are long-time friends. We tell her he's in trouble with Sabaal and she is VERY concerned. We assure her that we're here to help
	- When Orevana tells Martine about the location of the scrying, Martine immediately knows where it is - Felix used to hide at that location when he got in trouble when they were children. The "signal" was one of his boots hanging off a post near the abandoned cellar
	- We leave Nadiif in the Aminatu so he can keep the ship invisible while we fetch Felix (Orevana confirms that he isn't able to pilot the ship since he's not a spellcaster)
	- We have to hack through a bunch of underbrush/cactuses to get to the building. Once Felix hears Martine's voice, he immediately comes outside
	- Peri wild shapes to retrieve Felix's boot as we head back to the ship
- We are suddenly ambushed by a BUNCH of cactus spiders
