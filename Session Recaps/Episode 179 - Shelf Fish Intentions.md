## Detailed Notes
- We are all together, heading out from the main office. We decide to make a quick stop to The Voyager Grande on the way to the library
	- Michael is feeling nervous and does not join us at the casino
	- We go to the front desk and Mara pays 600gp for the room
	- Orevana hangs back to leave a note for Tryst - which Peri notices but does not say anything
- We get to the library to find Michael on the roof waiting for us - after convincing him to come down to us, we all go into the library
	- There are various artifacts around the interior of the library, including a bust of Bertrum Graywater
	- We ask about ship records and the receptionist says they don't have anything of the sort
- We suddenly hear crashes and exclamations in the back of the library followed by a hissing/rattling sound
	- While we walk off, Hollia spends her time reading kids books
	- A boney blowfish floats out from in back followed by a snake woman. The bonefish is named Bubbles
	- Skyfish is the name of a "mercane" that comes into town every 3-4 months or so, but we just missed them. They were apparently in town about 3 weeks ago
	- Orevana asks where the Skyfish sets up and she tells us he sets up shop near the North gate. The proprietor is named Nectis
	- We tell her that we'd like to hear about the artifacts
		- Space clown ray
		- Goldvein hydra tooth
		- Artuk bark - bad plant people and if you touch the bark, you get a headache
		- She mentions a war from 300 years ago (the Unhuman Wars) and how the elven navy fought with the Scro, illithids, golblinoids, and beholders. There were experiments on various elves that made them different/better and she has a drawing of one
	- Orevana manages to catch Bubbles and allow him to be dusted by the librarian
	- Mara draws a copy of the weird elf diagram. She has just discovered she can draw
	- There's a feather from the Holy Cow which is purported to be able to help if you're dying in the desert
	- Jellyfish spacejammers, once they die, calcify into rock. They are piloted by Rygar
	- Talrith is a piece of jewelry that the Rygar use that allow them to make copies of themselves - SABAAL is a Rygar
	- Peri asks to see maps of Thunder Junction. He specifically asks about maps of Prosperity. He gets a copy of a regional map, Prosperity, and Ironstone (what Davetown used to be)
	- Mara gives Tula a big donation for the library and asks if she can let Hollia take a book home with her - but pretend it's random and not asked for by Mara
- Mara goes to look for Hollia and finds her crying
	- Hollia used to teach children how to read and seeing these books brought back those memories
	- Mara's height used to remind her of children (she's over it now)
	- They also chat about the Unhuman Wars, but Hollia does not recognize the name. Hollia offers to read through the Elven medical jargon and copy it. It mentions a blade on one of the arms that is retractable as well as various flesh pockets
- Orevana asks about holidays and festivities of Thunder Junction. She also gets more information about Tula and her various adventures. The library used to be run by Caldryt's father until he passed away. Then Cal got into an accident and she stepped up in her role to help out more
- Tula tries to run the bit about Hollia taking a book home (Hollia sees right through it, but doesn't bring it up). She decides to take one of the children's books
- Orevana, Mara, and Michael talk more about Rygar but don't remember Sabaal having a piece of jewelry similar to the Talrith we saw. We also learn that Rygar seem to love the arts - but especially the art of war/violence
- In Episode 154, we ran into a big jellyfish ship that had the body of an ogre with 6 eyes, a dagger shaped like a trident, and Orevana's raven statuette on the ship
- Marcane is a race that are rivals to some bird race called Dohwar (another merchant race)
- After a while, we hear the bell ding and boots on the ground and someone grossly clearing their throat. He was looking for drawings of naked people and, when Cal asks for clarification, he changes his mind and says he'll go to the dance bar down the road to take a look. He accidentally leaves his bag on the ground. Peri goes to look but he feels something scurrying up his leg
	- Orevana's See Invisibility does not catch the cat-sized creatures. When she activates her Ring of Misdirection, the spell is pulled in and eaten by some kind of small mouth
	- Hollia's spirit guardians are also eaten. A lot of our other spells are also eaten somehow
- Hollia cleans up the mayo from the books and bookcase
- Peri follows Orevana and the two of them caught the guy (named "Clyde") and we Zone of Truth him in the middle of the library
	- Michael has a 14 left on his portent
	- Trystimar sent him to release his lizards in the library to "keep us busy"
	- We make him hand over the 20 gold Tryst paid him as a donation to the library. His lizards are known a Mage Banes. He raises them outside of town
	- They apparently only appear to people that they take magic from - and any magic items that have magic this way are only debilitated for 1 minute
- We let Clyde go look at nude men and he says he won't tell anyone he saw us
- We rush back to The Ru - Michael casts fly on himself, Hollia, and Orevana so we can move quickly while Mara rushes on the ground (rogue movement). Peri turns into a speedy eagle and gets there first
	- The Ru is gone and Marge, Jemzer, the hidoze, and Ellegorin are all angrily talking to the guard post
	- The ship has been impounded due to contraband weapons being seen in the vicinity and everyone has been arrested
	- The guard tells us to talk to Sgt Springbeam at the main station
	- Peri sees the Giff at a different ship in the area, Peri points Michael towards them and he has an animated conversation - they were apparently paid off to sell us out
	- Peri knows of other inns in town and Mara pays for them to stay somewhere
- We go to the constabulary to release our people
	- Orevana gets advantage on Charisma checks
	- They only have Zellhane, Goldengleam, Fiveboots, Dawnstrider. Jilly is missing
	- We ask to speak to Sgt Springbeam - he goes to see if she is available. She comes out to us and brings us in back
	- Jilly and Pharen were both waiting in the hallway. We direct them to where the others are staying
	- We find out that Trystimar paid off the Giff
	- Springbeam leaves us in an interrogation room. Orevana pops See Invisibility just in case. After a while, she returns with a man also dressed in Sterling. He introduces himself as Captain Tuldac
	- We're apparently being arrested - Orevana will spin a story (with advantage) +1 from Peri
