## Summary
The group heads over to The Voyager Grande to pay for Mara's room. Orevana leaves a note for Trystimar letting him know she'll be at the library, hopeful for the date he promised. We arrive at the library and learn from a librarian named Tula that Skyfish is the name of a merchant ship captained by a mercane named Nectis. He's apparently in town every 3-4 months and just left about 3 weeks ago. Tula gives us a tour of the various artifacts in the library. A few of note include a medical illustration of an Elven Bionoid, a calcified piece of a jellyfish spelljammer (which we've encountered before), and a Talarith, which is a piece of jewelry used by Reigars that allow them to copy themself (Sabaal is a Reigar!). Mara learns she can draw. Hollia reminisces about reading books to children and is allowed to take a book with her due to Mara's secret large donation to the library. Peri gets a few Thunder Junction maps memorized. Orevana learns about the customs and festivities of Thunder Junction. While we are milling around the library, a gross man comes in looking for "nudies". When pressed, he immediately decides to leave but left the large sack he was carrying on the ground - which was full of cat-sized creatures that we fight. After the battle, we interrogate the man and learn that the lizards are anti-magic lizards and he was sent here by Trystimar to "keep us busy". We rush back to The Ru to find it missing and the crew that stayed the night in Jemzer's room were nearby, arguing with the Sterling guard post. They inform us that the others were arrested so we send them to a nearby inn and go to the constabulary to release our people and get The Ru back. While we are taken to a back room, we find Jilly and Pharen, so we send them to the inn so they are safe while we take care of this. We end the session in an interrogation room with two Sterling officers asking us to hand over our weapons because we are under arrest for stealing Sterling property (the guns).

## Detailed Notes
- We are all together, heading out from the main dock office after not getting any more information about the Aminatu being impounded. We decide to make a quick stop to The Voyager Grande on the way to the library to pay for Mara's room
	- Michael is feeling nervous and does not join us at the casino
	- We go to the front desk and Mara pays 600gp for the room
	- Orevana hangs back to leave a note for Tryst saying she'll be at the library - which Peri notices but does not say anything
- We get to the library to find Michael on the roof waiting for us - after convincing him to come down, we all enter the library
	- There are various artifacts around the interior of the library, including a bust of Bertram Graywater
	- We ask about ship records and the receptionist (a dragonborn named Caldryt) says they don't have anything of the sort
- We suddenly hear crashes and exclamations in the back of the library followed by a hissing/rattling sound. We move around the front desk and see a boney blowfish floats out from in back followed by a snake woman
	- The bonefish is an enchanted bone sculpture named Bubbles and the woman is named Tula. She recognized the name "Skyfish" when we were asking Caldryt for information
	- Skyfish is the colloquial name of a merchant ship that comes into town every 3-4 months or so, but we just missed them. They were apparently in town about 3 weeks ago
	- Orevana asks where the Skyfish sets up and she tells us he sets up shop near the North gate. The proprietor is a Mercane named Nectis
	- Mercane is a merchant race that are 12' tall and are rivals to another merchant race of bird-like humanoids called Dohwar
- Tula offers to show us the artifacts around the library and we readily agree. They include:
	- A space clown ray gun
	- A Goldvein Hydra tooth (fauna on Thunder Junction)
	- Aartuk weedling bark - Aartuk are plant people. Tula says that people that touch the bark get a mild headache
	- An illustration of an Elven Bionoid. She mentions this is from a war that occurred 300 years ago called the Unhuman Wars. At the time, the elven navy fought with the Scro, illithids, golblinoids, and beholders. To give them an upper hand, the Elven Navy experimented on their own to make them different/better. This practice is no longer allowed
	- There's a feather from the Holy Cow which is purported to appear to help if you're dying in the desert. It has wings and is a legend among Thunder Junctionites
	- There is a calcified piece of an Esthetic (jellyfish spellhammer). We learn they are typically piloted by Reigar
		- When Esthetics die, their husks slowly calcify over time
		- **NOTE**: In Episode 154, we ran into a big jellyfish ship that had the body of an ogre with 6 eyes, a dagger shaped like a trident, and Orevana's raven statuette on the ship. Tula doesn't know of any races that look like 6-eyed ogres
	- We see a gold bangle that is called a "Talarith". Tula explains that it's a piece of jewelry used by the Reigar use that allow them to make copies of themselves. They can be bangles, necklaces, anything really - **NOTE: SABAAL is a Reigar**
- During & shortly after the tour:
	- Hollia quickly separates from the group and spends her time reading kids books instead of the tour
	- Orevana manages to catch Bubbles using spirits and mage hand. She brings Bubbles to Tula to allow him to be dusted by her before she releases him back into the air
	- Mara asks Orevana to make a copy of the Elven Bionoid illustration, but Orevana is a mediocre artist. Mara gives it a try and discovers she is actually a great drawer! She makes a copy of the diagram herself
	- Peri asks to see maps of Thunder Junction. He specifically asks about maps of Prosperity. He gets a copy of a regional map, Prosperity, and Ironstone (this is the mining town that Davetown used to be)
	- Mara gives Tula a big donation for the library and asks if she can let Hollia take a book home with her - but she asks her to pretend it's random and not asked for by Mara
	- Orevana asks about holidays and festivities of Thunder Junction, to which Tula gives her a book to read through. She also gets more information about Tula and her various adventures. Apparently, the library used to be run by Caldryt's father until he passed away. Then Cal got into an accident (which is why he's in a wheelchair) and she stepped up in her role to help out more
- Mara goes to look for Hollia and finds her crying in the stacks amongst the children's books
	- Mara learns that Hollia used to teach children how to read back in Silverymoon and seeing these books brought back those memories
	- Mara's height used to remind her of children (she's over it now)
	- They also chat about the Unhuman Wars, but Hollia does not recognize the name. Hollia offers to read through the Elven medical jargon and copy it. It mentions a blade on one of the arms that is retractable as well as various "flesh pockets"
- Tula tries to run the bit about Hollia taking a book home (Hollia sees right through it, but doesn't bring it up). Hollia decides to take one of the children's books
- Orevana, Mara, and Michael talk more about Reigar but don't remember Sabaal having a piece of jewelry similar to the Talarith we saw. We also learn that Reigar seem to love the arts - but especially the art of war/violence
- After a while, we hear the bell ding and boots on the ground and someone grossly clearing their throat. He was looking for drawings of naked people and, when Cal asks for clarification, he changes his mind and says he'll go to the dance bar down the road to take a look. He leaves and "accidentally" leaves his bag on the ground. Peri goes over to look, but the only thing he sees is the lip of the bag move up momentarilty. Shortly after, he feels something scurrying up his leg - INITIATIVE
	- Orevana's See Invisibility does not catch the cat-sized creatures. When she activates her Ring of Misdirection, the spell is pulled in and eaten by some kind of small mouth
	- Hollia's spirit guardians are also eaten. A lot of our other spells are also "eaten" somehow
	- Mara uses a jar of old mayo to coat one of the creatures to be able to hit them
	- Orevana follows the man out the door and chases him down the street to get more information. Peri follows her soon after
- After the fight, Hollia cleans up the mayo from the books and bookcase. Michael uses prestidigitation to clean up the garments she used to clean with
- Peri and Orevana return shortly with the man (named "Clyde") and we Zone of Truth him in the middle of the library to get more information
	- *Michael has a 14 left on his portent*
	- We learn that "some Hawkins guy" sent him to release his anti-magic lizards in the library to "keep us busy" - that's Trystimar
	- We make him hand over the 20 gold Tryst paid him as a donation to the library. His lizards are known a Mage Banes. He raises them outside of town
	- They apparently only appear to people that they take magic from - and any magic items that have magic this way are only debilitated for 1 minute
	- We let Clyde go look at nude men and he says he won't tell anyone he saw us
- We rush back to The Ru - Michael casts fly on himself, Hollia, and Orevana so we can move quickly while Mara rushes on the ground (rogue movement). Peri turns into a speedy eagle and gets there first
	- The Ru is gone and Marge, Jemzer, the hidoze, and Ellegorin are all angrily talking to the guard post
	- We learn from them that the ship has been impounded due to contraband weapons being seen in the vicinity and everyone on the ship has been arrested
	- The guard tells us to talk to Sgt Springbeam at the main station
	- Peri sees the Giff at a different ship in the area, Peri points Michael towards them and he has an animated conversation - they were apparently paid off to sell us out (also by Trystimar)
	- We want the crew to stay somewhere safe while we deal with this. After memorizing the map of Prosperity, Peri knows of other inns in town, so he tells them and Mara gives them gold to pay for it
- We go to the constabulary to release our people. The receptionist tells us that they were taken into custody for assault when The Ru was impounded
	- *Hollia works her magic so Orevana gets advantage on Charisma checks for the next hour*
	- We learn they only have Zellhane, Goldengleam, Fiveboots, and Dawnstrider. Jilly is missing from the list
	- We ask to speak to Sgt Springbeam - he goes to see if she is available. She comes out to us and brings us in back to one of the rooms
	- We see Jilly and Pharen both waiting in the hallway on the way to the back. Jilly and Peri share a moment where they both admit to being worried about the other. We direct them to where the others are staying so they stay out of trouble
	- Springbeam leaves us in an interrogation room. Orevana pops See Invisibility just in case. After a while (10-15 minutes), she returns with a man also dressed in Sterling garb. He introduces himself as Captain Tuldac
	- We're apparently being arrested for participating in the theft of Sterling Company property
	- They ask us to hand in our weapons and we CUT TO BLACK
