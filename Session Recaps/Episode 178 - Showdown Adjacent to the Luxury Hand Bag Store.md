## Summary
The entire party is separated, so we follow each of them separately until they are re-united the next day.
- Michael's projection is wandering around the Voyager Grande, speculating on why is portent didn't work at the high-roller table when it is approached by Sabaal. After briefly discussing who owns the Voyager Grande, Sabaal asks what we want in return for the key. Michael tells him we were thinking 50k gold and Sabaal immediately accepts. Suspicious, Michael asks what a "Nectis" is and Sabaal immediately attacks him (but misses) and then says he'll give us 100k gold. Michael tells him he would need to discuss with the team and Sabaal hits a magic attack, which dispels the projection
- Mara and Hollia are in Jemzer's suite, trying to have some private time with an actual BED when they are interrupted with terse knocking at the door. To their surprise, it's Sabaal standing before them. He asks what she wants in exchange for the key. She says 50k and he (again), quickly accepts. She toys with him a bit before slamming the door in his face. When the knocking starts back up, Hollia silences the door and he seems to leave shortly thereafter
- In the meantime, Michael and Peri are at The Ru and they are both acting strange - Michael is jumpy and distracted while Peri is more father-like and "responsible". They are approached by a skeletal bird (they thinks it's from Adizar) that breaks down into two bone messages - "Highnoon 17-19    Topaz Pillar Mist" and "Aminatu + Key -> Skyfish + Nectis". We later learn from Felix that Nectis is the name of the person that Sabaal was going to sell the key to and from Kiro that the "Topaz pillar" is a specific pillar and the "mist" part is probably referring to the steam that is always hanging over the city from the steam vents. Michael goes to nap on The Ru while Peri goes to the Voyager Grande to discuss next steps with the rest of the party. He has trouble getting up to Jemzer's suite, so he purchases a room in Mara's name in the middle floors
- Orevana is having a great time with Trystimar, complete with room service, sex, and the promise of a picnic the next day. They are interrupted when Tryst gets summoned to complete a quick task and he leaves to complete it. To his surprise, he runs into Peri in the hallway and the two of them get into a tussle because of Trystimar's lying before a couple of Sterling Deputies show up to break it up. Tryst gets away before Orevana opens the door to the suite to calm down the commotion in the hallway. She doesn't seem to notice Trystimar down the hallway. After Orevana convinces the Deputies to leave them alone, Peri asks her to come into his room to talk. She exasperatedly leaves Tryst a note explaining what happened before gathering her things and going into Peri's room for the night. The two talk for a bit (Orevana is quite snippy) before they decide they can talk to the others in the morning
- After Michael's nap, he decides to send another Sterling-gram to Sabaal to say that the key is hidden inside of Nadiif and that he is currently missing
The next morning, both Orevana and Michael use their sending stones to Hollia and Mara, respectively. Peri gets some payback on Orevana for being a brat the night before. Hollia also cures Orevana's hangover after she begs for help. We all decide to meet in the lobby of The Voyager Grande before Hollia teleports us to The Ru - but she does it before Mara could pay for Peri's room! Once we are all together at The Ru, we decide to check on the Aminatu, since it's a component of the note from the skeletal bird. When we go to check it's GONE and we find out it was taken into custody around 2am the night before. The party decides to go to the library to find out what "Skyfish" means and Orevana will make a quick pit-stop to pay for Mara's room.

This means that the "Topaz pillar" is a specific pillar and the "mist" part is referring to the steam that is always hanging over the city from the steam vents
	- Michael confides in Peri that he had been using his projection to go to the dinner & party and what happened with Sabaal just now
	- They agree that they need to talk to the rest of the party, but Michael needs to sleep so he goes below decks to take a nap
	- Jilly asks Peri about how he seems different today (more responsible) and he responds that he's a man now. He heads to the hotel and leaves her dumbfounded

	- He will need someone with a room to invite him up for them to allow him to pass
	- Peri returns to the receptionist to ring the bell to Jemzer's suite (Jilly told him the rom number) but Mara and Hollia don't notice/respond
	- He decides to purchase a normal room under Mara's name to get past the Deputies - **CUT**
- A bell dings and Tryst gets a note from a slot
	- He asks Orevana if he can leave for a bit (20 minutes or so) and she lets him leave on the condition that he makes it up to her (he will)
	- He goes to the hallway and asks Peri what he's doing here. P: "I'm looking for my friends", T: "I don't know where they are" - but Peri knows he's lying with his anti-lie ring
	- When confronted, Tryst misty steps to the elevator. Peri responds by casting grasping vine to stop him from getting away. Tryst gets caught in the vines and exasperatedly sighs
	- Peri plays good cop and heals Tryst a bit. He then notices Tryst looking past him and smiling slightly. Peri summons Filly Lou and turns to see a couple of Sterling Deputies coming down the hallway and a fight ensues

	- Orevana gets dressed and leaves Trystimar a note using Illusory Script before joining Peri in his room across the hall. The note said a friend came to ask her help with something urgent and she had to help them. She also writes that she asked the Deputies about allowing her back into his room but they won't allow her to return and she wasn't sure how long helping her friend will take. She ends the note with "hope to see you tomorrow <3 Orevana"
	- Peri explains what happened with the bone note that he believes Adizar sent. Orevana (who is still very drunk) is upset about having her night interrupted for this since they can't do anything about it right now with the others in the suites above and likely asleep.
