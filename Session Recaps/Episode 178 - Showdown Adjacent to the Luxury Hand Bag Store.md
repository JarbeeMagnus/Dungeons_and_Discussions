## Detailed Notes
**Night of Highnoon 16**
- We check-in with everyone
	- In Jemzer's suite, everyone except Hollia and Mara are asleep. They take a bed (with a high thread count) and do some off-screen stuff. In the middle, they hear some knocking at the suite door. They yell "we're busy" but the knocking does not relent. They throw on robes and Mara approaches the door
	- Orevana and Tryst had a good time. He asks if she wants anything to eat and writes down an order for her (with booze). They have another round while they wait
	- Peri, having felt rough most of the day, perks up a bit and looks around the ship for who's here. Felix is sleeping in a barrel, Martine set-up a bed on top of some cargo boxes. He casts Pass Without Trace and heads toward some voices he hears below the ship. He passes Zellhane and Jilly on his way out. He chats with Zellhane and hears about what we were up to today
	- Michael is hanging out on a rocking chair at the bottom of the lift, keeping watch while the Giff are playing cards nearby. Peri approaches him and checks in. Suddenly, a skeletal bird appears. Upon seeing Michael and Peri, the bird breaks down and the bones form words. Peri tries changing what the words say, but they immediately go back to their original words after he tries
	- Bird bone words:
		- Highnoon 17-19    Topaz Pillar Mist
		- Aminatu + Key -> Skyfish + Nectis
- Michael's projection spins around to see Sabaal
	- He asks Sabaal if he knows who runs the casino (he does). Michael wants to know how the high-roller table was able to get around his portant
	- Sabaal confirms that Bertrun Graywater (the head of The Sterling Company) owns the hotel/casino
	- Michael mentions that we would want 50,000 gold and Sabaal immediately accepts the offer. Michael asks about "Nectis" and Sabaal freezes before he swings his trident at Michael. He then says he would give us 100,000 gold for the key. Michael says he needs to check with the team
	- Sabaal responds by casting Chromatic Ray on the projection, which dispels it
- Mara answers the door and Sabaal is standing in front of her
	- He says he wants to discuss the key fragment
	- Mara asks for 50,000 and he immediately accepts and asks if she wants it in platinum
	- She toys with him a bit and slams the door in his face after saying he shouldn't have sent her girlfriend a head
	- The knocking starts again and Hollia casts silence on the door before proceeding with sexy-time (once the spell wears off, the knocking does not continue)
- Orevana and Tryst enjoy some food and he invites her to a picnic tomorrow (which she accepts). She also confirms that she'll be staying the night
- Michael goes back onto the ship as Jilly and Kiro come to The Ru. Jilly and Peri chat for a bit. Peri shows her the note and Kiro confirms that various pillars have names (like neighborhoods) - this confirms that the "Topaz pillar" is a specific pillar and the mist is up high from the steam that is constantly above the city. Michael confides in Peri that he had been using his projection with the party and what happened with Sabaal. They agree that they need to talk to the rest of the party, but Michael needs to sleep. Jilly asks Peri about how he seems different today (more responsible) and he responds that he's a man now. He heads to the hotel
- Hollia moves Mara's tattoo to be a full chest piece instead of the small neck tattoo it was previously - it also now says "Gatewatch" along the blade
- Peri arrives at the casino and gets stopped by a Sterling Deputy when he tries to go upstairs. He will need someone with a room to invite him up. The receptionist rings the bell but Mara and Hollia don't notice/respond. Peri purchases a room under Mara's name
- A bell dings and Tryst gets a note from a slot. He asks Orevana if he can leave for a bit (20 minutes or so) and she lets him leave on the condition that he makes it up to her (he will). He goes to the hallway and asks Peri what he's doing here. "I'm looking for my friends", "I don't know where they are" - but Peri knows he's lying. When confronted, Tryst misty steps to the elevator. Peri responds by casting grasping vine to stop him from getting away. Tryst gets caught in the vines and exasperatedly sighs. Peri plays good cop and heals Tryst a bit. He then notices Tryst looking past him and smiling slightly. Peri summons Filly Lou and turns to see a couple of Sterling Deputies coming down the hallway - Orevana hears them and a fight starts in the hallway
- After a short rest, Michael sends another Sterling-gram to Sabaal saying that they key is inside of Nadiif
- Orevana and Peri talk about what happened - Orevana is upset about having her night interrupted. They decide to meet up with the others in the morning and they plutonically spoon through the night in Peri's hotel room
