## Detailed Notes
- During the 9hr journey to Davetown:
	- Hollia, afraid of the height, puts temporary HP onto Mara
	- Hollia asks Mara to check beneath the saucer so she can accurately depict what’s going on with the saucer - but it was just a ruse to check out Mara’s butt
	- At some point, Orevana, Michael, and Peri notice Hollia crying. Peri (as an eagle) lets her pet him for comfort. Orevana asks what’s going on in Orcish and the two have a heart-to-heart about how Hollia is feeling replaced by Jemzer and Orevana assures her that she is a valued member of the party and nobody could replace her. And that she has LOTS of time to learn to do more things - she’s not limited to what she knows today
- We happen upon a broken down sign that says “Ironstone” and a broken mining cart with some kind of mass nearby (perhaps a body?)
	- We land and approach the body of a cow/oxen. It’s rotten and appears to have been partially consumed
	- Suddenly we are ambushed by sand lizards and a fight ensues
	- The building we were next to starts to collapse and someone in the rafters shouts - it’s (a) Dave!
	- Peri turns into a dragon and rescues Dave. He tells us he’s from Davetown and we offer him a ride. Dave is reeeeaaaally annoying
	- As he talks to us, he mentions his cart of Dave posters and Orevana blasts it so they don’t have to bother taking it with them (which he is upset by, but we move on)
	- Hollia and Orevana giggle in Orcish and mention if we meet anyone else this grating, we will mutiny
	- We travel for a few hours towards the mountain, taking ravines and crevice paths along the way
- We enter Davetown
	- About 15 people live here and they’re all happy to meet us. They appear to be growing crops on the mountaintops (this is the Cult of Daaa'v)
	- Hollia visually checks out the residents of Davetown for illness or injury. They all appear healthy
	- Orevana does not partake in the food
	- Some people walk into the mining cave and our Dave mentions that "original Dave" likes to have a snack of raw mushrooms before bed
	- Tally puts cotton in his ears and Orevana and Peri join him
	- Mara and Hollia follow Dave into the mining cave to meet the "original Dave"
	- The Dave’s start acting weird and demand the party bring mushrooms to Dave. They are led through the cavern toward an underwater lake. Michael uses mage hand to deliver baskets of mushrooms to the designated drop-off. Two pale aquatic creatures emerge to take the mushrooms and go back into the water
	- After a few heartbeats, they hear a voice in their heads thanking them for joining “new Daves”
		- He says a series of unrelated words, including Nuulvir and “sacrifice everything” after looking into Mara’s head (she feels water drip down her brain) - see Article below for details
		- He then says a series of different words and phrases after looking into Peri’s head - see Article below for details
		- Dave said he was eternal but could not give an additional information to us
	- At the surface, the town starts to swarm Orevana and try to pull her into the cave. She barely manages to escape using misty step and polymorph. As she flies up out of the gorge as a raven, she hears Tally say “I’m so glad you got away, that was WEIRD” after he fucking abandoned her
- IT’S T-REX TIME
- We have a long and difficult fight while Orevana and Tally both run away. Hollia is able to BAMF the rest of the team to The Ru
