The Izzet League is one of the 8 ruling Guilds of Ravnica. They focus on innovation and are constantly trying to make scientific breakthroughs.

Their leader is Niv-Mizzet, a 15,000+ year old red dragon, often referred to as the Fire Mind.

The party received a set of bracelets from Izzet for us to be able to telepathically communicate within 120ft of each other (like message).
