The Scro Empire is a group of Orcs in a war against the Elven Imperial Navy. Current commander is a sorcerer-king named Andropinis (AKA "The White Dragon").

#### From Rick RE: Andropinis: (This is from a 3-session arc around episodes 20-22)
- The White Dragon was the nickname the PCs gave to **Andropinis**, the sorcerer-king of a city-state named **Balic** on the planet **Athas** (the world in the D&D setting Dark Sun)
- The party got there when a planeswalker named **Tukesius** first ignited his spark and teleported them all right out of **Sigil** and onto the planet **Athas**
- When the PCs met Andropinis, he captured them and read all their minds, discovered that Tukesius was able to planeswalk, and performed a ritual where he swapped bodies with Tukesius to gain that ability
- As part of the ritual, he also transformed from a human into a white dragon (none of the players or their characters have the details of why)
