### Fun Facts
- Met as a group of lights, he directed us through various aspects of the Sargasso while we were lost
- He was called "a starlight apparition" and that "they are used by powerful fey" by Gomura, the space turtle
- Once we were out of the Sargasso and into normal space, we discover these lights were actually Algus Westerton! (see ep. 159)
- He was an original member of Gatewatch. While in the party, he was a warlock whose patron was killed which lead the group to [[Dis]] to renegotiate his contract.
- He died, but we now learn his new patron is "Morwel" (Queen of the Stars). She commanded him not to speak until he's completed his task (we assume the task was getting us out of the Sargasso). We also learn he was friends with a goblin named Psy (who Peri also knows). He also mentions that if we end up in **Arboria**, we should find him (and his patron?) and have tea
