### Fun Facts
- Is obsessed with crossbows & likes bubble baths and small pigs
- ~~2nd~~ 1st heir to the Pracovisku lineage *(updated after [[Ljudo Pracovisku Jr. ☠︎︎|Ljudo Jr.]] died)*
- Rubs the scar on his head when in distress - the scar was from a crossbow accident with Ljudo Jr. It was after this incident that Donwec became mentally stunted & Ljudo Jr. gave him the nickname "Dimwec"
- During Operation Heal Donwec, we learned that only Greater Restoration could help him
- Became distant after being healed; he only wanted to kill his brother (and bang Orevana)
- Has been inside Peri's mouth (as a slug)
- Was kidnapped by some eyeball parasite on Zuun 13 and taken to "the Monolith". He had an [[Intermediary_Device.jpg|intermediary device]] implanted into his spine and skull, just like what we found on Ljudo Jr
